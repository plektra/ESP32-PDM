## Notes for pdm-4ch U30 (Q30 source on VBAT_FL, drain on VS)

- VS, SRC and EN/UVLO all on VBAT_FL: SRC = VS, inside "SRC ≤ V(VS)" (forward) and "≤ V(VS) + 0.3 V" (reversed).
- OV on GND: reversed, OV − GND = 0 V lies between V(VS) and 65 V + V(VS) for V(VS) ≥ −65 V, the same limit as EN.
- Q30 AGM601LL VGS ±20 V ≥ 15 V: no zener. C32 1 µF ≥ 10 × Ciss (5.47 nF typ) and ≥ 0.1 µF.
- VS capacitor: C30 2.2 µF/100 V sits on the same node through the JP30 copper bridge (≥ 22 nF).
