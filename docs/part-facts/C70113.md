Notes:
- The datasheet has no "absolute maximum ratings" table: the closest are V_max and I_max (p. 1, defined as the maximum voltage at rated fault current and the maximum fault current at rated voltage) plus the warning on p. 4 that large inductance in the circuit gives L·di/dt voltage above the rating.
- Hold/trip currents are 25 °C values; I_hold is a maximum no-trip current, I_trip a minimum always-trip current, so the band 0.50–1.00 A is the undefined region.
- JLCPCB listing matches the datasheet: Ri min 150 mΩ, R1 max 1 Ω, I_max 100 A, V_max 60 V, I_hold 500 mA, I_trip 1 A, 150 ms, 800 mW.
- Series dimension row for the -60V part is thicker (up to 1.8 mm) than plain mSMD050 (0.9 mm); check the height clearance.
- EasyEDA footprint available (jlc_get_part). Not compared with the datasheet land pattern; do so before ordering.
