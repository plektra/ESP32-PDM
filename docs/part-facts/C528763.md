Notes:
- Only part CA-IS3050G is relevant here; the datasheet covers the whole family and package variants (W, U, WG), so check the column or label when reading a table.
- Bus-side VCC2 must be 4.5 to 5.5 V, so a 5 V isolated supply is needed on the bus side (a 3.3 V bus supply is out of range and below the 4.2 V UVLO start-up typ). UNVERIFIED: the recommended isolated-supply or transformer circuit; the datasheet gives none in the pages read.
- Land-pattern dimensions were read from the rendered drawing; verify them against the JLCPCB/EasyEDA footprint before ordering.
- The "Isolation voltage 5000 Vrms" and "extended" class in the JLCPCB listing agree with the datasheet. Library class "extended" means a loading fee per order.
