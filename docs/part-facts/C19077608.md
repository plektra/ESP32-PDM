## Fig. 1 readings (peak pulse power vs pulse duration, DS p. 4)

Axes log-log: tp 0.1 µs to 1000 µs, PPPM 0.1 kW to 100 kW. The curve is a straight line, so the graph covers **0.1 µs to 1000 µs** only (nothing beyond 1 ms). Reading from the rendered plot (±10 %):

| tp | PPPM (kW) |
|---|---|
| 0.1 µs | ~50 |
| 1 µs | ~18 |
| 10 µs | ~6.5 |
| 100 µs | ~2.3 |
| 1000 µs | ~0.85 |

**Inconsistency:** the curve reads about 0.85 kW at 1000 µs, but the ratings table gives 1500 W at 10/1000 µs. The 10/1000 µs waveform has a 1000 µs half-value time, and the curve does not pass through 1.5 kW there. Use the table figures (1500 W; 28.2 A) for design and treat the Fig. 1 curve as a shape only, or the curve as the more conservative value. The ratio tp 10 µs to 1000 µs is ~7.6×.

## Notes

- Dimension letters A (terminal width), B (body length), C (body width) are read from the p. 2 drawing.
- IR limit is doubled only for bidirectional devices with VR ≤ 10 V (p. 3 note 3), so it does not apply at 33 V.
- Typical IR "less than 5 µA above 12 V" is a feature line (p. 1); the table max for 33 V is 1 µA. The two differ; the table is the guaranteed figure.
- Test conditions for VBR/IR/VC: Ta = 25 °C (p. 2/3 header). No data at higher temperature other than the tempco line and Fig. 2.
- Use in a MOSFET clamp: VBR min 36.7 V, VC max 53.3 V at 28.2 A. Whether those fit the MOSFET's Vds rating is the caller's check. Not evaluated here.
