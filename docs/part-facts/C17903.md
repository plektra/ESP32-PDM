Notes:
- The datasheet is a series document; the 1206 rows apply. Rated power 0.25 W at 70 °C; at 10 Ω the 0.25 W gives only ≈1.58 V (0.158 A) continuous, so power, not the 200 V cap, limits the voltage.
- The overload voltage for 10 Ω (≈3.95 V) is a test condition, not a stated pulse-energy rating. Surge/pulse capability of this part is UNVERIFIED (no pulse-load curve in the datasheet).
- Short-time-overload limit placement: on p. 6 the limit text "±0.5 %,1 %: ±(1.0 %+0.05 Ω)" sits directly above the 4.13 / short-time overload entry; read as its limit from the table layout.
