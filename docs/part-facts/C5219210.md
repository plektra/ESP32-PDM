## Behaviour (key answers)

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Function | Controller for an external N-channel MOSFET, low-loss reverse-polarity protection. **No reverse-current blocking**: "does not have reverse current blocking"; suited to loads that can feed energy back to the input (motors, inductive loads) | p. 1, p. 13 |
| Current back from load to supply | When enabled, GATE is tied to VCAP, so the FET is fully enhanced (GATE–SOURCE ≈ VCAP–SOURCE) and conducts in both directions; no reverse-current comparator in the block diagram (only "reverse protection logic" for negative input). When disabled (EN low), forward current still flows through the FET body diode | p. 11 §8.4.1–8.4.2, p. 9 §8.2, p. 13 |
| Pins: no separate OUT/VS/IN pin | The device has no OUT or drain-sense pin. SOURCE is the only power input: connect to the FET source (the supply/battery side, see Fig. 9-4: VBAT → FET → load; Q1 source toward VBAT). It also powers the chip | p. 3 Table 5-1, p. 16 Fig. 9-4 (see Notes) |
| Reversed input | SOURCE rated down to −65 V; EN may go to −65 V too, so tie EN to SOURCE. Gate driver is off unless SOURCE > POR rising threshold, EN high and VCAP UVLO met; otherwise "GATE pin is internally connected to the SOURCE pin", so the FET stays off. Datasheet does not detail the pull-down circuit beyond that | p. 1, p. 9 §8.3.1, p. 10–11 §8.3.3 |
| Gate drive | Charge pump gives max gate drive ≈ 15 V ("maximum gate drive voltage of approximately 15 V"); §9.3.1.2.2 says "maximum VGS LM74500-Q1 can drive is 13 V", so pick a FET with VGS rating ≥ 15 V, otherwise add a zener to clamp VGS. There is no internal gate clamp spec: GATE-to-SOURCE is limited by VCAP charge-pump regulation (turn-off 12.4 V typ, 13.9 V max) | p. 9 §8.1, p. 16 |
| Gate drive voltage VGATE−VSOURCE | Table gives VCAP−VSOURCE: turn-on 10.3 / 11.6 / 13 V (min/typ/max), turn-off 11 / 12.4 / 13.9 V; 8 V (typ, no min/max) at VSOURCE = 3.2 V and I(VCAP) ≤ 30 µA. GATE = VCAP in conduction mode | p. 5 §6.5, p. 11 |
| Gate source current | Peak source current at VGATE−VSOURCE = 5 V: 3 mA min, 11 mA typ (no max) | p. 5 §6.5 (rendered table: min 3, typ 11) |
| Gate sink current | Peak sink current, EN high→low, VGATE−VSOURCE = 5 V: 2370 mA typ. Discharge switch RDSON 0.4 Ω min / 2 Ω max at VGS = 100 mV | p. 5 §6.5 |
| Charge-pump current | Source (pump on) at VCAP−VSOURCE = 7 V: 162 / 300 / 600 µA (min/typ/max). Sink (pump off) at 14 V: 5 typ / 10 max µA. Pump on below 11.6 V, off at 12.4 V (hysteresis 0.4 / 0.8 / 1.2 V) | p. 5, p. 10 |
| VCAP UVLO (gate-driver enable) | Release rising 5.7 / 6.5 / 7.5 V; falling 5.05 / 5.4 / 6.2 V (VCAP−VSOURCE) | p. 5 |
| Turn-on time | Enable (low→high) to gate turn-on delay ENTDLY: 75 µs typ, 110 µs max, condition V(VCAP) > V(VCAP UVLOR), CVCAP = 0.1 µF. Eq. 1 adds the VCAP charge time to the 6.5 V UVLO, see Notes | p. 6 §6.6, p. 10 Eq. 1 |
| VCAP capacitor | VCAP to SOURCE: minimum 0.1 µF (Recommended Operating Conditions: 0.1 µF min); recommended C(VCAP) ≥ 10 × CISS(MOSFET). CIN typ 0.1 µF (≥ 22 nF if supply is more than a few inches away); COUT typ 220 µF | p. 4 §6.3, p. 17 §9.3.1.2.3, p. 20 §10 |

| Pin | Min | Max |
|---|---|---|
| SOURCE to GND | −65 V | 65 V |
| EN to GND, V(SOURCE) > 0 V | −0.3 V | 65 V |

| GATE to SOURCE | −0.3 V | 15 V |
| VCAP to SOURCE | −0.3 V | 15 V |
| Operating junction temperature | −40 °C | 150 °C |
| Storage temperature | −40 °C | 150 °C |

Not rated separately: GND (reference), N.C pins. There is no OUT/drain pin; the drain/load voltage is not seen by the chip.

## Recommended operating conditions (p. 4, §6.3)

SOURCE to GND −60…60 V; EN to GND −60…60 V; C(SOURCE) external ≥ 22 nF (min); C(VCAP–SOURCE) ≥ 0.1 µF (min); external MOSFET VGS rating ≥ 15 V; TJ −40…150 °C.

Notes:
- Part is not a diode-OR/ideal-diode controller: use LM74700-Q1 (TI's own pointer, p. 12) when reverse-current blocking is needed.
- Wiring (Fig. 9-4, p. 16, rendered and read): VBAT → node with EN, SOURCE, TVS, CIN and the VCAP capacitor → Q1 source; Q1 drain → load/COUT; GATE → Q1 gate; GND to ground. SOURCE and EN sit on the battery side, and the FET body diode conducts battery → load. The chip does not sense the drain.
- Turn-on time: only ENTDLY (75 µs typ / 110 µs max, C(VCAP) = 0.1 µF) is specified (p. 6). Eq. 1 (p. 10) came out garbled in text extraction and was not rendered; it appears to be T(DRV_EN) = 75 µs + C(VCAP) × 6.5 V / 300 µA. Total gate-rise time with a given FET is UNVERIFIED (scope plots only, Fig. 9-9 to 9-11, p. 19).
- Land pattern (p. 27, rendered): pads 8X 1.05 × 0.45 mm, pitch 0.65, column centre-to-centre (2.6), rows of 4, pin 1 top-left, pins 1–4 left, 5–8 right (Fig. 5-1, rendered, p. 3). The JLCPCB/EasyEDA footprint (uuid 6283bbf23be94c6f86078b78d2dd7780) was not compared.
- Voltage range: operating input 4–60 V (p. 5) vs 3.2–65 V in the features (p. 1); 3.2 V operation is backed only by the 8 V VCAP−SOURCE figure at VSOURCE = 3.2 V (p. 5). Design to 4–60 V.
- Gate: abs max VGS 15 V; "maximum VGS ... can drive is 13 V" (p. 16) vs "approximately 15 V" (p. 9). No internal gate clamp and no gate resistor in any TI circuit; whether a gate resistor is advisable is UNVERIFIED.
- Reversed input: the gate pull-down is described only as "GATE internally connected to SOURCE" (p. 11); the circuit is not drawn.
- Peak source current is 3 mA min / 11 mA typ (rendered table, p. 5); peak sink 2370 mA typ.
