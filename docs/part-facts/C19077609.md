Notes:
- Fig. 1 reads ≈0.8 kW at 1000 µs, below the 1500 W stated for 10/1000 µs. The two do not agree; use the table figures (Ipp 25.9 A, VC 58.1 V at 10/1000 µs) for design and treat the Fig. 1 absolute level with caution.
- Ipp × VC = 25.9 A × 58.1 V = 1505 W, consistent with the 1500 W rating.
- VC 58.1 V is the maximum at the full 10/1000 µs Ipp. At lower currents VC is lower, but the datasheet has no VC-versus-current curve (UNVERIFIED).
- No datasheet for the reverse leakage at higher temperature (UNVERIFIED). Datasheet text says "Typical IR less than 5 µA above 12 V" (p. 1), table says 1 µA max at 25 °C.
- Junction capacitance: Fig. 4 on p. 4 is a plot against VBR with no value for 36 V; not read.
- Datasheet is the supplier's generic series sheet, not specific to the CA variant beyond the table row.
