## Use in pdm-4ch (RIS, decision 235)

- R103/R107, R203/R207, R303/R307, R403/R407: two in parallel per channel = 500 Ω. Worst-case BTT6050 IIS(FAULT)
  40 mA gives 0.80 W per pair, 0.40 W per part = 53 % of P70 at the 65 °C board temperature (assumption of design-notes §8.1:
  40 °C engine room + enclosure). The ambient limit at 0.40 W is ≈ 110 °C (155 − 0.40/0.75 × 85).
- Film temperature check without relying on the 110 K/W condition: 0.40 W × 150 K/W = 60 K → 125 °C; even at
  200 K/W, 80 K → 145 °C, below 155 °C. Terminal limit at 0.40 W: ≈ 142 °C.
- Voltage: 20 V across the pair at 40 mA (IS compliance VS − 3.5 V caps it lower on a 12 V system); 200 V rating.
- Footprint: KiCad `Resistor_SMD:R_1206_3216Metric` pads 1.125 × 1.75 mm at ±1.4625 mm (G 1.80, Z 4.05, X 1.75)
  against the Vishay land G 1.50, Z 3.60, X 1.80: longer toe and gap, 0.05 mm narrower; the terminals (inner edge
  0.75–1.35 mm from centre, outer edge 1.45–1.65 mm) sit fully on the pads (0.9–2.025 mm). Accepted, no footprint
  change. Before ordering, compare with the JLCPCB footprint of C844867 (pre-order checklist).
