Notes:
- **6 V rating:** V_max is only 6 Vdc, so the part does not suit a 12 V or 24 V rail. It is fine only on logic-level lines.
- **Test conditions:** values apply to a PWB with 0.020" × 1.5 oz copper traces (p. 2). Time to trip is a maximum at 8 A, so it is much longer at lower fault currents. The average time-current curve on p. 3 is a graph only; it was not read as numbers.
- **Datasheet quirks:** the datasheet text has stray spaces in some numbers (for example "1 .200"). B050TF values were read from the row as extracted. The JLCPCB listing matches: Hold 500 mA, Trip 1 A, 6 V, 100 A, Ri min 150 mΩ, R1 max 850 mΩ, 100 ms.
- **Use limits:** the datasheet says PPTCs are secondary protection, not for repeated faults, and the voltage across a tripped device can reach hazardous levels (p. 2).
