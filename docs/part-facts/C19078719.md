Notes:
- The JLCPCB attributes match the datasheet (60 V, 40 A, 0.5 A hold, 1 A trip, 150 ms, 150 mohm, 1.4 ohm, 1.2 W, 4.73 x 3.41 x 1.5 mm).
- Definitions (p. 4): V_max is the maximum voltage the device withstands at I_max; I_max is the maximum fault current at V_max. Operation beyond them may cause arcing or fire (p. 4 caution).
- Time-to-trip is a single maximum point (0.15 s at 8 A), not a curve. The datasheet has a trip-time curve section I did not read: the per-family figures on pp. 9–12 (rendered curves) were not checked, so UNVERIFIED for currents other than 8 A.
- p. 8 derating values are given per family (1812L050), not per voltage variant; the table has a typo-prone layout, so re-check against the page image before relying on the 85 °C value.
- The pdf is a LCSC-hosted copy; not stored in the repository.
