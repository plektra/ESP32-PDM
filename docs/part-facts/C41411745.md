Notes:
- Fig. 1 reads about 0.8 kW at 1000 µs, but the ratings say 1500 W at 10/1000 µs, so the graph's long-pulse end sits below the rating. Do not use Fig. 1 for pulses longer than about 10 µs. Use the table values (21.7 A, 69.4 V) for the 10/1000 µs case.
- The datasheet gives VC only at IPP. VC at lower currents and a VC-vs-current curve are UNVERIFIED (not in the document).
- IR is given at 25 °C only; IR at high temperature is UNVERIFIED.
- With the 0.1 %/K tempco (my arithmetic, not a datasheet figure), VBR min ≈ 47.8 V × (1 + 0.001 × 125) ≈ 53.8 V at 150 °C and ≈ 44.0 V at -55 °C. At -55 °C that is close to VRWM, and the datasheet shows no leakage figure there.
- Electrical table conditions: Ta = 25 °C (p. 2-3 headers). Rows are shared by "A" and "CA" parts.
