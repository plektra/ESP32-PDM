Notes:
- Fig. 1 pixel reading: x axis 0.1 → 1000 µs over 472 px, y axis 0.1 → 100 kW over 417 px. Line runs from 51 kW at 0.1 µs to 0.81 kW at 1000 µs (slope about -0.45 decades of power per decade of time). Reads are ±10 % at best.
- Fig. 1 contradicts p. 1 (1.5 kW at 10/1000 µs vs about 0.81 kW read at 1000 µs). Which is right is not stated. For a conservative design use the lower reading. For 1 ms to 100 ms pulses extrapolating the line gives only a rough guide (about 0.29 kW at 10 ms, 0.10 kW at 100 ms): UNVERIFIED, not design values. A manufacturer with a full curve (Littelfuse, Vishay SMCJ) would be needed.
- VC and IPP apply at 25 °C and 10/1000 µs. VC is the maximum clamp at IPP; VC at lower currents is not given. UNVERIFIED.
- Standoff margin: VBR min 44.4 V is 4.4 V above VRWM 40 V; max clamp 64.5 V at 23.3 A.
- Same datasheet family as the SMCJ30CA file (C19077607). Its Fig. 1 reading at 10 µs (about 9 kW) differs from my reading here (6.4 kW); by the line's own endpoints 6.4 kW is right. C19077607.md should be re-checked.
