Notes:
- VBR is quoted at 25 °C only; no VBR or VC at other temperatures is given.
- Clamping at lower pulse currents or other pulse widths is not tabulated. VC is only given at Ipp for 10/1000 µs.
  For a MOSFET drain–source clamp, 64.5 V max at 77.6 A applies to the 10/1000 µs pulse.
- Fig. 1 and Fig. 2 readings are graph estimates and carry an error of about ±10 %. Only the 5 kW at 1 ms point agrees with a tabulated figure (PPPM).
- The BORN table for 5.0SMDJ40CA matches the LCSC listing (Vrwm 40 V, VBR 44.4 V, Ipp 77.6 A, VC 64.5 V, IR 5 µA).
- Pulse waveform (Fig. 3, p. 5) shows tr and td as symbols only, with no numeric values on the graph. The 10/1000 µs shape is named in the Note 1 text.
