Notes:
- The IS output has no "maximum voltage" apart from the clamp: in normal use it is limited by the sense-range compliance VS − VIS(RANGE) (1 … 3.5 V below VS); with RIS = 1.2 kΩ and IIS = 6 mA, VIS = 7.2 V must stay below VS − 3.5 V (so VS above about 10.7 V). Sizing arithmetic is mine, not in the datasheet.
- IIS(FAULT) is a current source into IS (typ 20 mA, up to 40 mA): with RIS = 1.2 kΩ the voltage is up to 48 V, limited by VS − VIS(RANGE) and by VIS(AZ) 66 … 75 V. Any ADC/MCU input must be clamped or divided. Also derived, not a datasheet figure.
- Min/max kILIS in the table are percentages around the typical; the absolute values in parentheses are computed from them.
- Figure 21 (p. 25) shows min/max/typ IIS against IL (0 … 10 A, 0 … 3 mA axis) as curves only, with no numeric table.
