Notes:
- SOA values are digitised from the log-log plot (pixel measurement on a 2.5×–6× render, axes calibrated from gridlines); treat as ±15 %. The SOA at 30 V is far below the nominal 400 A pulse limit: this FET is a switch-mode part, so linear-mode (slow turn-on, inrush, short-circuit) use at 30 V is strongly limited – 1 ms ≈ 1.5 A, 10 ms ≈ 0.4 A.
- RDS(on) at 100/150 °C are read from the "max" curve in Diagram 9; the 25 °C readback (3.01 max / 2.55 typ) matched the table (3.0 / 2.6), which validates the calibration.
- Gate-charge figures are tabulated at VDD=40 V only (not 25 V); Qg(0–10 V) at lower VDS would be somewhat lower (UNVERIFIED, Diagram 14 not read).
- JLCPCB parametric summary says Vgs(th) 3.8 V, Ciss 5.6 nF, Qg 76 nC, Crss 56 pF: these are the datasheet maxima, not typicals.
