Notes:
- The ESP32-S3-MINI-1 datasheet (A) holds no ADC section of its own: the ADC figures are the chip's (B). The module's 3V3 pin feeds the chip supply pins, and (A) does not give a schematic; connection of 3V3 to VDD3P3 / VDD3P3_RTC / VDD3P3_CPU is UNVERIFIED in this task.
- ADC range at ATTEN3 0…2900 mV is the *effective calibrated* range from (B); the ADC attenuation selection and the nominal input ranges (e.g. 0…~3.1 V) are in the Technical Reference Manual / ESP-IDF docs, not read, so UNVERIFIED here.
- V_IH max (VDD1 + 0.3 V) is a level specification of the digital input; Espressif does not call it an absolute maximum. Design with a series resistor and clamp to 3.3 V if a pin may see more, or may be driven while 3V3 is off.
- The LCSC datasheet is an older (v1.2, 2023) Chinese edition; the figures used here come from the current English editions.
