## Short-circuit and overcurrent (8.3.3, p. 22–25; 6.5/6.6, p. 9–10)

| Item | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| ISCP behaviour | Two-level protection: adjustable overcurrent (IOC, delay tOC) and adjustable short-circuit (ISC, fixed fast tSC). After ISC is exceeded "PD pulls low to SRC within 1.2 µs in TPS48111 and 4 µs in TPS48110"; FLT_I asserts at the same time; CTMR charge/discharge cycles follow as in overcurrent. The comparator drives the **PD** pull-down (the same 4 A driver, R(PD)); no separate gate-discharge current or other pin is stated. | p. 22, 25 |
| tSC (TPS48110) | 4 µs typ, 5 µs max (VCS+–VCS− rising through V(SNS_SCP) to PD falling, CL = 47 nF). TPS48111: 1.16 (typ) / 1.6 µs (max) | p. 10, 6.6 (rendered) |
| ISCP threshold | V(SNS_SCP) = 35 / 40 / 45 mV at RISCP = 2.1 kΩ; 19 mV typ at RISCP = 750 Ω; I(ISCP) bias 13.7 / 15.6 / 17.6 µA | p. 9 |
| Eq. 11 | RISCP (Ω) = ISC × RSNS / 15.6 µ − 464 (rev. E changed −600 to −464) | p. 25, p. 42 |
| Overcurrent threshold | V(SNS_WRN) 29.2 / 30.6 / 31.5 mV at RSET = 100 Ω, RIWRN = 39.7 kΩ; 8 / 10 / 12 mV at RIWRN = 120 kΩ | p. 9 |
| Eq. 6 | RIWRN (Ω) = 11.9 × RSET / (RSNS × IOC + V(OS_SET)); RSET is the resistor from CS+ to VS | p. 22–23 |
| tOC | 25 µs typ / 30 µs max at CTMR = 0; 370 µs typ at CTMR = 22 nF (to PD falling) | p. 10 |
| Auto-retry (8.3.3.1) | CTMR charges at 82 µA; at V(TMR_FLT) 1.1 V FLT_I asserts; at V(TMR_OC) 1.2 V PD pulls low. Then CTMR cycles 2.5 µA down to V(TMR_LOW) 0.2 V and up; after 32 charge/discharge cycles the FET turns on again and FLT_I deasserts after 260 µs. Pulses shorter than tOC leave the FET on and CTMR is discharged. | p. 23 |
| Eq. 7 | CTMR = ITMR × tOC / 1.2, ITMR = 82 µA | p. 23 |
| Eq. 8 | TFLT = 1.1 × CTMR / 82 µ (FLT_I assert delay; 340 µs at 22 nF, p. 10) | p. 23 |
| Eq. 9 | tRETRY = 22.7 × 10^6 × CTMR | p. 23 |
| Latch-off | About 100 kΩ across CTMR clamps its charge below V(TMR_OC); Eq. 10: CTMR = tOC / (RTMR × ln(1 − 1.2/(RTMR × ITMR))), sign as printed on p. 24; latch reset by INP or EN/UVLO toggle or VS below V(VS_PORF) | p. 24 |
| IWRN/CTMR notes | IWRN to GND = OCP off, ISCP to CS− = SCP off; TMR to GND disables overcurrent protection; TMR open = fastest. | p. 6, 23 |
| Over temperature | TPS48110: 150 °C trips, FLT_T low, auto-retry 512 ms after cooling to 130 °C | p. 27, p. 10 |
