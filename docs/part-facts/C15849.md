Notes:
- The ±15 % X5R figure is the "with no bias" temperature limit; it does not cover DC-bias loss. Class II MLCCs lose capacitance under DC bias, much more in 0603 at 1 µF; at 12–14 V (≈25–28 % of 50 V) a loss is expected but its size is not given here. Do not assume the 1 µF nominal: take the figure from Samsung's simulation tool before relying on it.
- Catalog is a 2015 edition covering many series; the LCSC copy may differ from the current maker datasheet.
