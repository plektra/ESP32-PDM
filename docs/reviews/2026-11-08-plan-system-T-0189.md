# Plan, scope system, 2026-11-08 (T-0189)

Approved items: T-0187 and T-0188. Both are two-way cost levers (L5, L6) from `docs/reviews/2026-11-07-cost-system-2.md`. There are no owner rulings. No board is ordered.

## Packages

- **P1 (shared): C3 → C170101 on `power-usb`, both boards (T-0187).**
  - C3 is 2.2 µF/50 V X5R 0805 C377773 on +3V3. It is the only user of C377773 on each board (checked with `sch_query parts`).
  - It becomes C170101, 2.2 µF/100 V X7R 1206. That part is already on both BOMs (C1, C22, C30 and others).
  - The value is unchanged. The swap saves 1.53 $ per order per board design and costs +0.08 $ per part.
- **P2 (pdm-hc2): R105/R205 → C17927 (T-0188), after P1.**
  - R105/R205 are 1 MΩ 0402 C26083, from VS to EN/UVLO. They are the only users of C26083.
  - They become C17927, 1 MΩ 1206. That part is already on the BOM as R13.
  - The value and the UV/OV thresholds are unchanged. The voltage rating on VS goes up. The swap saves 1.53 $ per order.
  - P2 follows P1 only so that the two packages do not edit the pdm-hc2 project and its STATUS.md at the same time.

Both items are swaps to parts already on the BOM. No new parts, footprints or options.

## Deferred

None.

## Decisions

None to draft: neither item carries an owner ruling. The board-fixers record their swaps as **auto** decisions.
