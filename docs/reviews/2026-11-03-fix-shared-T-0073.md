## T-0043: VBAT full scale ≈ 32 V (ruling Q-0035)

- design-notes §2 had no full-scale figure. I added the VBAT_SENSE line:
  - The divider is 220 k / 22 k, a ratio of 1/11.
  - The ADC is calibrated for 0–2900 mV (ESP32-S3 datasheet v2.2, Table 5-6, as cited in the validator report).
  - Full scale is therefore 31.9 V, about 32 V. At 30 V the pin sees 2.73 V, which still reads.
- Decision 38 in `decisions.md` already amends decision 18. I did not edit `decisions.md`.
- Both STATUS files point to the amendment under "For /system".

## T-0051 / T-0060: IO3 strap

- design-notes §2 now has the controller/firmware rule: never burn `EFUSE_STRAP_JTAG_SEL`. IO3 is held low by:
  - pdm-4ch: IS2 through R204 10 k + R203 680 Ω, about 10.7 k to GND;
  - pdm-hc2: IMON2 through R212 10 k + R211 12 k, 22 k to GND.
- I added the same rule to the production notes, the "Pre-order checklist" in both STATUS.md files.
