## Packages

- **P1 – pdm-4ch routing to 0 unconnected under the Q-0077 standing rule** (`pcb-fixer`, scope `pdm-4ch`). This is
  layout only: small-signal parts move, signal copper is rerouted, the In2 exit and its VS-In2 notch grow, and
  stitched GND fragments are allowed. No schematic change is needed, so no board-fixer package goes before it. The
  first targets are the ones STATUS names as blocked under the old rules:
  - the ch2/ch3 resistor blocks (R201/R202, R301/R302), which box SW_IN2/SW_IN3 and ch3 R302.1 /DEN;
  - a wider exit for SW_IN1/SW_IN4;
  - the U1 strip and the analog block.

  The rule's limits are the gates. If a stage gains nothing, or needs one of the items that stay fixed, the package
  stops and returns.
