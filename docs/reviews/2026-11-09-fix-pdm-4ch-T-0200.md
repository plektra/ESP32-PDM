# pdm-4ch layout stage 4: power copper (T-0200, 2026-11-09, unattended)

## What the stage drew

The board has 18 zones, 103 vias (0.8/0.4 mm) and 17 short track segments. Details: design-notes §8.12. The coordinates are mm from the top-left corner.

- **VBAT_IN, both layers:**
  - Covers x 5–37.6, y 34.6–45.3, and reaches y 48.8 at JP30. About 3.4 cm² per layer.
  - Joins both J10.1 pins, D30, C30 and JP30.2, with 36 stitching vias.
  - Thermal spokes of 1.5 mm, because J10 is hand-soldered.
- **VBAT_FL, both layers:** covers the Q30 source pins, JP30.1, D31's anode and U30 pin 1 (through a via), with 10 stitching vias.
- **VS, top layer:**
  - The pour on Q30's drain tab is 2.67 cm² (rule ≥ 2 cm²).
  - C31, F4.1, C304/C303, D31's cathode and F1.1 sit on it.
  - The surge loop switches → VS → Q30 → JP30 → D30 is one compact area.
- **VS, bottom layer:**
  - A spine under U12/U13/Q30/D31, a front band for U11 and a rear band for U14, about 11 cm² together.
  - 40 vias join the top drain pour to the spine.
  - The C104/C103/C204/C203/C404/C403 VS pads join through a via each. C203 also has a stub to U12's tab.
- **Outputs:**
  - OUT1 is a bottom band with four vias to U11's pins.
  - OUT2 and OUT3 run on top through the corridor between the switch rows. OUT4 runs on top below D403.
  - Each output passes over its DNP TVS pad to J11, and each TVS GND pad has a via into the bottom GND.
- **GND, both layers:** fills the rest of the board.
  - A 3 mm strip along the power edge takes the J11 TVS returns to J10.2 without crossing VBAT_IN.
  - The pads connect solid: thermal reliefs gave 28 "starved thermal" errors, and GND carries no load current here.
- **CAN barrier:**
  - GND stops at x 67.6, and the GND_CAN island (both layers) starts at x 75.7, an 8.1 mm gap across U5.
  - At the J5/J4 corner the gap is 4.0 mm, the PCB rule 9 minimum away from the body.
  - **C70 is turned 180°** (the only part moved): its GND_CAN pad faced the GND side, so its stubs would have crossed. Each pad now has a short stub to its own side.
- **Keep-outs:** the antenna strip x 46.55–91.95, y 0–4.5 has no copper on either layer, checked on the filled zones. The four hole keep-outs are free (`hole_keepout: []`).
- **Clearances:** copper is at least 1.0 mm from VS/VBAT_IN/VBAT_FL to any other net (measured on the fills, EMC 2026-11-03 rule 7). OUT copper has 0.4 mm.

## Checks

- `kpcb drc`: 0 errors, 403 warnings (silkscreen overlaps and edge clipping, plus the FL30 missing-footprint notice). 256 unconnected, down from 469 before the stage. Parity 0. No SMT part on the bottom, nothing in a hole keep-out, nothing parked.
- Measured on the fills: GND to GND_CAN ≥ 4.0 mm everywhere, 8.1 mm across U5. VS to GND/VBAT_FL/OUT ≥ 1.0 mm on both layers.
- `python3 tools/fix_check.py --scope pdm-4ch`: **OK**. ERC 0 errors, 5 warnings; parts, DNP and unique counts unchanged.

## Findings and open points for the next stages

- FINDING (LOW): JP30 is still the 2 × 2 mm net tie (`NetTie-2_SMD_Pad2.0mm`), top layer only, whereas the STATUS rule asks for a bridge "as wide as the VS copper on both layers".
  - The bar is one square of 2 oz copper, about 0.25 mΩ, so about 64 mW at 16 A, sunk into the pours on both sides. That is acceptable for this prototype.
  - A wider two-layer tie is a footprint change in the lib and schematic. It is not done here. Revisit it with the FL30 decision at pre-compliance.
- FINDING (LOW): the OUT3 copper narrows to about 1.2 mm beside C302 (x 44.6–45.8), and OUT2 to about 1.7 mm by D101/D203.
  - Both are enough for the 4 A rating in 2 oz over a few mm.
  - Their pulse rating against the BTT6050 short-circuit limit is not checked; that is for the validator (pcb).
- **For routing (T-0201):**
  - U30 pin 5, C32 pad 2 (VBAT_FL; the bottom VBAT_FL pour lies under them), U30 VCAP to C32 and GATE to Q30.1.
  - R60 to VS (top pour or spine).
  - R106 to OUT1 (between FB1 and R106.2, too tight for a stub here).
  - C102 and C202/C402 are joined already.
  - The ground returns of the switch logic parts go into the bottom GND in the corridor and on the U11/U14 logic sides, which are GND on the bottom.
  - The C104/C103/C204/C203 GND pads sit over the bottom VS front band, so they need a top path to GND.
  - Signals crossing the VS spine on the bottom cut the pour; keep such crossings few and short.
- **For the silkscreen stage (T-0202):** none from this stage.

The battery path and the outputs are safety-relevant (outputs may feed a bilge pump or navigation lights). A competent person reviews the board before installation.
