## T-0054 / T-0006 / T-0061 – gate slew control: BLOCKED (one-way)

Sizing per the ruling: R1 = 100 kΩ, Eq. 3 with V(BST–SRC) = 12 V, 1000 µF, 20 A → C1 = 3.78 nF → **3.9 nF** (E12),
as the owner expected. Ramp at 30 V: t = C·V/I = 1.5 ms. Eq. 4: 2 × 76 nC + 10 × 3.9 nF = 191 nC < 1 µF CBST, OK.

The SOA check fails. In the common-source back-to-back pair, the input-side MOSFET (Q11/Q21) carries the whole
VS − VOUT during the ramp. The output-side MOSFET conducts through its channel or body diode. Calculation
(`$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/scripts/slew_soa.py`: power profile (V − vout) × I, superposed on the Zth max curve, log-log
interpolated):

| CLOAD | I_inrush | ramp | P peak | E in Q11 | ΔTj above case (Zth) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1000 µF | 20 A | 1.5 ms | 600 W | 0.45 J | **100 K** (125 K with a 15 A resistive load attached) |
| 1000 µF | 10 A | 3.0 ms | 300 W | 0.45 J | 68 K |
| 1000 µF | 5 A | 6.0 ms | 150 W | 0.45 J | 41 K |
| 470 µF | 20 A | 0.7 ms | 600 W | 0.21 J | 68 K |
| 220 µF | 20 A | 0.33 ms | 600 W | 0.10 J | 46 K |
| 100 µF | 20 A | 0.15 ms | 600 W | 0.045 J | 31 K |

- **SOA (Diagram 3):** the 1 ms line allows ≈ 1.5 A × 30 V ≈ 45 W. The ruled ramp needs 600 W for 1.5 ms, about
  13 × too much. Slower ramps make it worse, because the 10 ms line allows only ≈ 12 W while the energy stays 0.45 J.
  Even 100 µF at 20 A (600 W for 150 µs) is above the 100 µs line (≈ 450 W). This OptiMOS 5 part is made for
  switch mode. Its curves fall steeply at 30 V (∝ VDS⁻², the thermal-instability region), so it cannot run in linear
  mode for milliseconds.
- **Thermal (Zth alone, independent of how the SOA plot is read):** ΔTj ≈ 100 K above the case at 20 A / 1000 µF.
  With the case at 40–60 °C that reaches 140–160 °C. On a retry after running at 15 A (Tj ≈ 87 °C, heat budget) it
  reaches ≈ 190 °C. Either way it is over 150 °C.
- **Short-circuit response (checked as asked):** the PD path stays direct, so turn-off would not be slowed (tSC
  4–5 µs, 4 A PD sink). **Turn-on into a short gets worse with slew control**, though. The gate then rises slowly
  through the linear region with the full 30 V across Q11 until 80 A trips ISCP: about 1 kW for ≈ 100 µs with
  100 kΩ / 3.9 nF (τ = 0.39 ms), against ≈ 450 W at 100 µs on the SOA. The fast trip itself is not delayed, but the
  MOSFET would be outside SOA before it trips.

So no R1/C1 pair meets both the ~20 A / 1000 µF target (Q-0045) and the MOSFET SOA. The brief says to stop here: the
answer needs either a maximum load capacitance or a different MOSFET (decision 19, **[proposed]**). R104/R204
therefore stay 10 Ω PU → gate. No C1, R2 or RGATE_PD was added, and RG_PU 100 Ω was not fitted (Q-0043 supersedes
it). The no-PWM rule of Q-0001 is recorded.

Options for the owner (not done, all need a ruling):
1. **State a maximum load capacitance** for HC2 outputs and keep the BSC030N08NS5, with a short slew (tens of µs)
   mainly for EMC E2. The SOA allows only about ≤ 50–100 µF at ~20 A. It can be set once the prototype measures the
   real loads.
2. **Linear-mode MOSFET** with a guaranteed SOA (hot-swap/"linear FET" class) instead of Q11/Q21 only, the input
   side. This is a part change under decision 19, at some extra cost and with its RDS(on) and heat budget to check.
3. **Pre-charge path**: TPS48111-Q1 has a separate G pin for a pre-charge FET + resistor (TI's method for large
   capacitive loads). This is a controller variant change with added parts.

## Failure-mode note (bilge forced-run)

Nothing on the outputs changed, so their behaviour is as before: hard turn-on, 80 A fast trip, 25 A / 146 ms
breaker, 3.8 min retry. Points that matter for safety while this is open:
- A bilge pump or a load driver with a large input capacitance (more than ~50–100 µF) can trip the 80 A fast
  short-circuit protection at switch-on (T-0054 estimate). The output then stays off for ≈ 3.8 min, also on the
  hardware forced-run path. The float switch stays independent (invariant 8). Measure each intended load on the
  prototype (Q-0045).
- A stalled pump draws a steady overcurrent. The 25 A / 146 ms breaker turns it off, and it retries every 3.8 min.
  The slow-ramp design would have added linear-mode stress here at every retry. That is a further reason not to fit
  it with this MOSFET.
- A competent person must review the bilge outputs before installation.
