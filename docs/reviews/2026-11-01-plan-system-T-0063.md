Why the packages are split this way:
- **`shared` scope.** Everything that edits a shared sheet, `lib/` or `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` is `shared`. P9 changes mostly
  HC2 hardware, but it also changes the ARCHITECTURE HC2 budget and the pin map (IO4/IO5), so it is `shared` as well.
- **One sheet at a time.** P1 touches `channel` and `hc_channel`, so P6 and P7 wait for it. P7 and P8 both edit
  `hc_channel`; the filters in P8 are checked against the protection timing that P7 sets.
- **`design-notes` §8.** P5 and P9 both edit it, so P9 runs after P5.
- **BOM codes last.** P10 waits for every package that swaps parts, so the codes it writes are the final ones.
- **Q-0001 superseded.** The owner wrote that Q-0043 replaces the plain RG_PU 100 Ω of Q-0001. Both items are in P7, and Q-0043 is
  the one that applies.
- **Check and swap together.** Every part check that decides a swap is in the same package as the swap:
  - SMAJ33CA → P1;
  - H5VL10B and the ESD TLP check, L78M05, the 1812L050 Imax check together with the F2 swap → P2;
  - CA-IS3050G → P4.

### Points for the owner raised while planning

1. **HC2 breaker against decision 19.** Q-0042 sets the HC2 hardware breaker to about 17.5 A (RIWRN 68 k). Decision 19
   **[proposed]** still foresees a ≈ 21 A load on HC2, and that load would trip the breaker. P9 applies the ruling and must
   not touch decision 19. The board-fixer raises it as an owner question: either a per-build RIWRN for a 21 A build, or drop
   that option.
2. **Heat-budget tool.** No package gate allows changes to `tools/heat_budget.py`. T-0057 (BSC030 RDS(on) 11.7 mΩ) is
   therefore deferred to the owner. Until then the packages run their checks on a scratch copy with 11.7 mΩ and `--p-other`
   ≈ 2.1 W (4-ch) / 1.9 W (HC2). For T-0047, P5 documents the 2.1 W. Changing the tool's default is an owner or `/system`
   job.
3. **Keyed J10.** Q-0044 asks for a keyed/polarised J10, but J10 is not chosen yet. P9 writes the warnings and the
   design-notes correction now. The keyed J10 requirement goes into "For /system" in STATUS, for `/system mechanical`.
4. **SW_SUPPLY diode and the switch margin.** The series diode of Q-0040 makes the switch ON margin at 10.5 V even smaller
   (V2/Q-0034). P5 has a stop condition on invariant 1 for this.
