# T-0158: pdm-4ch 16 A input: J10 set and Q30 re-selection

Date: 2026-11-07. Scope: `pdm-4ch`. Item: T-0148 (owner ruling "yes", 2026-11-06; decisions 103, 104). Unattended.

**Result: done, with one thing the owner needs to know. C577772, the J10 socket, is out of stock at JLCPCB today (0, minimum order 18).**
The schematic names it as ruled, but it cannot be bought until it is restocked. No stop condition applied.

## What changed

**J10 (root sheet, fields only).** KF2EDGSRAM-10.16-2P C6394053 + C6394029 became KF2EDGSRM-7.62-2P C577772 + plug C577752, footprint `ESP32-PDM:Kefa_KF2EDGSRM-7.62-2P`. Pin 1 stays VBAT_IN and pin 2 stays GND. D30, C30, FL30 and JP30 are untouched.

- **Footprint against the drawing (rendered and read, rev. A 2023-05-12): pass, not changed, so HC2 J11 is unaffected.** Rows and columns 7.62 mm; drill 1.5 mm; flange holes Ø3.2 mm, 22.86 mm apart and y = 11 mm (13 mm from the rear edge, first row 2 mm from it); body 30.48 × 29 mm. The facts file left open which side the second row is on. The side view settles it: pins at the rear, mating face opposite.
- **The drawing does not number the poles.** VBAT_IN on pad 1 is this design's choice, to be marked at layout.
- **16 A under derating: the drawings publish no derating curve and no contact resistance.** The stop condition is not met, but this is an estimate, not a datasheet result. 16 A is 53 % of 30 A UL, so about 28 % of the contact heat at rated current. Assuming a 30 K rise at rated current, that is about 8.5 K: about 59 °C in decision 97's 50 °C air, against the 105 °C part limit.

**Q30 (root sheet, fields only).** IPT007N06N C122552 became AGM601LL C5464765. Footprint, wiring and C32 are unchanged. The symbol stays `ESP32-PDM:IPT007N06N` with value AGM601LL (same pins, same practice as Q3–Q6); a clone in `lib/` would add nothing electrically.

Cost: Q30 0.85 instead of about 3.55 USD per board, no change in unique parts or loading fees. Decision 91 (an auto decision, rejected at 40 A only) is replaced.

**Decision 96 recheck.** D31 SMDJ40CA is unchanged.

- **VDS:** 59.4 V at 31 A is set by D31 and is below AGM601LL's 60 V minimum. With D31 at 47 °C it is about 60.4 V, as the owner's basis already says. AGM601LL's rating-versus-temperature coefficient is not in its facts file. Any small avalanche share is bounded by the 190 mJ pulse against EAS 1225 mJ (25 °C start).
- **VS:** −4.0 V at 31 A, independent of the FET.
- **Energy:** about 190 mJ in D31, ×5.3 margin at 25 °C, independent of the FET.
- **Steady reversal ≤ 28 V:** D31 does not conduct; Q30 IDSS ≤ 1 µA, the same as before.

**Invariant 4.** The path OUT → VS → Q30 → D30 is intact. The added drop is 0.18 V at D30's about 62 A (0.10 V more than IPT007N06N); IDM is 892 A, against 1200 A before.

**Heat (decision 9).** Tool results are under Checks. By hand at 16 A with the §8.6 method: loss 0.51–0.55 W; 66 / 72 / 80 °C at 40 / 50 / 62 K/W; 85 °C at 62 K/W in 50 °C air. **AGM's 50 K/W has no stated copper area and is not relied on.** The 40 and 62 K/W are Infineon's TOLL figures, used as an analogue (an assumption). Tj stays ≤ 125 °C up to RthJA ≈ 118 K/W, so the result is not sensitive to this.

**Copper rule.** ≥ 2 cm² of 2 oz VS copper joined to the drain tab, replacing the 6 cm² of decisions 90 and 95. That is the tab land (0.8 cm²) plus D31's terminal copper (0.64 cm²), rounded up. The Tj is taken at the minimal-pad figure, so the 2 cm² is margin, not a calculated RthJA.

**Documents.** README line 68 (16 A); design-notes §4, §7 (connectors, footprint check, rating), §8.6 (part, gate drive, heat, D31 recheck, failure modes, PCB rules, cost); `hardware/pdm-4ch/STATUS.md` (entry, Next, sample check and stock lines, For /system (a) and (b)). pdm-hc2 and its 36 A are unchanged. New facts files: `docs/part-facts/C577772.md`, `C577752.md`.

**Failure modes (battery path).** Added to §8.6: more than 16 A (nothing on the board limits the sum, the fuse must; four BTT6010 at 8 A would take Q30 to about 179 °C or runaway); a bad J10 joint or loose plug; a pdm-4ch plug in a pdm-hc2 J11 socket. **A competent person reviews the battery path (J10, Q30/U30, D31) before installation**, because F4 feeds the panel switches for navigation lights and bilge forced run.

## Findings and open points

- **C577772 stock 0 (MAJOR for ordering, not for the design).** It was 40 on 2026-11-05; live `jlc_get_part` and `jlc_stock_check` both show 0 today, minimum order 18 (about 9.4 USD at the 10+ price). C577752 has 63. The socket is hand-soldered (decision 85), so another seller would do, but none was searched for. If it stays out of stock, the fallback is the old 10.16 mm set (`orchestrate.py undo T-0148` also undoes Q30), or a socket with the same drawing and a part check.
- **Plug fit at the 36 mm rail pitch not settled (MINOR).** C577752's 30.60 mm overall height would be 36.6 mm if it lay across the rail. The drawing does not settle this, so the sample check decides. The same plug is already on HC2 J11.
- **AGM601LL gaps in the datasheet (MINOR):** no Ciss max, no ISM, no hot avalanche curve, RthJA copper area unstated. The hot avalanche gap matters only in the double fault of D31 failed open plus a negative spike (§8.6 failure modes). The margin there is unverified.
- 4 × 4.5 A now reads OVER instead of marginal with the conservative `--r-path 0.0069`. It is above the 4 A rating either way.
- The owner's choice on cross-board keying and the ARCHITECTURE rows are in STATUS "For /system".
