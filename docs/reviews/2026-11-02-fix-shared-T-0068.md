# Fix report T-0068 – panel interface (`analog_sw`, shared), 2026-11-02

Unattended run (decision 23). The `analog_sw.kicad_sch` sheet was edited by script on `pdm-4ch` and copied to `pdm-hc2`.
The two files differ only in the `(project "…")` name.

## Per item

### T-0012 (Q-0006) – debounce: done
- C14–C17 changed from 100 nF (C307331) to **470 nF/10 V X5R 0402, CL05A474KP5NNNC, C47339 (preferred)**. Same footprint,
  on the node after R34–R37. The zener holds that node at ≤ ≈ 3.3 V, so 10 V is ample.
- τ changes from 0.32 ms to ≈ 1.5 ms (3.2 kΩ × 470 nF). The DC-bias loss at ≈ 3 V is UNVERIFIED (the Samsung catalogue has
  no bias curve), so the real τ is somewhat lower.
- README: added the dry (unlit) contacts note and the 12 V cranking note. Outputs go off below about 8 V at the switch,
  ≈ 8.5 V at the battery with D55.
- Facts: `docs/part-facts/C47339.md`.

### T-0049 (Q-0040) – SW_SUPPLY series diode: done
- **D55 DSK16 (C22466351, preferred)**. The part is already on both BOMs (D2/D3/D11), so it adds no unique part.
- Wiring: F4.2 → anode (new net `Net-(D55-A)`), cathode → SW_SUPPLY. The label moved from F4.2 to the D55 cathode, so J5.5,
  D54 and C54 are still on one net at the connector and D54 still clamps J5.5.
- Ratings, from the datasheet (`docs/part-facts/C22466351.md`):
  - VRRM 60 V (meets ≥ 60 V);
  - IF(AV) 1 A, which meets F4's I_trip of 1.0 A (`docs/part-facts/C19078719.md`: I_hold 0.5 A, 0.15 s at 8 A);
  - IFSM 25 A.
- Rechecked the switch thresholds with the diode drop:
  - The diode costs 0.3–0.7 V. 0.70 V is the only VF limit in the datasheet (at 1 A); the low-current VF is UNVERIFIED.
  - The ON threshold seen from the battery rises from ≈ 8 V to ≈ 8.3–8.7 V.
  - The Q-0034 margin is under T-0042.
- Stop condition (invariant 1) checked, not met:
  - D55 is upstream of SWIN, so at SWIN ≥ 10.5 V the IN path is unchanged.
  - Even at a 10.5 V *battery* (SWIN 9.8–10.1 V) with the 3.3 V rail down, IN reaches ≈ 2.05 V before turn-on. That is
    ≥ VIN(H) min 2.0 V, so turn-on is guaranteed; with the RIN 4.7 k fallback it is ≈ 2.17 V. No divider change is needed.
- Failure-mode note in design-notes §5. It covers navigation lights and bilge forced run, the 470 nF, low battery, D55
  open or short, and a panel-cable short through D55. It includes the reminder that a competent person reviews it before
  installation.

### T-0042 (Q-0034) – margin claim: done (documentation, no circuit change)
- design-notes §3: the "~0.4 V margin" sentence is replaced by a worst-case table:
  - SWIN 10.5 V, with the 3.3 V rail down and up;
  - a 10.5 V battery behind D55;
  - each case with and without RIN 4.7 k.
- Result: turn-on is guaranteed. Once the channel is on and the 3.3 V rail is down, IN − device GND is ≈ 1.7–1.9 V
  (≈ 1.55–1.75 V behind D55). That is not guaranteed to hold the channel on, even with RIN 4.7 k (≈ 1.85–2.0 V).
  - This was already true before this package, and the owner's ruling covers it: no circuit change, measure.
  - The diode only lowers the case where the battery, not SWIN, is at 10.5 V.
- pdm-4ch STATUS has a new "Prototype test list" with the IN − GND measurement and the RIN R101–R401 4.7 kΩ fallback.

### T-0047 – p_other: done (documentation)
- design-notes §4: on the 4-ch board at 30 V with all panel lines on, the panel interface adds ≈ 1.6 W:
  - switch loads 0.75 W;
  - dividers and zeners 0.31 W;
  - LED sinks 0.55 W;
  - D55 + F4 0.03 W.
- That gives p_other ≈ 2.2 W (the validator estimated 2.1 W).
- Scratch runs at 4 A: `heat_budget.py --p-other 2.1` and `--p-other 2.2` both give Tj 101 °C (100 °C at 0.6 W). The
  rating stands.
- The tool default is unchanged (tools/ is outside the gates). It is listed under "For /system".

### T-0050 – reverse battery qualification: done (documentation)
- design-notes §8 and the README (pdm-4ch) now state the datasheet limits:
  - VS(REV) ≤ 28 V, t < 2 min, RL ≥ 12 Ω, TA 25 °C (P_4.1.2);
  - heavier loads need the battery fuse to clear: ≈ 2.4 W in the body diode against PTOT 1.6 W.
- Both texts also mention the panel-LED protection by D55.
- The HC2 part of §8 was not written (P9).

### T-0046 (Q-0038) – switch-load resistors: BLOCKED (one-way)
- The ruling asks for "2.2 k + 2.4 k, basic parts" in 1206. JLCPCB has **no basic or preferred 1206 2.2 k or 2.4 k**.
  Those values exist as basic parts only in 0402/0603/0805.
- The basic 1206 values are 0, 0.1, 1, 10, 20, 22, 100, 120, 180, 300, 750 Ω, 1 k, 2 k, 4.7 k, 10 k, 100 k, 1 M and 1.2 M.
- Every way to do it departs from the ruling: other values, extended parts (two loading fees), or another package. The
  conditional check failed, so the item is not done. R3/R4/R7/R8 stay 4.7 k (0.19 W at 30 V, 0.49 W at the 48 V clamp).
