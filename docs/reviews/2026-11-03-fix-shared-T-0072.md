# Fix T-0072 (shared scope; hardware on pdm-hc2) – 2026-11-03

HC2 breaker level, float-run OUT divider, reverse-battery documentation. Board-fixer, unattended run (decision 23).
Shared sheets (`power-usb`, `can`, `analog_sw`) not touched; pdm-4ch unchanged.

## Per item

### T-0053 (Q-0042): breaker RIWRN 68 k
- **R113/R213 47 k (C25792) → 68 k 0402 1 % UNI-ROYAL 0402WGF6802TCE, C36871 (preferred).** Same footprint; C25792
  had no other use, so unique parts stay the same.
- Eq. 6 (SLUSEE5E p. 22–23): VSNS = 11.9 × 100 Ω / 68 k = 17.5 mV, so IOC = 17.5 A with RSNS 1 mΩ.
  V(OS_SET) = 0 ± 0.2 mV (p. 9; added to `docs/part-facts/C17556513.md`).
- Spread: V(SNS_WRN) has table rows only at 39.7 k (29.2/30.6/31.5 mV) and 120 k (8/10/12 mV). Interpolating
  between them gives −1.8/+1.6 mV at 17.5 mV. With 1 % resistors, IOC ≈ **15.2–19.7 A**. This is `UNVERIFIED`
  because there is no 68 k row.
- tOC is unchanged: 10 µF × 1.2 V / 82 µA = 146 ms. The fast trip stays at ≈ 80 A.
- Heat check on a scratch copy of `heat_budget.py` with an 11.7 mΩ path, 40 °C, both channels loaded:

  | Current | Tj |
  |---|---|
  | 15 A | 88 °C |
  | 17.5 A | 111 °C |
  | 18 A | 117 °C |
  | 18.5 A | 123 °C |
  | 19.7 A | 139 °C |

  With `--p-other 1.9`: 113 °C at 17.5 A and 118 °C at 18 A. Every case is below the 150 °C Tj max of the
  BSC030N08NS5. The real tool was not changed.
- ARCHITECTURE budget row: "breaker ~17.5 A / 146 ms (≈ 15–20 A over tolerances); fast trip 80 A; external breaker
  ≤ 15–18 A per output plus firmware soft fuse".
- README: external breaker ≤ 15–18 A per output, plus the label requirement. The board table now says ≈ 17.5 A.
- Firmware soft-fuse requirement: in HC2 STATUS "For /system".
- Pre-order checklist: "measure the bilge pump start current before ordering".
- No measured or datasheet start current exists for the pump (SEAFLO does not publish one), so the stop condition
  did not apply. The value stays as ruled.
- Failure mode (bilge forced run): design-notes §4a.2.

### T-0062 (Q-0046): OUT divider on IO4/IO5
- New parts on the HC2 root sheet. All of them are already on the BOM (VBAT_SENSE parts):
  - **OUT1 → IO4:** R63 220 k (C25767), R64 22 k (C25768), C62 100 nF (C307331).
  - **OUT2 → IO5:** R65, R66, C63, same parts.
- The no-connects on U1 IO4/IO5 are removed. The parts are placed next to the controller part, which itself is
  unchanged.
- IO4 = ADC1_CH3 and IO5 = ADC1_CH4 (ESP32-S3-MINI-1 Table 3-1), so invariant 10 holds.
- Full scale ≈ 31.9 V (ATTEN3, 2.9 V); 28.8 V reads as 2.62 V. Time constant τ = 2 ms.
- Checks:
  - **Unpowered ESP32 with a float switch driving OUT:** the pin stays ≤ 2.62 V (Thevenin source) and the
    injection is ≤ 0.13 mA. Espressif gives no injection limit (part facts C2913206, new file, `UNVERIFIED`).
  - **Freewheel:** −45 mV at the pin.
  - **Reverse / negative OUT:** ≤ 0.14 mA through the pin's ESD diode to GND.
  - **D103 clamp (53 V):** ≤ 0.24 mA.
- Failure mode and invariant 8: the divider loads the pump feed with 0.12 mA, or 0.13 mA with a shorted lower part.
  The float circuit stays wired to the battery. Details in design-notes §4a.2.
- ARCHITECTURE pin-map row: "IO4/IO5 OUT1/OUT2 sense".

### T-0058: design-notes corrections
- §1 now names the TPS48110A-Q1, ≈ 15 A continuous and the ≈ 17.5 A breaker.
- §9 bilge item: float runs are seen by the OUT divider on IO4/IO5, and by SW3/SW4 if they are wired. IMON cannot
  see them.
- §4a: RIWRN row and heat paragraph updated.

### T-0055 (Q-0044): reverse battery, documentation
- README warning: a reversed battery destroys both HC2 controllers. Use the keyed/polarised J10; the label carries
  the warning.
- design-notes §8: new HC2 paragraph after the pdm-4ch reverse-battery paragraph. It says the survival statement
  does not apply to HC2 and explains why.
- HC2 STATUS "For /system": J10 keyed/polarised (mechanical; J10 is not chosen yet).
- HC2 STATUS next-revision list: full reverse-polarity protection.

## Open points
- **Decision 19 conflict (owner, one-way):**
  - Decision 19 [proposed] plans an ≈ 21 A load on HC2. The ≈ 17.5 A breaker (15.2–19.7 A) excludes that load.
  - The owner chooses: a per-build RIWRN for that output (e.g. 47 k ≈ 25 A, which brings back the unprotected band
    of finding H1 unless the MOSFETs of decision 19 are fitted), or drop the 21 A option.
- The bilge pump start current must be measured before ordering. If the start current stays above ≈ 15 A for more
  than ≈ 120 ms, the issue goes back to the owner.
- The V(SNS_WRN) spread at 17.5 mV is interpolated. Check the trip level on the prototype.
- A competent person reviews the bilge wiring, the breaker sizes and the OUT-divider failure mode before
  installation.
