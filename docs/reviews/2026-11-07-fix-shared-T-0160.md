# T-0160: sign-of-life LEDs, spare GPIO and serial console access (scope shared)

Items T-0150, T-0151 and T-0152, owner rulings "yes" (2026-11-06). 2026-11-07, unattended.

**Result:** all three items are done on both boards, in the controller part of the root sheet. The only difference between the boards is J6 pins 4–6, which are not connected on pdm-hc2 as the brief asked. The optional CAN TX/RX LEDs were checked and left out.
Each board gains +4 fitted parts, +1 DNP footprint and +1 unique line (C2286).

## Per item

**Pin check (brief step 1).** Facts are in `docs/part-facts/C2913206.md`, T-0160 section, from ESP32-S3-MINI-1 v1.7 Table 3-1 and ESP32-S3 v2.2 Tables 2-1, 2-4, 2-14 and 3-5.
- None of IO13, IO35, IO36, IO37, IO42, TXD0 or RXD0 is a strapping pin.
- None of them is used inside the module on N8 (quad flash, no PSRAM). IO33–IO37 are taken only by octal SPI.
- Reset state: IO13, IO35–37 and IO42 are inputs with no pull. TXD0 and RXD0 have a weak pull-up, and TXD0 carries the ROM boot log.
- IO42 is the MTMS pad, but it is a plain GPIO while the eFuses are at their default.
- IO45 and IO46 are not used.

The stop condition did not trigger.

**T-0150: LEDs.**
- **LED part:** one new line, the basic 0603 red KT-0603R (`docs/part-facts/C2286.md`): 25 mA abs max, VR 5 V, Vf ≈ 1.85 V at 1 mA.
- **Current:** with 2.2 k C25879 each LED gets about 0.66–0.7 mA, at least 9× below the rating.
- **Brightness:** an extrapolated 6–12 mcd. That is visible indoors and dim in daylight.
- **IO13 drive:** IO13 has 20 mA default drive. Even at the VOH floor of 2.64 V the LED still lights at about 0.38 mA.
- **Status LED off at start-up:** with IO13 undriven (input, no pull), D7 stays off.
- **Placement:** status LED in the controller part of the root sheet, and the power LED next to it on the root sheet, the same on both boards.
- **Invariant 1:** neither LED touches the switch-to-output path.
- **Continuous draw:** D8 draws about 0.7 mA (2.3 mW) all the time, also in Eco and Sleep (decision 13). That is about 0.1 mA at 24 V and 0.2 mA at 12 V from the battery with ideal conversion; the buck's light-load efficiency is not bounded.

These figures are added to the ARCHITECTURE controller power row, with the calculation in design-notes §8.7.

**T-0150: optional CAN LEDs, left out.**
- An LED + 2.2 k to GND on TXD fails the check. While IO17 is undriven, R70 10 k and the LED path form a divider that holds TXD at about 2.0 V. That is exactly the CA-IS3050G VIH minimum of 2 V, so R70 loses its recessive margin.
- An LED + 2.2 k from +3V3 to TXD or RXD would not load the lines: no current flows in the recessive state, and RXD VOL is ≤ 0.4 V at 4 mA (`C528763.md`).
- But that LED lights only for dominant bits. At 0.7 mA a 250 kbit/s frame would not be visible without a pulse stretcher, which would be a new circuit.

So the LEDs would add parts with no visible function, and they were left out as the item allows. This used one facts lookup, the RXD VOH/VOL rows of C528763.

**T-0151 and T-0152: J6.**
- **Footprint:** J6 is one 1×8 2.54 mm footprint from the installed KiCad library. It is DNP and has `in_bom no` and no LCSC code, so no BOM line. Reference J6 is the next free J number after J5 on both boards.
- **Pin order:** GND on pin 1 and 3V3 on pin 8, at the far end from GND, as the brief asked.
- **pdm-hc2:** IO35–IO37 are FLT_T1, FLT_I2 and FLT_T2, so pins 4–6 carry no-connect flags and the FLT nets are untouched (auto decision).
- **Not added:** IO33 (HC2), IO34 (4-ch), IO45 and IO46.
- **Layout and invariants:** the layout rule is in both STATUS files. J6 goes inside the board near the EMC test points of `docs/reviews/2026-11-03-emc-system.md` item 9, not at an edge connector, for on-board use only (invariants 4 and 9).

**Docs.**
- ARCHITECTURE: pin map rows IO13, IO34, IO35–IO37, IO42 and TXD0/RXD0, plus the controller power budget row. The Invariants section is untouched.
- design-notes: new §8.7.
- Both STATUS files: new entry, layout rule, pre-order CPL check of D7/D8, and "For /system" updated. The 4-ch T-0150/T-0151 line now says done, and both boards' firmware lists now cover IO13.

## Checks

- ERC: pdm-4ch 0 errors and 5 warnings, pdm-hc2 0 errors and 5 warnings. Both are the same 5 `lib_symbol_mismatch` warnings (Q3–Q6, J3) as before, so no new error or warning. I normalised the copied symbols `Device:LED` and `Connector_Generic:Conn_01x08` to this file's format.
- Netlist: compared with HEAD by `tools/fix_check.py`. The only differences are U1 pins 17, 38–40 (and 31–33 on pdm-4ch) and the new D7, D8, R59, R67 and J6.
- `python3 tools/fix_check.py --scope shared --refs U1,D7,D8,R59,R67,J6`: **OK**.
  - pdm-4ch: 223 parts (+5), 16 DNP (+1), 60 unique (+1).
  - pdm-hc2: 237 parts (+5), 12 DNP (+1), 65 unique (+1).
- Heat budget: no output rating or switch part changed, and the LEDs add about 5 mW against `p_other` 2.3 W. I ran `tools/heat_budget.py` with defaults for reference: 4 × 4 A Tj 101 °C, unchanged.
- Footprint polarity: the KiCad `LED_0603_1608Metric` (pad 1 = K) and the JLCPCB `LED-SMD_L1.6-W0.8-R-RD` of C2286 (pad 1 = A) put the cathode on the same side. Only the pad numbers differ, so the CPL rotation goes on the pre-order list.
- Safety: no safety-relevant output changed. A probe that shorts J6 pin 8 to GND resets the ESP32, and the panel switches keep working (invariant 1).
- One process note: a compound command that would have restored the two schematics with `git checkout` was refused by the permission system. I didn't retry it; I fixed the edited sheets in place with a second script (`fixup_t0160.py`) instead.

## Open points

- KiCad tidy of the new group on the root sheet: D7, D8, R59, R67 and J6 sit below the "Decision 18" note at x ≈ 130–165 mm, y ≈ 169–188 mm.
- Which state the CA-IS3050G RXD takes with VCC2 down is unverified. It doesn't matter now, since the CAN LEDs were left out.
- ARCHITECTURE's root (controller) row does not name D7/R59, D8/R67 or J6. That row is left for the sync sweep and is listed under "For /system".

## Suggestions (not done)

- Bring IO33 (HC2) or IO34 (4-ch) out too, if a later revision wants one more spare line on J6. They would need a 9th pad or must replace one of the existing pins.
- CAN activity LEDs only with a pulse stretcher, for example a firmware-driven LED on a spare GPIO, if bus activity is ever wanted.
- Firmware: give unused J6 inputs a pull so short pad traces don't float. This is in the "For /system" firmware lines.
