# Fix report T-0066 (shared): custom footprints in lib/ (TDSO-14 paste, LMR51610 → SOT-23-6)

Date: 2026-11-02 · unattended run (decision 23) · items T-0044 (Q-0036), T-0045 (Q-0037)

## T-0044: PG-TDSO-14 paste between the thermal vias (done)

**Ruling Q-0036:** "move the paste windows between the thermal vias (no plugged-via surcharge); recheck the footprint
against the package drawing".

**Change:** in `lib/ESP32-PDM.pretty/Infineon_PG-TDSO-14_ThermalVias.kicad_mod`, the six 1.0 × 1.6 mm paste apertures
centred on the via columns (x = ±0.6, y = 0/±2) are replaced:

- 4 × 2.55 × 0.6 mm at y = ±0.6, ±1.8 (between the via rows)
- 2 × 2.55 × 0.5 mm at y = ±2.95 (beyond the outer rows, ending at the pad edge)
- all roundrect, corner ratio 0.1

Paste area is 8.67 mm² of 16.96 mm², **51 %** (was 57 % and sat over the vias). Every window edge is ≥ 0.30 mm from every
via centre: 0.15 mm from the drill and 0.05 mm from the via pad. Webs are 0.6 mm. Copper, vias, lead pads and the outline
are unchanged. Only the description text was updated.

**Recheck against the drawing:** Infineon BTT6050-1ERA datasheet Rev 1.00, Fig. 53, PG-TDSO-14, page rendered and read:

- body 3.9 × 8.65; pitch 1.27; lead 0.4 ± 0.05; span 6 ± 0.2; foot 0.67 ± 0.25; exposed pad 2.65 × 6.4 ± 0.1
- the lead pads (1.6 × 0.6 at ±2.6, x = 1.8…3.4) cover the foot at all tolerances (1.98…3.1)
- the exposed pad is 1:1 with the package pad
- pin 1 and the pin order match

**The drawing and the pads agree, so there was no reason to stop.**

**Compared with the JLCPCB/EasyEDA footprint of C534868** (`PG-TDSO-14_L8.6-W3.9-P1.27-LS6.0-BL-EP`, decoded from the
EasyEDA API):

- lead pads 1.8 × 0.6 at ±3.0; the exposed pad and pin-1 corner are the same
- EasyEDA draws the package rotated 90°, so the CPL rotation needs a check at the first order

Both footprints cover the foot. Ours is kept.

The heat budget is not affected: no output rating or switch part changed, and the copper and vias are unchanged.

## T-0045: U3 LMR51610 → `Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-23-6` (done)

**Ruling Q-0037:** "use the standard KiCad Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-23-6 after checking it against TI DBV0006A and the
JLCPCB footprint".

**Check** (TI LMR51610 datasheet SLUSEY1B, Table 5-1, DBV0006A pp. 32–34; KiCad 8 stock library; EasyEDA footprint of
C22427220):

| Land | Pad | Centre x | Extent x | Pitch |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| TI DBV0006A example | 1.1 × 0.6 | ±1.30 | 0.75…1.85 | 0.95 |
| KiCad SOT-23-6 | 1.325 × 0.6 | ±1.1375 | 0.475…1.80 | 0.95 |
| JLCPCB SOT-23-6_L2.9-W1.6-P0.95-LS2.8-BL | 1.072 × 0.532 | ±1.149 | 0.61…1.69 | 0.95 |

- **Pin numbering:** identical in all three (1–3 on one side, 4–6 opposite, 6 across from 1). TI's pinout is 1 CB, 2 GND,
  3 FB, 4 EN, 5 VIN, 6 SW, and the pad numbers are unchanged from the old footprint.
- **Fit to the package:** DBV0006A puts the foot within 0.7…1.5 from the centre, and all three lands cover it.
- **Differences:** KiCad's toe ends 0.05 mm short of TI's. Its heel reaches 0.275 mm further in (IPC-7351 nominal, which TI note 6 permits).
  The JLCPCB pad lies entirely inside the KiCad pad.

I judged this a match, so I made the change.

**Change (both boards, identical):** U3's `Footprint` on the shared `power-usb` sheet of `pdm-4ch` and `pdm-hc2` (the instance
property and the cached `ESP32-PDM:PLMR51610XDBVR` lib symbol) is now `Package_TO_SOT_SMD:SOT-23-6`. The library symbol
default in `lib/ESP32-PDM.kicad_sym` pointed to the non-existent `ESP32-PDM-BASE:LMR51610` and now points to the same
footprint. Making the cache and the library agree also cleared U3's `lib_symbol_mismatch` warning. LCSC C22427220 was
already set. A repo-wide search found no other user of `ESP32-PDM:LMR51610`, so I deleted
`lib/ESP32-PDM.pretty/LMR51610.kicad_mod`. The two `power-usb.kicad_sch` files are still identical apart from the project name.

## Checks

- **ERC** (`kicad-cli sch erc --severity-all`): 0 errors on both boards. Warnings went from 6 to 5 on each board; the removed
  one is `lib_symbol_mismatch` U3 and nothing new appeared.
- **Netlist** (`kicad-cli sch export netlist --format kicadxml`, before/after diff): on both boards the only change is
  U3's footprint (component and libpart field). Nets are unchanged.
- **Footprint:** `kicad-cli fp export svg` loads and plots the TDSO-14 footprint. A scratch script checked every
  paste window against every via, and the EP bounds and coverage.
- **Heat budget:** not rerun; no output ratings or switch parts changed.

## Open points

- 51 % paste coverage is at the low end of the target range; the via grid sets the limit. If first-article or X-ray
  inspection shows a thin or voided VS joint, the options are to widen the windows to 2.65 mm (53 %) or to buy plugged vias. Plugged
  vias cost money, so that choice is the owner's.
- At the first order, check the JLCPCB CPL rotation for U11–U14 (TDSO-14) and U3 (SOT-23-6). EasyEDA draws both
  rotated 90° relative to KiCad.
