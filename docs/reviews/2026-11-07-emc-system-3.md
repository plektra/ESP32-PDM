# EMC review, scope system, 2026-11-07 (third pass, after decision 121)

Re-check limited to what changed since `2026-11-07-emc-system-2.md` (E20, E21): `git diff 026eb95 HEAD -- hardware lib README.md` shows one hardware

## Findings

None new.

**E21 is closed by decision 121.** The pdm-4ch has no battery plug now, so a battery plug can no longer go into an HC2 J11 socket. HC2 J10 (C6394053, 10.16 mm)
and HC2 J11 (C577772, 7.62 mm) are different sets and do not fit each other. The README sentence added for T-0179 about marking the plug is now
only partly relevant (the J11 marking on HC2 stays useful). Not a finding.

## Passes (checked, nothing new)

- **Battery input, 4-ch, protection path.** D30 SMCJ30CA and C30 are on net VBAT_IN, the terminal net. The connector part changed, the net did not
  (decision 121: 169 nets identical). Invariant 4 and the clamp path through Q30/D31 are as in the earlier reports.
- **Pole geometry.** Pads 3.6 mm at a 10.16 mm grid: the gap between the VBAT_IN and GND pole pads is about 6.5 mm edge to edge, enough for 24 V. Each pole has
  two pads on the same net (`C475108.md`), so the copper can take the 16 A and D30/C30 can sit on both.
- **Reverse battery.** The old mitigation "keyed J10 plug" (design-notes lines 692, 1252, 1383) no longer exists on pdm-4ch. Q30/U30 blocks a reversed battery up to 30 V
  (README says so); the README warning text matches. Those design-notes lines are stale for pdm-4ch (a documentation sync item, not a failure mechanism).

## Layout rules

All earlier rules stand. Additions for the screw terminal:

1. **D30 and C30 at the J10 pads.** Put D30 and C30 within a few mm of the VBAT_IN and GND pads and connect through the pour on both pads of each pole; the clamp loop J10 - D30 - GND pad is
   then as short as for the plug (rule 1 of 2026-10-30).
2. **Wire side.** The wire openings face away from the board interior (`C475108.md`, to be confirmed on a sample); keep the VS copper leaving the pads toward the board, with no
   other tracks or components under the cable bend and screw access.
3. **Silkscreen.** "BATTERY", `+` and `-` beside J10 (T-0177 rule 2, still valid).

## Installation guidance (for the README)

Only one addition: **tighten the J10 screws to the stated torque (1.2 N m max, M4) and re-check after the first week of vibration.** A loose 16 A terminal on a long, inductive battery
cable chatters, arcs and creates repeated fast transients and broadband noise that D30/C30 must then absorb; this is cable practice, not a design change. The rest of the Installation section stands.
