P2 runs after P1 because both edit the `power-usb` sheet. P3 and P4 have no dependency, and the orchestrator runs
them one at a time on pdm-4ch anyway. No item needs a new part where a part on the BOM fits. P2 and P3 only change
a value and an LCSC code. P1 brings in the two parts the owner named. Of all the packages, only P4 needs a new part.

**Points to watch in P4.** These are one-way stops in the brief:
- **Invariant 4.** Positive surges on a PROFET output go through the body diode to VS and then to D30 at J10.
  - A plain P-FET with its gate to GND keeps that path: when it is on, it conducts in both directions.
  - An ideal-diode controller blocks current from VS back to J10. That would need a TVS on VS and new invariant
    wording, which is for the owner.
- **The ARCHITECTURE budget "shared supply path ≤ 2 mΩ"** cannot be changed in a pdm-4ch package. If the blocker
  breaks it, it goes under "For /system".
- **The 4 A per channel rating.** If the blocker's heat lowers it, the package stops and asks.

1. **system, amends 73 (Q-0060):** series resistor and PTC part for F1/F3/F4, with F2 moving to the same PTC.
2. **pdm-4ch (Q-0054):** reverse blocking on VS this revision, the interim README limit, and J10 keyed.
3. **system, amends 31 (Q-0058, Q-0061, Q-0062):** the USB shield RC becomes 1 MΩ ‖ 1 nF/2 kV, and USB-C stays
   fitted on every run.
4. **system, amends 26 (Q-0063, Q-0064):** at least 1 µF/100 V at each VS tab, 2.2 µF C170101 on pdm-4ch.
5. **system (Q-0057, Q-0059, Q-0065):** Economic PCBA without the four through-hole connectors (the owner
   hand-solders them), and the owner checks the coupon at order time.

Q-0055 and Q-0056 restate decision 33 word for word, so they get no new row.
