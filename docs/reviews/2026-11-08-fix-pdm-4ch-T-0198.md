# pdm-4ch layout stage 2: power path and output channels (T-0198)

Date: 2026-11-09 (unattended, pcb-fixer). Board `hardware/pdm-4ch/ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`. All edits were made with
`tools/kpcb.py place`. No copper was drawn, and the schematic, `lib/`, outline and hole keep-outs are unchanged.
Coordinates are in mm from the top-left board corner.

## Placed

- **Battery path (rail side, behind the J10 clear zone x 20–35, y 33–56, which stays free):**
  - JP30 at 38.1/47 (rot 180): VBAT_IN pad toward J10, VBAT_FL pad toward Q30.
  - Q30 AGM601LL at 48.2/47 (rot 0): source/gate on the left, drain tab (VS) on the right.
  - D31 SMDJ40CA at 48.2/56.2, directly across Q30: VBAT_FL pad under the source pins, VS pad under the tab.
  - C31 100 µF at 60.9/47, VS pad toward the drain.
  - C32 at 42.5/35.9, at U30's VCAP end, away from the tab.
  - There is room for more than 2 cm² of VS copper at the drain: about x 44–60, y 34–43, plus the tab and the C31 pad.
- **Output channels in a 2×2 grid** (pitch about 19 mm across and 17–19 mm along, spread for heat), all switches at
  rot 180 with the OUT pins toward J11:
  - U11 32/9.2, U12 51/9.2, U14 32/25.8, U13 51/27.8.
  - OUT1/OUT4 run straight to J11. OUT2/OUT3 use the corridor between the rows (y ≈ 14–19) to J11 pins 2/3, so no
    two outputs cross.
  - Each channel has C_n04 2.2 µF and C_n03 100 nF at the VS tab end, and C_n02 and the DNP R_n06 beside the OUT pins.
  - On the logic side of each channel: R_n05/D_n01 (GND network), R_n01 (IN), R_n02 (DEN), C_n01, the RIS pair
    R_n03 ‖ R_n07, and R_n04 + D_n02 toward the logic half.
- **TH1** at 45.2/33.9, at the tab end of U13. U13 is the hottest switch: the inner rear position next to Q30.
- The logic half is still parked. FL30 has no footprint, so JP30's spot stands in for it.

Design-notes §8.10 has the layout reasoning and the failure-mode note. **A competent person reviews the board
before installation** (battery input, Q30 blocker, outputs that may feed a bilge pump or navigation lights).

## Finding

FINDING (MEDIUM): U30 (LM74500-Q1) uses the stock KiCad `Package_TO_SOT_SMD:TSOT-23-8` footprint. Its pads are 0.50 mm
wide at 0.65 mm pitch, which leaves 0.15 mm gaps.
- Placed on the board, this gives 6 DRC clearance errors (board rule 0.2 mm). JLCPCB's 2 oz spacing is also about
  0.2 mm.
- TI's DDF land (pre-order checklist) uses 0.45 mm pads, which gives a 0.2 mm gap.
- The fix is a footprint change: a custom `lib/` footprint from the TI DDF land pattern, checked against the JLCPCB
  footprint of C5219210, then assigned in the schematic. That is board-fixer work, so U30 stays parked.
- Reserved spot: **42.5/39.3, rot 180**. VCAP/SOURCE pins 4/5 face C32; GATE is about 4 mm from Q30's gate pad.
