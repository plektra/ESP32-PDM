## T-0257: done

**Problem.** The T-0253 note in design-notes §8.20 said the +3V3 path through the two 0.2 mm necks "feeds clamp diodes
and pull-ups only". STATUS said "branch currents there are < 0.1 A". Nobody traced which +3V3 loads lie behind the
necks. +3V3 also feeds L1.2, C3/C8/C10 and U5.1, and will feed U1.3 later.

**Measured on the current board** (`ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`, the T-0259 build; coordinates are board-relative, file
origin offset 100/50). Both necks are still there:

| Layer | Segments | Length | Squares (0.2 mm) |
|---|---|---|---|
| In2.Cu | (65.65, 59.85)–(65.65, 59.75)–(65.75, 59.65) | 0.24 mm | 1.2 |
| B.Cu | (45.85, 68.05)–(46.05, 68.05)–(46.15, 68.15) | 0.34 mm | 1.7 |

The rest of +3V3 is 0.3 mm or wider: B.Cu 136.5 mm, F.Cu 35.7 mm (+2.1 mm at 0.6 mm) and In2 21.8 mm.

**Bound.** Copper resistivity is 1.72 µΩ·cm. The stackup (design-notes §8.13: In1/In2 0.5 oz, F/B 2 oz) gives
In2 17.5 µm ≈ 0.98 mΩ/sq and B.Cu 70 µm ≈ 0.25 mΩ/sq.
- Neck resistance: 1.2 × 1.0 + 1.7 × 0.25 ≈ 1.6 mΩ. Worst case, with all 2.9 squares at 1 mΩ/sq and +30 % for
  hot copper: < 4 mΩ.
- Drop at 0.5 A (the ESP32 peak plus U5): < 2 mV, against the 50 mV limit in the brief. This is negligible
  against the 3.3 V tolerance, so it is not a finding for the owner.

**Changes.**
- `docs/design-notes.md` §8.20, T-0253 bullet "+3V3 width": removed the consumer claim and the "< 0.1 A". It now
  gives the measured necks, the sheet resistances and the bound. It also says that the loads behind the necks were not
  traced. The battery-input/reverse-battery/output-path sentence and the competent-person reminder are kept word for
  word.
- `hardware/pdm-4ch/STATUS.md`, T-0253 entry: "Branch currents there are < 0.1 A." was replaced with the bound and
  a pointer to §8.20. No later entry repeats the claim, and neither STATUS file has a "For /system" entry for this
  item.
- `docs/decisions.md` was not edited.
