### MINOR

**E20 – pdm-hc2, hc_channel 1/2: the turn-off edge of an HC2 output is not slewed; only the turn-on edge is (R116/R216 0 Ω, PD direct).**
- Parts: U11/U12 pin 14 (PD) → R116/R216 0 Ω (C17168) → GATE; GATE loaded by Q11/Q12 (2 × BSC030N08NS5, ≈ 9.5 nF
  together, design-notes §4a.3) and C114/C214 1 nF. Netlist: `R116 pin 1 → PD: U11.14`, `pin 2 → GATE: Q11.4, Q12.4, R104.2, R115.1`.
- Evidence: turn-on is slewed to about 0.5–0.77 V/µs (design-notes §4a.3, 40–57 µs for 30 V). Turn-off is "unchanged": PD sinks 4 A typ with
  R(PD) 0.69 Ω typ / 1.34 Ω max (`docs/part-facts/C17556513.md`, p. 8). The gate time constant is then about 1.34 Ω × 10.5 nF ≈ 14 ns, so the
  channel current stops within the order of 30–60 ns (estimate, not simulated; `UNVERIFIED`). The output follows: for a resistive
  or LED load the voltage falls with the current, for a motor or solenoid it falls to the freewheel Schottky D102 (SS510B) at the same speed.
  That is 28 V in about 50 ns, order 500 V/µs, roughly 700 times faster than the turn-on edge the owner paid for in T-0106. The switched
  current (up to 15 A per output) also leaves the battery-side loop in the same time.
- Mechanism for this product: every time a person opens a panel switch or the firmware turns a pump, wiper or work light off, the
  load cable, which runs metres beside other wiring and possibly beside the VHF antenna coax, carries a 28 V step with a fast edge. The
  result is a click in the VHF receiver and in nearby NMEA wiring at switch-off, a single event and not a periodic emission. That is why
  it is MINOR: it is not a continuous emission and the loads are on/off (no PWM). The 4-ch PROFET outputs turn off at the device's own
  controlled slew; the part facts files hold no figure for it (`UNVERIFIED`).
- Fix, cheapest: no change now. R116/R216 already exist as 0 Ω parts, so the experiment costs only a hand-swap of an 0402. Put "R116/R216 0 Ω → 10–22 Ω,
  look at the turn-off edge and the VHF click" on the pre-compliance scan list that decision 28 already has. The fast trip depends
  on the same PD path (tSC 4–5 µs, `C17556513.md` p. 10), so any value above 0 Ω needs the short-circuit SOA of
  design-notes §4a.3 recomputed (the gate then needs about 230 ns for 22 Ω) before it is kept. That is why no value is proposed for the schematic now.
- Door: two-way (scan list and notes only; no part and no decision changes now). A later change of R116/R216 would amend decision 25
  ("PD direct") and is the owner's.

**E21 – system (pdm-4ch J10 and pdm-hc2 J11), README: with decision 104 the battery plug and the HC2 output plug are interchangeable, and the README still says all connectors are keyed.**
- Parts: pdm-4ch J10 and pdm-hc2 J11 are both KF2EDGSRM-7.62-2P C577772, plug C577752 (netlist: 4ch `J10 pin 1 → VBAT_IN, pin 2 → GND`;
  HC2 `J11 pin 1 → OUT1, pin 2 → OUT2`, no ground pole). README line 65: "All connectors are keyed plugs". It is true within one board only
  (T-0158 and `pdm-4ch/STATUS.md` list it as a choice for the owner and say the consequence "has not been analysed").
- Analysis of the two mis-plugs, from the netlists:
  - **Battery plug into an HC2 J11 socket:** battery + lands on OUT1 and battery − on OUT2. Nothing is damaged while the outputs are off (the
    back-to-back MOSFETs block both ways; D103/D203 SMAJ33CA is above 30 V). When channel 2 turns on (panel switch or bus command), VS is connected
    to battery negative through Q21/Q22: a dead short, 71–91 A fast trip. Design-notes §4a.3 says that turn-on into a short from a hot channel is
    outside the calculated SOA (margin 0.69–0.89). Q21 can fail short and then only the external fuse clears the fault. That is a burn risk behind a
    plug that fits.
  - **HC2 load plug into the pdm-4ch J10 socket:** the board loses its supply and one load wire sits on GND, the other on the unfed VBAT_IN. Nothing is damaged
    and nothing is powered. It shows as a dead unit.
- Fix: text only. Add one README sentence (and a coloured sleeve or label on the battery plug) saying that the pdm-4ch battery plug and the pdm-hc2 output plug are the same
  7.62 mm set and fit each other, and what a swap does. No part, no cost. A physical fix (another set on one board) stays the owner's choice, already listed in STATUS.
- Door: two-way (documentation). It does not change decision 104.

## Passes (checked again, nothing new)

- **Battery input, 4-ch.** J10 → D30 SMCJ30CA and C30 2.2 µF/100 V on VBAT_IN → JP30 → Q30 → VS. Netlist agrees with §8.6: D31 across Q30
  (`D31.1 VBAT_FL`, `D31.2 VS`). Positive surge from a PROFET output goes OUT → body diode → VS → Q30 (U30 keeps it on both ways, `C5219210.md`)
  → D30. Invariant 4 holds. Negative transient: D31 plus D30 as in §8.6 (the owner's accepted residual risk, Q-0067; not repeated).
- **Panel connector J5.** SWIN1–4: D16–D19 SMAJ30CA at the pin, then 4.7 k/10 k/470 nF and the zener divider; LED_K1–4: D50–D53, 100 Ω, 1 nF/2 kV.
  SW_SUPPLY: C54 and D54 at J5.5, then D55 (DSK16, reverse 60 V against a 48.4 V clamp) → R56 0.2 Ω → F4 → VS. J5.10 is GND (drain end).
- **N2K J4.** CANH/CANL: D9 PESD2CAN at the connector, then FL1/R71/R72; J4.2 → F3 → R55 → D10 SMAJ18CA (29.2 V clamp against the 35 V of L78M05) → FB2.
  J4.1 = GND_CAN. The 8 mm barrier of the CA-IS3050G stays a layout rule.
- **ADC sense lines at a clamp.** HC2 OUT sense 220 k/22 k with 100 nF and VBAT_SENSE 220 k/22 k with 100 nF: 53 V at the clamp gives 4.8 V at the divider, but
  τ ≈ 2 ms against a ≈ 1 ms pulse, and the pin sees at most about 0.2 mA through the pin diode. Normal range: 30 V → 2.7 V.
- **Buck.** f_sw 0.935–1.265 MHz (design-notes §8.3). JP1 bypass, L2/C22/R73 DNP option and the "L2 only with C22/R73" rule stand (T-0161). The loop rules stand.
- **Outputs, 4-ch.** C102 10 nF at J11.1 is harmless (about 8 mA at 0.8 V/µs). D103–D403 stay DNP footprints.
- **Cable practice in README.** The Installation section (E17) is present and matches the list in this report.

## Layout rules

All rules of the earlier reports stand (2026-10-30 1–13, 2026-11-03 1–14, this morning's L2/JP1/C22/J6/J10 rules). Additions:

1. **HC2 gate path (E20).** R116/R216 close to U11/U12 pin 14 (already a rule), and the PD → GATE track short and wide; leave the pads so that a 0402 can be
   swapped by hand. Keep the loop OUT → D102 → GND (J10.2) short and wide; it carries the freewheel step.
2. **J10 (4-ch) and J11 (HC2) silkscreen.** Mark each connector with its function ("BATTERY" / "OUT1 OUT2"), and for J10 the polarity, close to the socket (E21).
3. **R56/F4/D55 chain at J5.** Pad-to-pad in a row from J5.5: C54/D54, D55, R56, F4. (Same as rule 8 of 2026-11-03; repeated because three parts changed since.)

## Installation guidance (for the README)

No change from the Installation section already in the README, with these additions:

- **Plugs.** On a boat with both boards, the pdm-4ch battery plug and the pdm-hc2 output plug are the same set and fit each other. Mark the battery plug (red sleeve) and check it before power-up.
- **Switching noise.** Where a VHF click is heard when a load switches off, move the load cable away from the antenna coax and fit a diode or TVS at the load; the HC2 turn-off edge is fast (E20).
