# Fix report – shared – T-0064 (2026-11-01)

Work package T-0064: battery input bulk/HF capacitors and output surge protection (invariant 4).
Items T-0007, T-0009, T-0013, T-0017. Board-fixer, unattended (decisions 23/24). Rulings: Q-0002, Q-0003, Q-0007
(decisions 26, 27). Calculations and sources: `docs/design-notes.md` §8.1 and §8.2.

## What changed and why

### T-0007 – battery input J10 (root sheet, identical on both boards)
- J10.1 and D30 (SMCJ30CA, unchanged part) moved from `VS` to a new terminal net `VBAT_IN`.
- **C30** 1 µF/100 V X7R 1206, Samsung CL31B105KCHNNNE, **C13832** (extended). It sits on VBAT_IN–GND at the terminals.
- **FL30**: ferrite/CMC position VBAT_IN–VS, **DNP**, per the ruling. No 40–50 A ferrite or CMC footprint exists in
  the libraries (the KiCad Ferrite_THT bead has a 0.8 mm hole), so FL30 has no footprint yet. The part and its
  footprint are decided at pre-compliance or the PCB phase. A snap-on ferrite on the battery cable is noted as the
  first, board-free option.
- **JP30**: `Device:NetTie_2` in parallel with FL30. It is a copper bridge with no component, so VS stays connected
  and the netlist is continuous (VBAT_IN → JP30 → VS). It carries the KiCad DNP flag because nothing is placed, but it
  conducts. The PCB phase must draw it as wide as the VS copper; the placeholder `NetTie-2_SMD_Pad2.0mm` is far too small.
- **C31** 100 µF/63 V Al electrolytic, Ymin VKMD1001J101MV, **C487384** (extended). It sits on VS–GND near J10.
  - Datasheet (Ymin VKM catalogue): 8 × 10 mm, 902 mA rms at 100 kHz/105 °C (451 mA at 120 Hz), tan δ 0.14,
    10 000 h at 105 °C, AEC-Q200.
  - ESR is not specified. From tan δ, ESR(120 Hz) ≤ 1.9 Ω; this is recorded.
  - Ripple is not a limit (no PWM on HC2; the cable carries the 4-ch PWM ripple).
- A PWR_FLAG on VS replaces the one that had been at J10 (that one is now on VBAT_IN).
- No basic or preferred part exists for the ≥ 63 V bulk capacitor or the 1–2.2 µF/100 V MLCC, so both are extended:
  2 new unique extended parts.
- Bulk cost check: C31 0.22 USD + loading fee ≈ 0.30 USD/board at 10 units ≈ 0.52 USD/board. That is at the
  ~0.5 USD limit, not clearly over it, so it is not a stop.
  - Cheaper fallback: C116430, 0.13 USD, but only 2000 h. It is not chosen because of the 40 °C engine room
    (≈ 18 years vs ≈ 3.7 years at an assumed 65 °C board).
- **4-ch, channel sheet ×4: C104/C204/C304/C404** 1 µF/100 V (C13832) at each BTT6050 VS tab.
- **HC2 amount (EMC finding E3): C106/C206** in `hc_channel` (HC2-only place), the same 100 µF/63 V on each channel's VS,
  so HC2 has 3 × 100 µF.
  - Reason: at the 80 A fast trip with a 5 µH loop (16 mJ), the ideal excursion is 33.9 V with 100 µF and 30.6 V
    with 300 µF.
  - The ESR is divided by three, and each channel gets bulk next to its switch.

### T-0009 – HC2 output TVS
- **D103/D203 SMAJ33CA, C19077549 (hongjiacheng, preferred, 0.028 USD)**, from OUT1/OUT2 to GND at J11 (`hc_channel`).
  The ruling's condition (basic/cheap) is met.
- Datasheet: VRWM 33 V, VBR 36.7–40.6 V, VC 53.3 V at 7.5 A (10/1000 µs), 400 W, IR 1 µA.
- 28.8 V float case (and ≈ 30 V maximum): below the 33 V stand-off.
- Clamp 53.3 V is below Q12/Q22 VDS 80 V and the SS510B's 100 V. These are the only parts on OUT (checked in the netlist).
- Return-path rule written into pdm-hc2 STATUS (PCB): TVS next to J11, GND straight to J10.2.
- The failure-mode note is in design-notes §8.2.

### T-0013 – 4-ch outputs
- **D103/D203/D303/D403 SMAJ30CA, C19077548 (preferred), DNP**, from each OUT to GND (channel sheet ×4).
- ARCHITECTURE invariant 4 reworded. Only the PROFET output (pdm-4ch J11) is described as protected through its body
  diode to VS and D30 (wide VS copper), its own VS − VDS(AZ) clamp and the DNP TVS footprint. Every other external
  line keeps the full wording, and HC2 outputs are named explicitly as having their own TVS. Nothing else is relaxed.
- The wide-VS-copper rule is in pdm-4ch STATUS (PCB).
- Added a note before anyone fits the DNP TVS. During inductive turn-off the PROFET drives OUT to VS − VDS(AZ)
  (VDS(AZ) 66–75 V, BTT6050 P_5.5.5), i.e. about −42…−51 V. The bidirectional SMAJ30CA (VBR 33.3 V) would conduct
  and take the demagnetisation energy. Check the energy, or fit SMAJ30A unidirectional instead.

### T-0017 – recorded, no change
- In design-notes §8.1, next to the new capacitors: SMCJ30CA VC 48.4 V at 31 A (hongjiacheng datasheet) vs BTT6050
  VS 48 V (P_4.1.1) and VS(LD) 65 V with RI 2 Ω, ISO 7637-1 (P_4.1.3). Short excursions are covered.
- The new VS-side parts are rated 63 V and 100 V.

## Failure-mode note (safety-relevant: HC2 bilge forced-run output)

FINDING (MAJOR, documented): D103/D203 sit outside the back-to-back MOSFETs, which exist to block back-feed from the
float switch.
- If a TVS fails short and the float switch runs the pump, the float circuit feeds the short through the PDM output wire.
- The brief's claim that "the float-switch circuit is unaffected" holds only if the PDM leg has its own fuse, between
  J11 and the junction with the float circuit, that opens before the float-circuit fuse (fuse selectivity).
- With the channel on, the TPS48110 breaker trips and reports the fault.
- An open TVS only removes the surge protection.

This is written into design-notes §8.2 and pdm-hc2 STATUS "For /system" as a README installation rule. It is not a
schematic change and does not change a decision. **A competent person must review the bilge output, its fusing and
the battery-input changes before installation.**
