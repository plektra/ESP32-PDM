Date: 2026-11-13 · Role: planner (unattended) · Items: 1 (T-0218, owner ruling Q-0072)

## Overview for the owner

**Where it stands.** T-0241 left the board unchanged: 74 unconnected, 0 DRC errors, parity 0, VS-In2 1 island,
1185.4 mm². What it did achieve:

- The router export now loads with 0 violations.
- The corridor rule and the generated channel bus are ready but not applied. They are in
  `hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/T-0241-next-build.patch`, which the 2026-11-13 validator found applies cleanly.

Two problems are left:

1. **SW_IN1 and SW_IN4 have one exit between them.** It is the via strip at x 45.2–46.2, which also carries USB.
2. **Freerouting still leaves short links open,** even on the clean DSN.

You ruled on Q-0072:

- Move the ch1/ch4 small-signal parts toward U1.
- Add no via in VS-B-front/rear and change no power copper.
- Drop Freerouting and route everything left with `maze_route.py` as fixed routes.

**Package P1 (pcb-fixer, pdm-4ch)** – one item, done in this order:

1. Apply the patch.
2. Move the ch1/ch4 signal parts out of the west B.Cu pocket toward U1. Only parts whose nets are not VS, OUT or the
   load path may move.
3. Take Freerouting out of the build.
4. Script all remaining connections in this order:
   - short local links first;
   - then IO2–IO5;
   - then the SW_IN lanes;
   - then the J3 VBUS/D3-A legs from §8.18.
   In2 runs stay ≥ 0.9 mm away from GND THT pads.
5. Land the board as the build output.
6. Run the pour, thermal-via and heat-budget checks.

The goal is 0 unconnected. If it is not reached, the report says exactly which nets are left and why.

- **Deferred:** none.
- **Decisions:** one new row (Q-0072). It amends decision 176 (the routing method; it also replaces the Freerouting
  step of decision 163). The placement freedom for the channel signal columns is already in decision 172 and is not
  repeated.

**Safety.** The ruling keeps the PROFETs, VS caps, OUT TVS diodes, every F.Cu/B.Cu power pour, the 16 A paths and the
surge path where they are. Only small-signal parts move. VS-In2 stays under the existing corridor rule (decision 178),
and the heat budget is rechecked. A competent person should review the battery path, the VS copper and the outputs
before installation.
