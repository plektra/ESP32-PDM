already use 11.7 mΩ, but only on scratch copies of the tool. The real tool still has 11.2 mΩ.

design change. The ≈ 15 A rating stands: 15 A gives 88 °C, against the 150 °C Tj max. The only text that quotes the
old tool output is the heat line in design-notes §4a (87 °C at 15 A, 137 °C at 20 A). It gets the rerun values so the
notes match the tool again.

the fix report.
