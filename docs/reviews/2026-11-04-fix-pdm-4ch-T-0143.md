- F1 → R54 reaches D1 (bidirectional, 30 V, does not conduct) and D2 (blocks, 60 V).
- F4 → R56 reaches D55 (blocks, 60 V).
- F3 is not on VS.

I added three checks beyond T-0137, which are worth a look:
- **U11–U14:** the facts file states the 28 V reverse rating with RGND 150 Ω, while the board has 22 Ω (R105). The GND pin current does not depend on that here, because D101–D401 are in series and block it. The body-diode load current at 4 V is about 1 A for a 3 Ω channel, under the 2.3 A of the rating's own case. These are my calculations; the rating does not cover them directly.
- **R60:** about 17 µA at −4 V, and τ ≈ 2 ms on VBAT_SENSE, so a 1 ms spike barely moves it.
- **(f), charged reversal:** about 40–70 mJ, under 38 A, computed from 109 µF nominal. This is inside the pulse rating.

**(f), "no time limit" is kept.** At the −0.48 V upper bound the source is leakage of a few µA, far below C31's own 63 µA forward leakage limit. This is my argument: C31's datasheet still says nothing about reverse voltage, and D31's IR when hot is not in its datasheet. Both are stated as unverified. "Up to 30 V" also still holds, because D31's VRWM of 40 V is above D30's VRWM of 30 V.
