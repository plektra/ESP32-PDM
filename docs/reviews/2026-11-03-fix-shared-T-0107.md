## T-0098: RIS 680 Ω → 2 × 1 kΩ in parallel (pdm-4ch, `channel` sheet × 4)

- R103/R203/R303/R403: 680 Ω C17975 → **1 kΩ 1206 C4410**. New **R107/R207/R307/R407** (same part, same symbol on the
  multi-instance channel sheet) are placed at (116.84, 97.79). Pin 1 is on U1x pin 6 (IS) and pin 2 is wired to the RIS
  GND point. Netlist: each new resistor is on `Net-(U1x-IS)` and GND, parallel to R1x3.
- Part facts (`docs/part-facts/C4410.md`): UNI-ROYAL 1206W4F1001T5E, 1 kΩ ±1 %, **0.25 W** up to 70 °C (derated to 0
  at 155 °C), 200 V working, basic. Decision 36 (RIS 1206 ≥ 0.25 W) is met by each part.
- Full-load IS at 500 Ω uses kILIS from Table 9 p. 30 (facts files C534868 and C534858, worst case = min kILIS):
  - BTT6050 at 4.5 A: 1.50 V typ, 1.63 V max.
  - BTT6010 at 8 A: 1.03 V typ, 1.13 V max.
  - BTT6010 at 9 A: 1.27 V max.

  All are below the 2.9 V calibrated ADC range and the 3.3 V clamp, so the "stop" condition does not apply.
  FINDING (MINOR, brief): the brief's "BTT6010 at 8 A ≈ 2.7 V" is wrong. The BTT6010's kILIS is 3900, not 1500. The
  real value is lower, so the conclusion only gets stronger.
- Fault case: 40 mA IIS(FAULT) gives 0.80 W in total, 0.40 W per part. That is still over the rating and still covered
  by the firmware DEN rule (design-notes §6.1, now with a 500 Ω column). Full scale is now reached at ≈ 5.8 mA. This is
  below the 6 mA IIS(FAULT) minimum, so DEN rule 2 still sees every fault.
- IO3 strap (channel-2 network): 10 k + 500 Ω ≈ 10.5 k to GND, still held low.
- Firmware scale constant **RIS = 500 Ω (≈ 0.33 V/A BTT6050)** is recorded in design-notes §6.1 and in the pdm-4ch
  STATUS "For /system".
- Failure mode (sense path used for bilge-jam detection) is added to design-notes §6.1:
  - One of the two parts open doubles the reading. That gives a false high or fault report, which is the safe direction.
  - Both parts open reads zero, as before.

  A competent person reviews this before installation.

## T-0099: C170101 at the 1 µF/100 V places

- Condition holds: decision 33 sets the first order at 5 + 5 boards.
- **C30** (root, controller part, identical on both boards) is now 2.2 µF/100 V X7R 1206, Samsung CL31B225KCHSNNE,
  C170101. Decision 26 allows 1–2.2 µF at J10. Facts are in `docs/part-facts/C170101.md`: 2.2 µF, 100 V, X7R, 1206
  3.2 × 1.6 mm, so the footprint is unchanged.
- **HC2 C102/C202** (CVS on VS_CTRL, auto decision 58) are now the same part.
  - TPS4811-Q1 (facts file C17556513, added to it today) gives RVS 100 Ω and CVS 0.1–10 µF in Table 9-3, p. 39. It
    states **no upper limit**, so the stop condition does not apply.
  - τ = 100 Ω × 2.2 µF = 0.22 ms (≈ 0.1–0.15 ms with DC bias). That is still far from the 60 V/µs pin limit and short
    against the EN/UVLO and CTMR times.
  - The RVS drop (≤ 70 mV) does not depend on CVS.
- Result: pdm-hc2 no longer needs C13832 (−1 unique, extended types 20 → 19). On pdm-4ch, C13832 stays at C104–C404,
  so C30 costs +0.07 USD per board there with no fee saved. This follows the brief: C30 is a shared sheet and must be
  identical on both boards.
- **C104–C404 not changed**: confirmed decision 26 names "1 µF/100 V at each BTT6050 VS tab", so this is a one-way door
  (blocked below).
- Both STATUS pre-order checklists now note that for runs of 9+ (pdm-4ch) / 15+ (pdm-hc2) boards, C13832 at these
  places is cheaper again.

## T-0096: C18 → C9196, blocked (one-way)

FINDING (MAJOR): confirmed decision 31 (owner: Q-0008/Q-0020/Q-0029) specifies the shield-to-GND RC as
"1 MΩ ‖ 4.7 nF". Auto decision 46 only picked the part for it. Changing C18 to 1 nF changes a value the owner set,
which is policy item 2. The cost report saw only decision 46 (auto). The planner treated the same kind of wording in
decision 26 (C104–C404) as one-way. C18 is unchanged (4.7 nF/1 kV C107198). design-notes §8.3 is unchanged and C9196
was not looked up.
