# pdm-4ch – layout stage 6a: short local links and ground vias (T-0207)

Date: 2026-11-10 · Role: pcb-fixer (unattended) · Board: `hardware/pdm-4ch/ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`

## What the stage did

I drew every track and via by script (`kpcb track`/`via`). Tracks are on F.Cu only: nothing is on In1.Cu, nothing
crosses the CAN barrier, and nothing is in the antenna strip or a hole keep-out. No autorouter was used. The
schematic, `lib/`, the outline and the hole keep-outs are unchanged.

- **Buck input** (D3-K): U3.4/5–C19.1–C1.2 (0.6 mm), C1–D3.1, C1–JP1.2, JP1.2–L2.2, L2.2–C22.1.
  - D2-K: JP1.1–D2.1–L2.1.
  - D2.2–R54.2, R54.2–D1.2 (0.8 mm), R54.1–F1.2 (1.2 mm).
- **Buck input loop:** a 0.3 mm GND track runs from U3.2 under the IC body to C19.2, with a 0.6 mm strap C19.2–C1.1.
  The C1/C19/U3 loop is on F.Cu with no via.
- **SW, CB and FB:**
  - SW: U3.6–C2.2–L1.1 (0.6 mm), and L1.1–R44.2.
  - R44.1–C20.2.
  - CB: C2.1–R43.1, R43.2–U3.1.
  - FB: U3.3–R9.2–R10.1.
- **U30/Q30/C32:** C32.1–U30.4 (VCAP), C32.2–U30.1/via–VBAT_FL pour, U30.5–VBAT_FL pour, U30.6–Q30.1 (gate).
- **Other links:** C4.1–F2.2, F2.2–FB1.1, R106.1–OUT1 pour, D8.2–R67, D7.2–R59, C22.2–R73.1, L1.2–C3.1 (+3V3).
- **GND vias.** Every GND/GND_CAN pad was checked: it must sit in an F.Cu pour island that has vias to In1. The
  check script is in the job folder. New vias:
  - U30.2 and R106.2: the new tracks closed their pockets.
  - Two beside C19/C1, outside the loop.
  - Nine under the ESP32 module. Its centre pad was 7–10 mm from the nearest via.

## Findings

- **MINOR – bootstrap placement.** R43/C2 sat above L1, so an F.Cu CB track would have had to cross SW. I moved C2
  and R43 into the corridor between L1 and U3 (C2 at 17.3/61.5 rot 90, R43 at 17.3/63.45 rot 270). The
  bootstrap loop now stays on F.Cu and is about 2 × 3 mm.
- **MINOR – LED resistors reversed.** In R67 and R59 the +3V3/IO13 pad sat between the LED anode and the
  resistor's anode pad, right under the antenna strip. I turned both by 180°, and the anode links are straight.
- **MAJOR (fixed) – buck GND pocket.** With FB going down to R9, U3.2 had no F.Cu path to C19/C1. The loop would
  have closed through vias. A 0.3 mm GND track now runs under the IC body, between pins 3 and 4 (0.32 mm
  clearance), and closes the loop on F.Cu.
- **INFO.** One clearance error during the work: the C1→JP1 track came within 0.19 mm of a GND via. I moved the
  track to y 60.85.
- **INFO.** One more clearance error: C4→F2 came too close to FB1.2. I rerouted it with a jog at y 7.72.

## Checks

- `kpcb drc`: **0 errors**, 222 unconnected (253 before), parity 0. No SMT part on the bottom, nothing parked,
  nothing in a hole keep-out.
- Pours after the final refill: VS-F-drain, VS-B-spine/front/rear, VS-In2, VBAT_IN-F/B, VBAT_FL-F/B, OUT1–4: **each
  1 island**. No starved thermal, and no GND zone-island unconnected item.
