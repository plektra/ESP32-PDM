## Overview for the owner

**One package, nothing deferred, one decision row.**

- **The problem:** the pdm-4ch routing build stalls at 78 unconnected items.
  - 27 west channel signals are walled in by the VS-In2, VS B.Cu and OUT2/OUT3-F outlines.
  - 47 items in the U1 strip and analog block (Q3–Q6, R17–R24/R50/R51) have no room.
- **Your ruling (Q-0070):** "Option 3 + option 2. Signal vias (not tracks) may pass through VS-In2; each pour must
  stay one island and keep >= 95 % of its area. Also allow a placement stage that gives the U1 transistor/resistor
  block (Q3-Q6, R17-R24/R50/R51) and the channel signal columns room. Recheck the heat budget (decision 9) after the
  change. No corridor notch. The scripts gate (question 4) is already settled by decision 163."
- **P1 – pdm-4ch, pcb-fixer: placement room plus vias through VS-In2, then rebuild to 0 unconnected.**
  - This is layout only: no schematic, `lib/` or outline change. So it runs as a pcb-fixer package (decision 130).
  - Placement and the change to the build script go in one package. The rebuild is the only way to show that the
    placement worked, so splitting them would leave a stage that cannot pass its own gate.
  - Order inside the package:
    1. Change the build: on In2 only, VS-In2 becomes a keep-out for tracks only, not for vias. The pour acceptance
       goes from ≥ 98 % to ≥ 95 % per pour, still one island each.
    2. Move the U1 transistor/resistor block and the channel signal columns.
    3. Rebuild.
    4. Recheck the heat budget.
  - No corridor notch, and no change to any power zone outline.
  - The scripts gate is already open (c9fa4fa, decision 163, confirmed by T-0221). Nothing left to plan there.
- **Deferred:** none.
- **Decisions:** one row from Q-0070. It amends decision 166 (pour acceptance 98 % → 95 %) and records the
  via-only permission and the placement stage.
- **Safety:** VS-In2 carries part of the battery-side current and the surge path. Signal vias take some of its area.
  The 95 % floor, the one-island rule and the heat-budget recheck bound that loss. A competent person reviews the
  battery path, the VS copper and the outputs before installation.
