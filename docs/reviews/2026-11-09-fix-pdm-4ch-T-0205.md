# pdm-4ch – layout stage 5: 4-layer planes and stitching vias (T-0205)

Date: 2026-11-09 · Role: pcb-fixer (unattended) · Board: `hardware/pdm-4ch/ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`

## What the stage drew

No part was placed or moved. The outer zones, tracks and vias of stage 4 are unchanged. The schematic, `lib/`, the
outline and the hole keep-outs are untouched.

**Inner zones (5).**

| Layer | Zone | Net | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| In1.Cu | GND-In1 | GND | Solid plane in one piece (5583 mm²) under the whole GND side. It has the outer GND outline: antenna notch, barrier cut at x 67.55, Ø 8.8 mm arcs around H1–H3. |
| In1.Cu | GND_CAN-In1 | GND_CAN | Same as the outer island (x 75.65–98.95, y 51.35–73.95, H4 corner cut). |
| In2.Cu | VS-In2 | VS | 12.2 cm² under U11–U14, Q30's drain, C31 and D31's VS side. Pads solid, 0.5 mm clearance. |
| In2.Cu | GND-In2 | GND | The rest of the GND side. |
| In2.Cu | GND_CAN-In2 | GND_CAN | Under the CAN island. |

**Barrier (PCB rule 9):** GND to GND_CAN is 8.1 mm across U5 on all four layers and 4.0 mm above J4 (as on the outer
layers). There is no copper on any layer under U5 between pins 1–4 and 5–8.

**Thermal vias into VS:**

- The 10 footprint thermal vias under each PROFET tab are all inside VS-In2, 40 of 40.
- All 38 VS vias at Q30's drain and D31 are inside VS-In2. That took an extension of the zone down over D31, see the
  findings.
- Q30's drain pad has no via in the pad, which would need plugged vias.

**Stitching vias, 90 new (0.8/0.4 mm, tented).** Every site is inside the GND or GND_CAN fill on F.Cu, B.Cu and In1,
≥ 0.75 mm from every pad, and clear of the holes (≥ 4.9 mm from their centres), the antenna strip and U1's body.

| Group | Vias | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Board edges | 34 | GND, about every 5 mm |
| CAN island edge | 5 | GND_CAN |
| Buck | 12 | Ring around U3/L1/C1/C19/C3/C20/R44, about every 4 mm |
| ESP32 | 8 | Around U1's pads |
| Open GND areas | 30 | Grid of ≥ 8 mm |
| Island tie | 1 | Top GND island below D31 |

After the refill, no GND or GND_CAN item is unconnected: every outer pour island is tied to In1.

**Load current:** the 16 A paths stay on the 2 oz outer copper. The only inner copper on a power net is VS-In2, which
runs in parallel and takes about 11 % (under 2 A, spread over ≥ 10 mm). OUT, VBAT_IN and VBAT_FL have no inner copper.

The plane plan and the safety note are in `docs/design-notes.md` §8.13, and the dated entry in
`hardware/pdm-4ch/STATUS.md`.

## Findings during the stage

- MINOR: the hole keep-out rule areas (decision 123) and U1's antenna keep-out cover only F.Cu/B.Cu. The inner zones
  therefore leave both areas out in their own outlines. Adding In1/In2 to the rule areas is a keep-out change, for the
  owner at a later revision. It is not needed now.
- MINOR, fixed: J6 pin 1 lost a thermal spoke on In2 next to the first VS-In2 outline. The VS lobe now ends at y 50.5.
- MINOR, fixed: 9 VS vias under D31 were outside VS-In2. The zone now reaches down to y 58.7 over x 46.5–53.3.

## Checks

- `kpcb drc`: 0 errors, 404 warnings (silkscreen, U1 library mismatch, FL30 without footprint), 253 unconnected.
  - None of the unconnected items is GND, GND_CAN or a zone.
  - One is VS: R60 pad 1, the sense resistor, left for routing.
  - The rest are signal nets.
  - Before the stage: 256.
- Schematic parity: 0. No SMT part on the bottom, nothing in a hole keep-out, nothing parked.
- `python3 tools/fix_check.py --scope pdm-4ch`: **OK – passes the merge gates.**

## Open points for the next stage (T-0206, routing on 4 layers)

- Route the signals: U30/C32 (VCAP, GATE, SOURCE), R60 (VS sense), R106, all logic nets, the C104–C404 returns.
- Use In2 first, outside VS-In2, then the outer layers. Keep In1 unbroken under the ESP32, the buck and the PROFET
  IN/IS/DEN networks (EMC rule 3).
- A track on In2 displaces the fill. Refill afterwards and check that VS-In2 stays one piece around the switch tabs.
- No track or via crosses the barrier band x 67.55–75.65 (or y 47.35–51.35 above J4) on any layer, except C70's
  pads.
- The antenna notch and the hole arcs stay free of copper on the inner layers too. `kpcb` has no inner keep-out, so
  watch track ends near the corners and in y < 5 at x > 45.85.

A competent person reviews the board, in particular the battery path and the outputs, before installation.
