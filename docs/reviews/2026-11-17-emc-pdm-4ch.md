# EMC review, scope pdm-4ch, layout (2026-11-17)

First EMC check of the finished pdm-4ch PCB (decision 219: fully routed, 0 DRC errors, 0 unconnected, parity 0).
The schematics have not changed since the last EMC pass (`git diff c08d513 -- hardware/*/*.kicad_sch lib` is empty;
only the `.kicad_pcb` and `scripts/` changed), so the findings E1–E21 of the earlier reports are not repeated. This
report checks the copper against their layout rules and the "points to check first" in `STATUS.md` (decisions 217, 218,
219).

Sources: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (invariant 4, "PCB layout rules"), `docs/decisions.md` (27, 28, 123, 168,
216–219), `docs/design-notes.md` §8.2, §8.5, `docs/reviews/2026-10-30` … `2026-11-11-emc-*`, README "Installation",
part facts `C528763` (CA-IS3050G), and the BTT6050-1ERA datasheet (LCSC copy of C534868, read for slew and ESD).
Method: the board was loaded with KiCad's Python (`pcbnew`, read only, scratch folder); footprints, pads, tracks, vias and
the filled polygons of every zone were dumped, and distances and net paths were computed from that dump (pour outlines
sampled every 0.25 mm, so distances are good to about 0.1 mm). The netlist came from `kicad-cli sch export netlist`.
Nothing in the repository was changed.

## Findings

### CRITICAL
None.

### MAJOR
None.

### MINOR

**E22 – pdm-4ch, analog_sw LED1 line: C50 (1 nF, the RF/ESD shunt of the LED_K1 sink) sits at the end of a 21–25 mm stub, not at R19.**
- Parts: R19 (100 Ω) at (75.3, 28.0), Q3 (MMBT5551) at (69.2, 22.5), C50 at (78.2, 7.9). Netlist: `Net-(Q3-C)`: R19.2, Q3.3, C50.1.
- Evidence: the routed copper of `Net-(Q3-C)` is 7.4 mm on F.Cu and 20.9 mm on In2. Shortest copper path C50 → R19.2 is
  25.5 mm and C50 → Q3 collector 21.0 mm (graph over the tracks of the net; straight-line distance 19.8 mm). The same filter on
  the other three lines is where the rules put it: C51 (LED2) beside R22, C52 (LED3) 1.5 mm from R25, C53 (LED4) 1.8 mm from R28.
- Mechanism: the filter D50 → R19 → C50 only works if C50 is close behind R19. At 160 MHz a narrow In2 track that is 1 mm above
  the In1 plane (core 0.98 mm in the stackup) is about 1 nH/mm (estimate, `UNVERIFIED`), so 21 mm is ≈ 21 nH ≈ 21 Ω. The shunt is
  then ≈ 20 Ω instead of ≈ 1 Ω: attenuation of the R19/C50 stage at 160 MHz ≈ −14 dB instead of ≈ −40 dB. The panel LED cable is a
  metres-long antenna for a 25 W VHF transmitter; Q3's collector–base junction rectifies what gets through, so the LED1 current
  shifts (brightness flicker during a transmission). Nothing else on the line is at risk (SMAJ30CA D50 is at the connector, the LED
  is only an indicator), hence MINOR.
- Fix, cheapest: no part change. In the next layout session put C50 beside R19.2 (within about 5 mm, ground via next to its pad)
  and let the router run Q3 from there; the track after the filter may be long, as on LED2–LED4. Door: two-way (layout of an
  unordered board).

**E23 – pdm-4ch, CAN sheet: D9 (PESD2CAN) is a 6 mm side branch on CANH_BUS, while CANL_BUS runs through its pad.**
- Parts: J4.3 (93.3, 56.28), D9 pin 1 (87.96, 53.45), R71 pad 1 (85.2, 57.11), FL1 pad 1 (79.77, 55.38).
- Evidence (hand routes of decision 168): CANH_BUS leaves J4.3 on B.Cu; at x ≈ 88.8–89.3 it splits into two branches 0.4 mm
  apart: one B.Cu → via (85.1, 58.05) → R71, the other B.Cu diagonal 4.0 mm → via (86.0, 53.45) → F.Cu spur 2.0 mm east to D9
  pin 1 (and west to FL1). The way to D9 is therefore ≈ 6 mm and one via, and R71 (→ U5) is tapped at the same point. On
  CANL_BUS the track from J4.4 enters D9 pin 2 and leaves it toward R72/FL1: flow-through, as rule 1 of 2026-10-30 asks.
- Mechanism: for an ESD or fast-transient hit on CANH, the clamp is reached over ≈ 7 nH (6 mm of 0.2 mm track plus a via,
  ≈ 1 nH/mm, `UNVERIFIED`), while the transceiver branch hangs on the unclamped node. A first-peak rate of 30 A/ns (IEC 61000-4-2,
  8 kV) would put a few hundred volts of overshoot over 1–2 ns on the branch to U5 pin 7; the CA-IS3050G bus pins are rated
  ±8 kV HBM (`C528763.md`, p. 7) and ±58 V DC (§7.1). The on-chip structure will probably take it; the point is that D9 protects
  CANL fully and CANH less well. N2K connectors are plugged in on installation only, hence MINOR.
- Fix, cheapest: no part change. Re-route the CANH_BUS hand route so that it passes D9 pad 1 and goes on from there to R71/FL1,
  like CANL. Door: two-way (layout; the pair is a hand route of decisions 168/169 and the change stays inside them).

**E24 – pdm-4ch, buck: the FB divider (R10 pad 1) is 0.7 mm from the SW-node pad of the DNP snubber resistor R44, and the SW spur to the snubber footprints is ≈ 6 mm long.**
- Parts: R10 pad 1 (FB node) (20.79, 67.7), R44 pad 2 (SW node) (19.26, 66.8). Track `Net-(U3-SW)`: pin 6 → L1.1, plus a dead-end
  spur (16.0, 64.85) → (18.2, 64.85) → (19.2, 65.95) → (19.2, 66.74) to the R44/C20 footprints (both DNP, design-notes §8.3).
  The FB track runs at x 20.8–21.7, 1.6 mm from that spur for about 3 mm.
- Mechanism: the SW node swings 24–30 V in a few ns. A coupling capacitance of about 0.05 pF (estimate from the 0.7–1.6 mm gaps,
  `UNVERIFIED`) onto a node of 16.6 kΩ (R9 ∥ R10) with a few pF stray gives a few tenths of a volt of spike at each edge on a
  0.8 V reference; the regulator may show jitter or pulse bunching at light load, which turns the 1.1 MHz line into subharmonic
  content on the battery input (T-0161 scan) and ripple on +3V3. With R44/C20 unfitted the spur is also an open SW stub of ≈ 6 mm.
  Rule 5 of 2026-10-30 says R9/R10 "away from SW and L1".
- Fix, cheapest: no change now. The T-0161 scan list gets one line: "scope U3 FB and +3V3 at light load for jitter". Only if it
  shows, move R9/R10 about 3 mm away from the R44 pad (or the R44/C20 footprints to the other end of L1). Door: two-way.

## Passes (measured on the board)

- **Battery input.** J10 pads (17.2, 39.5) / (17.2, 49.66). D30 SMCJ30CA sits on the VBAT_IN pour 6 mm from J10.1, C30 right
  behind it, JP30 bridges to the VBAT_FL pour, Q30 source and drain pads are wide copper, D31 (SMDJ40CA) is 3.4 mm below the Q30
  source pad with its VS pad on the VS drain pour: the loop J10 – D30 – C30 – Q30 – VS/C31 is compact and D31 is in the VS loop,
  not on a stub (rules 2, 10, 11 of 2026-11-03/04 hold). U30's gate track is 3.6 mm of 0.25 mm copper, far from the buck SW node
  and the antenna.
- **D30's return.** D30 pad 1 goes through one 0.3 mm via into the In1 plane (single island of 5420 mm², no cut except the CAN
  barrier) and by the plated through-hole pads to J10.2, 6 mm away. 31 A for 1 ms in a 0.3 mm via is far from fusing (Onderdonk
  ≈ 215 A for 1 ms, estimate). Not a finding.
- **J5.** Order connector → TVS → R → C holds on every line: the track from each J5 pin runs through its TVS pad (SWIN1–4,
  LED_K1–4, SW_SUPPLY). D16, D18, D51, D53, D54 are 3.7–3.8 mm from the pin; D17, D19, D50, D52 are 10–12.6 mm away along
  cable-side copper on the source side of the clamp, so the clamp still sees the pulse first. Each TVS has a GND via within
  0.8–2.0 mm. R34–R37 and C14–C17 are 2.3 mm apart. The 1206 pad capacitance of R34 (≈ 0.2 pF) limits that stage at 160 MHz to
  about −60 dB, far more than the input needs (thresholds, margin and debounce are those of design-notes §3, unchanged).
  R3 and R45 (the SWIN1 2 kΩ load) sit 35 mm from J5, so the raw SWIN1 net runs through the logic area, 0.2–0.3 mm beside
  TXD0/RXD0/IO35–37 at J6: a switch-contact edge (≈ 24 V) couples a few volts for nanoseconds onto a console pin, clipped by the
  pin diodes, and no function reads those pins. Observation, not a finding.
- **J4.** D9 5.3 mm from the pins (CANL flow-through, see E23 for CANH); F3, R55, D10 on the GND_CAN side in the order
  J4.2 → F3 → R55 → D10 (a short U-turn at R55 pad 2, harmless). D10's GND_CAN via is 2.0 mm away.
- **J3 (USB).** D5/D6 4.9–7.6 mm from the pads along the pair; C18/R13 3.3–3.6 mm from the shield pads.
- **J11 / outputs.** C102–C402 (10 nF) sit at the PROFET OUT pins, where the RF has to be stopped; D103–D403 are DNP footprints
  3.8 mm from the J11 pads with 1.4–2.2 mm GND vias. The OUT pours are 8 mm wide for heat. Output edges are slow: BTT6050-1ERA
  dV/dt(on) and dV/dt(off) 0.3 / 0.8 / 1.4 V/µs (min / typ / max, RL = 12 Ω, P_5.5.12, PDF page 15), so 24 V takes about
  17–80 µs; a radiated step that slow is a click at most, no VHF content (this resolves the "slew UNVERIFIED" of 2026-11-07 for the
  4-ch). Load returns stay outside the board, as invariant 4 and the README assume.
- **Buck loop.** C19 (100 nF) is 2.8 mm from U3 VIN and GND pins, C1 5 mm; the loop is closed on F.Cu with no via (the GND vias at
  (23.0, 64.4) and (25.0, 64.85) are outside it); SW is a 0.6 mm track of 3.4 mm to L1 with no copper area; CB runs apart from SW;
  L1 is 12 mm from J10. Analog nets (IS, NTC, VBAT_SENSE) are ≥ 4.9 mm from the SW and Q30 gate nets.
- **CAN barrier (decision 217).** Across the U5 body the logic copper (GND, x ≤ 67.4) and GND_CAN (x ≥ 75.6) are 8.2 mm apart on all
  four layers, y 53.1–66.6; nothing but C70 (pad gap 1.8 mm, DNP) crosses; 4.0–4.3 mm elsewhere (J4 to J5 pins 4.2 mm, island edge to
  GND 4.0 mm). The stepped outline leaves 4.3–5.6 mm diagonally at the two corners of the band, as the rule's rectangle implies.
  No logic net enters the island; all island nets belong to J4, D9, FL1, F3/R55/D10/FB2/D11/U6, C11–C13 and the U5 pins 5–8.
- **Ground plane.** In1 GND: one island, 5420 mm². The only voids are the barrier notch, via anti-pads and the PROFET thermal-via
  fields (rule 5). Stackup: F.Cu 2 oz / 0.21 mm / In1 GND / 0.98 mm core / In2 / 0.21 mm / B.Cu 2 oz. Signals on B.Cu reference In2
  (VS or fragmented GND) at 0.21 mm, not In1; for 3.3 V edges at a few ns this works because of the B.Cu GND fill and the VS
  decoupling, and decisions 150/165 state it. Noted for the scan, not a finding.
- **Antenna.** No track, via, pad or zone in x 45.85–92.5, y 0–4.5; D7/D8/R59/R67 at y 5.3 are outside it.
- **Creepage (rule 4, 1.0 mm).** VS/VBAT_IN/VBAT_FL tracks and pours keep ≥ 1.0 mm from other nets on F.Cu and B.Cu except near
  pads. The PROFET and U30 pad pitch (0.2–0.4 mm) is the package's own. One deviation: the VBAT_FL track at x = 40.25 (U30 pin 5 to
  the pour) is 0.3 mm from the GND fill over about 4 mm, beyond the 0.6 mm pad zone of rule 4; at 24–30 V on a coated board
  this has no failure mechanism beyond salt creep that the coating guidance already covers. J10 pad 1 to B.Cu GND fill 0.75 mm.
  Mounting holes: no net, nearest copper 4.9–9.6 mm from centre.
- **SW_SUPPLY chain.** J5.5 → D54 → C54 → D55 → R56 → F4 → VS in a row, series parts in line.

## Layout rules

All rules of 2026-10-30 (1–13), 2026-11-03, 2026-11-04 and 2026-11-07 stand; `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "PCB layout rules" holds
the list the build enforces. Additions from this pass (for `/system` or the layout session to add there):

1. **Filter capacitors behind the series resistor.** A shunt capacitor of an RC/RF filter goes within 5 mm of the resistor it
   works with, ground via beside its pad, on the cable-side node; the track onward to the protected part may be long (E22).
2. **TVS flow-through, also on differential lines.** Every line passes through its TVS pad: nothing taps the line upstream of the
   clamp (E23).
3. **Dead-end SW copper.** DNP snubber or option footprints on the buck SW node are kept short (≤ 3 mm) and ≥ 3 mm from the FB
   node (E24).
4. **For the next reroute:** re-check E22/E23 (the router knows nets, not the order along a filtered line); see lever E25.
5. **Silkscreen (stage 6, still open):** mark J10 "BATTERY" with + and − (E21 of 2026-11-07), J11 "OUT1–OUT4", J5 "PANEL" with
   pin 1 and the drain pin J5.10, J4 "CAN".

## Installation guidance

The README "Installation" section matches the board and needs no change for pdm-4ch. Points confirmed or sharpened by this layout:

- **Panel cable.** Screened multicore, drain at the PDM end only, on J5.10 (the one GND pin of J5). The board filters the switch
  edges; the cable screen is what keeps VHF off the whole bundle.
- **Outputs.** The pdm-4ch output edges are slow (0.3–1.4 V/µs, datasheet), so no VHF click is to be expected at switch-off (that
  was the pdm-hc2 case, E20). The D103–D403 footprints stay empty; a freewheel diode or TVS at the load for inductive loads stays the
  rule (design-notes §8.2).
- **NMEA 2000.** As in the README. The node's GND_CAN island is isolated by design; do not tie J4 pin 1 to the boat negative at the node.
- **Battery.** As in the README; J10 is a fixed screw terminal with wire entry at the board edge, so keep the pair twisted right up to it.

- The BTT6050 datasheet used is the LCSC copy; `C534868.md` records that its diagnostic section carries a "Target Data Sheet Rev 0.11"
  header. Slew and ESD values were read from the tables in the same PDF and not cross-checked against the Infineon site.
