## Safety note

Plugging the battery plug into a pdm-hc2 J11 is a burn risk (dead short through Q21/Q22, outside the calculated SOA from
hot, design-notes §4a.3). The only guard now is the marking and the check before power-up. **A competent person should check
the plug marking before installation on a boat that carries both boards** (CLAUDE.md item 7).
