# pcb-fixer pdm-4ch T-0253 – maze copper clean-up and +3V3 class width (T-0250, T-0251)

Date: 2026-11-13 · unattended · layout scripts only. No part moved, no schematic, lib, outline or rule change.

## Start state (confirmed)

`kpcb drc`: 0 errors, 427 warnings, 52 unconnected, parity 0. VS-In2 was 1 island, 1165.5 mm², 40/40 thermal vias.
kpcb listed 15 dangling items. A full `kicad-cli` JSON DRC showed 21, because kpcb truncates the list:
- 16 vias connected on only one layer;
- 5 stubs.

Causes:
- **maze_route.py raster edge.** Goal and start cells were taken from the rasterised copper, and each leg emitted
  the cell corner. A leg could therefore stop up to 0.1 mm outside its pad: U14-IS→R403.1, Q6-B→Q6.1,
  R3-Pad2→R3.2 and R4-Pad2→R4.2. Both J6.8 legs ended about 1 mm from the centre of the round pad.
- **Dead U1 fan-out copper.** 15 U1 fan-out vias (pad → F.Cu stub → via, nothing on any other layer) and one
  SW2_IO stub at R31 are left over from the dropped Freerouting copper.
- FINDING (MINOR): `prep` skipped a fixed track when the same geometry was already on the board, without comparing
  width. Widening a track in `fixed_routes.py` therefore never reached the board.

## What was done

**Step 1 – T-0251, class width**
- `geom_export.py` writes each net's class width (`geo['width']`).
- `maze_route.py` emits `w=` for nets whose class width is not 0.2 mm. It grows its track keep-outs by the extra
  half-width and draws earlier foreign legs at their real width. Smoke test on the start board: +3V3 legs come out
  at `w=0.30`.
- `fixed_routes.py`: the 17 maze-made +3V3 lines are now `w=0.3`.
- `routing_build.add_fixed` widens an identical locked track to the width in `fixed_routes.py` and never narrows
  one. 67 segments were widened.
- In the first build, 2 clearance errors against GND vias (0.175 and 0.191 mm). I measured each segment, and only
  the two failing necks stay at 0.2 mm:
  - In2 (65.70, 59.90)–(65.70, 59.80)–(65.80, 59.70): 0.24 mm long, GND via at (65.04, 59.50);
  - B.Cu (45.90, 68.10)–(46.10, 68.10)–(46.20, 68.20): 0.34 mm long, GND via at (46.37, 67.52).

  These legs were **not** re-routed with maze_route.py at 0.3 mm. That would have moved about 30 mm of copper to
  clear 0.6 mm of neck. Branch current is < 0.1 A (brief step 3).
- Locked-width histogram: 232×0.4, 55×0.6, 12×0.5, 10×0.8, 8×0.25 and 1×1.2 are unchanged (plus 1×0.15 that was
  already there). 0.3 mm went from 51 to 120 segments: 67 widened plus 2 new J6.8 links, all +3V3.
- +3V3 at 0.2 mm went from 72 segments / 161.4 mm to 4 segments / 0.6 mm (the two necks).
- +3V3 feeds:
  - U5.1 and L1.2 are as before, unchanged.
  - **U1.3 (ESP32 3V3 pin) has no connection at all yet.** It is one of the open items for T-0218 and is not
    changed here. Its dead fan-out via was removed.

**Step 2 – T-0250, dangling copper**
- Six one-segment links into the item's own pad, in `fixed_routes.py` under "T-0253":
  - J6.8 ×2 (0.3 mm), R403.1, Q6.1, R3.2, R4.2.
- New `routing_build.prune()` in `finish()`, before `clean()`/refill. It uses KiCad's own connectivity:
  - tracks: `TestTrackEndpointDangling`;
  - vias: `IsConnectedOnLayer` on fewer than two layers, **and** touching at most one item;
  - power and GND nets are never touched.
- FINDING (MAJOR, caught in a trial build and fixed before landing): without the "at most one item" condition,
  prune removed the /IO13 via at (64.15, 16.80). KiCad flags that via as dangling because both of its tracks are
  on F.Cu, but it carries U1.IO13's connection, so /IO13 went from 0 to 1 unconnected. The via now stays. It is
  the one via_dangling warning left, explained.
- KiCad 10 pcbnew quirks are documented in the code:
  - `BOARD::Remove()` breaks the SWIG track list;
  - a connectivity object reused after a via was deleted segfaults.

  prune uses `Delete()` and rebuilds connectivity on every pass.
- Pruned: 32 items, all non-power signal copper. That is 15 U1 fan-out vias with their stubs (+3V3, IO2, IO5,
  IO6, IO7, IO11, IO17, IO18, IO39, SW1_IO, SW2_IO, SW4_IO, DEN, MOD_ID, VBAT_SENSE) and the SW2_IO stub at R31.
