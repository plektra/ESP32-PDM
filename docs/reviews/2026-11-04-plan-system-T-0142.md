## Two things to know about your ruling

Neither changes the part you chose, and neither stops the package. Both are limits on the numbers, so the design notes
will state them in full. If either changes your mind, undo T-0129 and it comes back as a question.

**1. "VDS 59.4 V worst case" is the worst case at 25 °C, and it assumes a limited source.**
- **Warm TVS:** its breakdown voltage rises with temperature, so VDS reaches about 60.4 V with the TVS at 47 °C. That is
  slightly over Q30's 60 V, which is a 25 °C minimum and also rises when Q30 is warm (about 63 V at 125 °C). If the
  worst figures all coincide, Q30 takes a small share in avalanche, far less than the 80–115 mJ it takes today without a
  TVS. T-0137 said this in its reasoning, but the inbox question gave only 59.4 V and 0.6 V margin.
- **Limited source:** 59.4 V holds if the spike's source can drive at most 31 A into a short. A source that drives
  exactly 31 A into D30's clamp but has a low voltage behind it drives more at first. SMDJ40CA then stays under 60 V
  only for sources of about 334 V or more. An inductive load on the bus is limited by its own running current, so it
  fits the 31 A model.

**2. VS goes negative from about 20 A, not only at 31 A.** VS stays at 0 V or above up to about 20.3 A, and reaches
−4.0 V at 31 A. With a 31 A spike, VS passes 0 V after about 170 µs and stays negative until the spike ends. The
textbook pulse (ISO 7637-2 pulse 1, 12–15 A, figures from secondary sources) keeps VS at +2 V or above. Your acceptance
of "about −4 V" still covers this range, because −4 V is the lowest value in it.

## Which parts see negative VS

I traced the VS net in the schematic, because the ruling names only C31:

| VS branch | What it sees at −4 V | Source |
|---|---|---|
| C31 | **Reverse-biased, no rating in its datasheet. This is the accepted residual risk** | `docs/part-facts/C487384.md` |
| C103–C403, C104–C404 | Non-polar ceramics, no concern | netlist |
| F1 → R54 → U3 and C1 | D2 (DSK16) blocks it. U3 VIN (abs max −0.3 V) does not see it. D1 is bidirectional and does not conduct at 4 V | netlist, `docs/part-facts/C22427220.md` |
| F4 → R56 → panel supply | D55 (DSK16) blocks it | netlist |
| U11–U14 | Rated for reverse, per T-0137 | T-0137 report |
| R60 → IO10 | Current into IO10 is under 70 µA, per T-0137 | T-0137 report |

So C31 is the only polarised part exposed, which matches your ruling.
