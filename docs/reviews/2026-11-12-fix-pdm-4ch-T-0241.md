## Stage 1 – clean router export (done)
Freerouting on the main board's DSN: **723 pre-existing violations** (the brief said 722). They were:
- 125 pin-to-pin;
- 598 fixed trace/via vs ObstacleArea.

After the fix: **0** (no design warning at all; 408 unrouted at the start, as before). Three causes, all fixed in
`routing_build.py` (DSN/router copy only, no KiCad rule or zone touched):
1. **Fixed copper inside router keep-outs (598).**
   - What happened: a DSN keep-out holds for every net. So a pour's own vias and tracks inside its `pour-*`
     keep-out were violations, and so were foreign vias whose 0.8 mm escape was smaller than their class clearance.
   - Fix (`clear_fixed()`): every router-made keep-out (pour-*, antenna, CAN barrier, JP1/JP30, edge strip) gets a
     hole around each fixed track/via it touches. The hole is the item's class clearance + 0.05 mm. Another net
     cannot be placed in that hole anyway, so the protection is unchanged.
2. **Pin-to-pin (125).**
   - What happened: Freerouting checked fixed pad pairs against the net-class clearance (VS 1.0 mm, OUT 0.3, VBUS
     0.25). The footprints' own pad gaps cannot meet that: PROFET VS pins, VS capacitors, F1, R60, U30 and the J3
     VBUS/GND pins (KiCad gap 0.200 mm).
   - Fix (`pin_classes()`): every placed pin gets clearance class `PIN<class>`. Pin↔wire/via keeps the class value
     (the larger of the two). Pin↔pin is 190 µm; that covers only two fixed pads, which the router never moves, and
     the 190 absorbs Freerouting's polygon approximation of the 0.200 mm J3 gap. KiCad DRC (0.2 mm) stays the
     authority.
3. **Fixed vias at a same-net SMD pad (6, left after 1 and 2).**
   - Which: the R60.1 VS via column (57.4/40.8 and 42.2, VS copper, not to be moved), and the GND vias at D6.1,
     D103.1, D203.1 and R106.2 (gaps 0–0.15 mm).
   - Fix: fixed vias get `FIXV<class>`: 0 against pins and other fixed vias, the class value against routed
     wires/vias.

Pass 1 of a run now ends with "128 unrouted and 0 violations" (T-0236: 138 unrouted, 722 violations).

## Stage 3 – channel bus
**Placement comparison (relative to each PROFET, all rot 180):**
- Channels 1, 3 and 4 are identical: Rx01/Rx02/Rx05/Cx01 at +5.20 x, Rx03 +8.30, Rx07 +10.90, Rx04 (+7.00, −4.20),
  Dx01 (+7.00, +4.20), Dx02 (+10.50, −5.00).
- **Channel 2 differs**, because U12 sits against the U1 block: R203 (+7.55, −0.95), R204 (+5.20, −4.00), R207
  (+7.55, +3.80), D201 (+6.50, +7.50), D202 (+8.20, −5.25).
- The C/D power parts (Cx03/Cx04) are mirrored in y for ch3/ch4. They are not part of the signal bus.
- No power part was moved.

**Why a copied pattern does not work here.** The destinations are not channel-symmetric:
- D12–D15 sit in a column at x 59–63, y 56–72.
- U1's IO pins are at x 62.
- The two west channels (1, 4) sit in a B.Cu pocket (x 36–45.5) closed by VS-B-front/rear and the spine.
- The two east channels (2, 3) sit beside the spine.
- So the bus is generated per net by a script instead (`maze_route.py`: grid A* at 0.1 mm over F/In2/B, with the
  KiCad clearances, the pour keep-outs and the corridor rule built in). Its output is the generated block at the end
  of `fixed_routes.py`.
- Every SW_IN line uses one lane: pocket → via strip x 45.2–46.2/y 16–18 (In2 hop under the spine edge) → F.Cu
  between U12 and U13 → B.Cu lane x 56–61 → D12–D15.
- The strip holds only one lane besides USB, so SW_IN1 and SW_IN4 cannot both pass. Every one of 6 orders closed
  3 of 4.
