# Fix report – pdm-4ch T-0171: J10 socket C577772, stock fallback

Date: 2026-11-07 · Scope: `pdm-4ch` · Unattended (decision 23) · No owner ruling; all items two-way

## Summary

The J10 socket C577772 (Kefa KF2EDGSRM-7.62-2P) has JLCPCB/LCSC stock 0 and a minimum order of 18. There is no
design change. Two possible substitutes were checked against the existing footprint and the locking plug C577752,
and both fail. The only fallback is the same part, C577772, bought from another seller. That fallback is now in the
pdm-4ch pre-order checklist. Picking a different socket would need a new plug or footprint, which amends decisions
98/104. That question goes to the owner (T-0166 below).

  - the two other parts checked, why they fail, and that any other part goes to the owner (decisions 98/104).

### T-0166 (cost F1): name a fallback (C577767 or C7422678) – blocked, one-way
- **C577767**, KF2EDGSR-7.62-2P (`docs/part-facts/C577767.md`, new). Pad grid 2 × 2 at 7.62 × 7.62 mm, Ø1.50
  +0.10/0 mm holes: matches the footprint `Kefa_KF2EDGSRM-7.62-2P`. Rated 30 A UL group B and 32 A IEC (≥ 16 A).
  **It has no flange or flange holes.** C577752 is KF2EDGSKM, a plug with ear screws. With no flange on the header,
  those screws have nothing to go into, and the drawings show no latch. Mating and locking are UNVERIFIED and not
  supported by the drawings. Neither part shows keying. **Fails.**
- **C7422678**, DORABO DB3ERC-7.62-2P-GN (`docs/part-facts/C7422678.md`, new). Same pad grid and holes, same
  30 A / 32 A rating, also no flange. The drawing describes locking with C577752 as "UNVERIFIED, and unlikely".
  **Fails.**
- Neither part fits the existing footprint AND mates and locks with C577752 AND is rated ≥ 16 A AND keeps the keying
  against the 5.08 mm J11. Under the brief's stop rule, I did not pick either part. Only C577772 from another seller
  is named.
