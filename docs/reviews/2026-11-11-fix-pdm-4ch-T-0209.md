## What was written

The build lives in `hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/`:

- `build_routing.sh [runs=3] [passes=25]`. Close KiCad before running it. Two optional environment variables:
  `FR_STRATS` and `KEEP_WORK`. The script:
  - copies the project to a temp folder;
  - runs **prep**;
  - runs up to three headless Freerouting 2.5.0 runs in parallel, each with its own strategy and in its own folder;
  - runs **apply** on each run, then `kpcb drc` with parity;
  - keeps the best clean run, but only if it beats the board as it is at that moment.
- `routing_build.py` (pcbnew):
  - **prep:**
    - The first build locks every existing track, via and zone (the stage 4–5 power copper and the T-0207/T-0208
      links and fan-out). This was done in this stage.
    - Unlocked tracks and vias are removed, and the fixed routes are added, locked.
    - The pour baseline is recorded.
    - Router copy: power outlines become plane + keep-out, with 0.5 mm escapes for foreign pads. GND pours on
      F/In2/B are removed and the In1 planes stay. Keep-outs on all layers: antenna, CAN barrier, JP30/JP1, the
      holes and a 0.35 mm edge strip.
    - DSN net classes: In1 is barred for signals, and the buck stays on F.Cu only.
  - **apply:**
    - imports the SES and refills;
    - removes dangling vias and stubs;
    - adds GND/GND_CAN stitching on a 5 mm grid;
    - ties each GND island without a via;
    - checks the pours (island count and area against the baseline).
- `fixed_routes.py`: router classes, parameters and the fixed TRACKS/VIAS lists. The lists are empty so far.
  Hand-made routing goes here, never onto the board.

## Findings

- **MAJOR (fixed in the build): a power plane alone does not stop the router.** In a first 1-pass test, foreign
  tracks crossed the power planes in the DSN. After the refill, VS-B-spine, VS-In2, OUT1-B and OUT3 were split. The
  fix: each power outline is also exported as a keep-out. After that, every pour stayed whole.
- **MINOR (fixed): copper-to-edge.** The router did not keep 0.5 mm from the edge, which gave three edge errors. The
  fix is a 0.35 mm keep-out strip along the edge.
- **MINOR (rejected variant): own-net copper cut out of the keep-outs.** A test cut own-net copper out of the
  keep-outs to quiet Freerouting's "pre-existing violations". The tracks then ran through the VS pours along the
  cut-outs, so the variant was reverted. Freerouting itself marks those violations "unfixable", and the KiCad DRC
  is clean.
- **INFO: Freerouting 2.5.0 is deterministic and peaks at pass 1.** Three runs on one DSN were identical, and later
  passes ended worse (250–280 router-unrouted against ~130). More runs therefore do not help. Progress needs
  changed input: fixed routes, moves or copper. A build with `2 3` takes about 5 min.
- **MAJOR (open, structural): no lane west across the VS block.** In2 (VS-In2), B.Cu (VS spine) and F.Cu (PROFETs,
  OUT pours) are all closed, so the router cannot reach the channel side x < 58. That blocks:
  - DEN to R102–R402;
  - SW_IN1–4 from R101–R401 to D12–D15;
  - +3V3 to D102/D202/D402 and R9/C3;
  - EN and IO0;
  - USB_DN/DP, F2/J3 CC/VBUS;
  - IO2, IO5, NTC and U12/U14-IS.

  A deliberate lane is needed. Options: an F.Cu gap between U12 and U13, or a narrow signal channel through
  VS-In2. Either is a power-copper change that needs a current-path check, and it goes into the scripts.
