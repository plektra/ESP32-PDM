# Fix T-0071 – pdm-hc2 controller pin filtering (T-0056, T-0005, T-0008)

Unattended run, two-way items (no owner ruling), scope `pdm-hc2`, sheet `hc_channel` (×2; refs are ch1/ch2).
Part facts: `docs/part-facts/C17556513.md` (extended in this run with IQ, POR, UV/OV thresholds and delays, CS bias,
DIODE, Table 9-3 and Fig. 9-18 – TPS4811-Q1 SLUSEE5E). Details and calculations: `docs/design-notes.md` §4a.1.
Every new fitted part is already on the BOM, so there is **no new unique part**: pdm-hc2 201 parts (+16), 9 DNP (+4),
75 unique (+0).

## T-0056 – DIODE bypass (done)

C107/C207 1 nF 0402 (C1523, basic) from DIODE to GND across Q13/Q23, fitted. TI 9.6.1 (p. 40) says this capacitor
"must be placed". τ ≈ 3 µs at the 10 µA sense current, which does not matter for a temperature reading. STATUS notes
the PCB rule: put it at the MMBT3904 and route the emitter Kelvin to U11/U12 GND (pin 6).

## T-0005 – RVS/CVS (done)

- R114/R214 RVS 100 Ω 0402 (C25076, basic) from VS to the new local net `VS_CTRL` (U11/U12 pin 20).
- C102/C202 became the CVS: they moved from VS to `VS_CTRL` and changed from 100 nF/100 V 0805 (C28233) to 1 µF/100 V
  X7R 1206 (C13832, already on the BOM as C30). C28233 stays in use (C19, C54).
- IVS check: the datasheet has no separate IVS row. I(Q) at the GND pin is 613/700 µA typ/max (EN = 2 V). I take it to
  include the charge pump, which is fed from VS (stated as an assumption). Drop ≤ 70 mV, well under 0.3 V. At a 6 V
  crank dip the pin is still ≈ 5.9 V, far above V(VS_PORR) 3.2 V max. The 3.75 A gate-drive peak is drawn from CBST,
  not VS.
- UVLO/OV thresholds are not moved: the R105 divider stays on VS ahead of RVS.
- CS+ reference is not moved: RSET (R102) stays on VS, as in TI Fig. 8-1. Fig. 9-18 shows RVS between the supply line
  and VS, so this is the topology TI draws.
- τ = 100 µs, so the pin's 60 V/µs slew limit cannot be reached.
- A PWR_FLAG (#FLG906/907, not a BOM part) on `VS_CTRL` clears the ERC error "power pin not driven" that RVS caused.

## T-0008 – CS / EN / OV / IWRN filters (done, Y caps DNP)

| Ref | Value | Fitted | Time constant vs the protection it filters |
|---|---|---|---|
| C108/C208 X cap CS+–CS− | 1 nF C1523 | yes | 100 Ω × 1 nF = 0.1 µs = 2.5 % of tSC 4 µs (80 A trip); ≪ tOC 146 ms |
| C109/C209 Y CS+–GND, C110/C210 Y CS−–GND | 100 pF/100 V C0G C296717 | **DNP** | timing passes (10 ns), but the matched-value check fails in effect (below) |
| C111/C211 EN/UVLO | 1 nF C1523 | yes | UV turn-off delay 0.06–0.21 ms |
| C112/C212 OV | 1 nF C1523 | yes | OV cut-off delay 0.10–0.40 ms (≤ 1 % of a 40 ms load dump) |
| C113/C213 IWRN | 1 nF C1523 | yes | 47 kΩ × 1 nF = 47 µs vs tOC 146 ms |

- **Y caps DNP:** CS− sits directly on the shunt, so C110 has no effect. C109 alone, behind RSET, would turn about 9 %
  of the common-mode VS noise into a differential error (C_Y/(C_X+C_Y)). They are matched in value but not in source
  impedance. TI uses them only together with FB1/FB2 in both lines. DC offset: none, since the ceramic caps carry no
  DC current and V(OS_SET) ±200 µV is unchanged.
- **10 nF rejected for EN/OV:** with the 1 MΩ divider, 10 nF delays the OV cut-off by 1–4 ms, which leaves no clear
  margin. 1 nF already shorts VHF pick-up (≈ 1 Ω at 156 MHz).
- **IWRN 1 nF instead of 100 pF:** this reuses C1523 and still has a clear margin. If CTMR is ever removed (tOC 25 µs),
  go back to 100 pF.
- The DIODE capacitor is handled under T-0056.
- Final values are set at the pre-compliance scan (Q-0012).

None of the filters slows the 80 A trip or the breaker beyond the datasheet response, and RVS keeps VS far above
V(VS_PORR), so no stop condition was hit.

## Failure mode (safety)

Design-notes §4a.1 has the full note. In short:
- A shorted C108 blinds the breaker and the short-circuit comparator.
- A shorted C113 turns overcurrent protection off.
- A shorted C112 removes the OV cut-off.
- A shorted C111, a shorted C102 or an open R114 turns the channel off. For the bilge pump that means no pump.

A competent person must review these protection circuits before installation.

## Checks

- ERC after each step: step 1 gave 0 errors. Step 2 first gave 2 `power_pin_not_driven` errors on U11/U12 pin 20; the
  PWR_FLAG cleared them. Step 3 gave 0 errors. Final: 0 errors, 5 `lib_symbol_mismatch` warnings (unchanged).
- Netlist (kicadxml before/after per step): only the intended nodes were added. U11/U12 pin 20 and C102/C202 pin 1
  moved from VS to `/HC channel n/VS_CTRL`. R114/R214 bridge VS and VS_CTRL.
- `tools/fix_check.py --scope pdm-hc2`: **OK – passes the merge gates.**
- Heat budget: not rerun, since no output rating or switch part changed.

## Open points

- The VS-pin current with the charge pump running is not stated separately (I(Q) used). Measure on the prototype.
- The 0402 RVS pulse rating under a full VS step is UNVERIFIED. It is not expected in practice because VS has ≈ 300 µF
  of bulk capacitance and a TVS (design notes).
- The Y caps, and whether to add ferrites, are decided at Q-0012.

## Suggestions (not done)

- If the EMC scan needs the Y caps, add FB1/FB2-type ferrites on CS+/CS− (Fig. 9-18) so the two lines are balanced.
