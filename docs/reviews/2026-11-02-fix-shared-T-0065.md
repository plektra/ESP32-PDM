# Fix report T-0065 – shared supply, USB and fuse parts (2026-11-02, unattended)

Scope: shared sheets `power-usb`, `can`, `analog_sw`, edited with `tools/ksch.py` scripts and kept identical on
`pdm-4ch` and `pdm-hc2` (only the `(project …)` name differs). Calculations and sources: `docs/design-notes.md` §8.3.

## Per item

### T-0018 (Q-0011) – PTC Imax check: **FAILED → blocked, one-way, nothing changed**
LUTE 1812L series datasheet (Rev. 2020, p. 3), 1812L050/60GR: I_max **40 A** at V_max 60 V, R_min 0.150 Ω. Note 3: the part must not
be used where I_max can be exceeded in a fault condition. The ruling's prospective current: 30 V / (0.150 + 0.010) Ω ≈ **188 A**
(4.7 × I_max) for a hard short directly behind F1 (on-board: D1, C1, U3 input), F4 (J5.5 terminal / first metres of the
panel cable, VS-fed) or F3 (J4/NET-S side; in practice the N2K power-tee fuse and drop-cable resistance limit this, but it is not guaranteed).
Even with 0.3 Ω of wiring the current is ≈ 65 A. F4 feeds the panel switches (bilge forced run, navigation lights), so this is
safety-relevant. A competent person must review F1/F3/F4 before installation. Following the brief, nothing was changed and the F2 swap
(which depended on this check) was not done.

Proposal for the owner (also in the fix block):
- (a) Swap F1/F3/F4 to an 1812 60 V PTC listed with I_max 100 A (TECHFUSE mSMD050-60V C70113, 0.088 USD, or JDT
  ASMD1812-050-60V C427396; same footprint; datasheets to be checked) **and** add a series resistance so that 30 V / R_total
  ≤ I_max. For example, ≥ 0.3 Ω total including the PTC: a 0.15–0.22 Ω pulse-rated resistor per PTC. Its pulse energy until trip must be
  checked. The F1 load is ≈ 0.2 A, F4 ≈ 50 mA, F3 ≤ 77 mA, so the DC drop is negligible.
- (b) Accept the residual risk and document it (prospective current at the board is limited only by battery, cable and terminals).
- No JLCPCB 1812 PTC covers 188 A on its own.

### T-0026 (Q-0019) – four L3 swaps: three adopted, F2 not adopted
- **FB1, FB2** BLM18AG601SN1D → **GZ2012D601TF (C1017, basic, 0805)**. Sunlord GZ catalogue p. 3: 600 Ω ± 25 % at 100 MHz,
  DCR ≤ 0.30 Ω, 500 mA. FB1 carries ≤ 0.40 A from VBUS (USB-only operation). **Pass.**
- **F2** SMD0805B050TF stays: the swap depended on T-0018, which failed.
- **D4–D6** PESD0402V05 → **H5VL10B (C7420372, preferred, DFN1006-2 → `Diode_SMD:D_SOD-882`)**. V_RWM 5 V, V_C ≤ 8 V at 1 A /
  ≤ 10 V at 5 A (8/20 µs), C_J 15 typ / 18 max pF, ±25 kV IEC 61000-4-2. This is inside the ≤ 15–20 pF USB FS budget. The old
  polymer part clamped at 40 V (8 kV contact), so ESD protection is **stronger**, not weaker. **Pass.**
- **U6** TPS7B91 → **L78M05ABDT (C58069, basic, DPAK `TO-252-2`)**. V_I abs max 35 V vs the SMAJ18CA clamp 29.2 V (20 % margin).
  I_d 6 mA (+0.8 mA). **LEN unchanged:** with ISO1044 I_CC2 (TI SLLSFB0A) 38 mA max (1 Mbit/s traffic) the NET-S current goes
  38 → 44.8 mA (LEN 1), and 70 → 76.8 mA held dominant (LEN 2). Dropout 2 V: 8.5 V available at NET-S 9 V. DPAK pinout
  1 IN / tab GND / 3 OUT matches the KiCad L7805 symbol and the TO-252-2 pads. The symbol was placed on the old pin points (same UUID),
  so IN/GND nets are unchanged and OUT moved from pin 5 to pin 3. Input capacitor C13 raised 0.1 µF → **1 µF/50 V 0805 (C28323, basic)**
  for the datasheet's C_I 0.33 µF. Heat ≈ 0.48 W → +48 K (R_θJA 100 K/W). DPAK area noted for `/system mechanical`. **Pass.**

  (Yageo CC1206KKX7RCBB472, C107198, extended)** fitted, and **R14 0 Ω 0603 (C21189) DNP** as the hard-tie option (auto choice:
  RC fitted, 0 Ω DNP). No basic 4.7 nF part ≥ 250 V exists.
- TLP/clamp check of the remaining ESD part (H5VL10B): no TLP curve published; 8/20 µs clamp data verified (above). Recorded
  on the pre-order checklist. A USBLC6-2-type part is the alternative if TLP data are required.
- J3 USB-C stays fitted (Q-0029).

### T-0015 (Q-0009) – capacitor ratings: done
- **C1** 2.2 µF/50 V → **2.2 µF/100 V X7R 1206 Samsung CL31B225KCHSNNE (C170101, extended)**. No basic or preferred ≥ 63 V 2.2 µF
  exists. Reusing C13832 (1 µF/100 V) twice would give 2 µF, below TI's 2.2 µF minimum, so a new extended part was accepted.
- **C50–C53** 1 nF 0402 → **1 nF/2 kV X7R 1206 FH 1206B102K202NT (C9196, basic)**. This is the only basic 1 nF part ≥ 63 V. Values kept.

### T-0022 (Q-0015) – L1 second sources: done (documentation only)
SWPA6045S8R2MT, Sunltech SLW6045S8R2MST (C47018494) and cjiang FNR6045S8R2MT (C168074) have the same datasheet values:
8.2 µH ± 20 %, DCR 0.043 typ / 0.056 Ω max, I_sat 3.90 A, I_rms 2.60 A, 6.0 × 6.0 × 4.5 mm, land a 2.8 / b 1.7 / c 5.7 mm. Both
are approved and recorded in both STATUS files. The stock recheck is on the new pre-order checklist. SLW has no EasyEDA footprint at JLCPCB.
