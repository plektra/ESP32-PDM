# Cost review, pdm-4ch, 2026-11-21

Full re-pricing of pdm-4ch at HEAD `e039a92`, with decisions 235 and 238 in and 4 layers (decision 150). The previous full report is `2026-11-07-cost-system-2.md`, which priced 2 layers and 55 lines.

Method:
- **BOM:** `fab/ESP32-PDM-4CH-bom-jlc.csv` has 54 LCSC lines and 199 placed parts, matching the 199 CPL rows. I added the seven hand-soldered connector codes (J4, J5, J10 and J11 sockets and plugs).
- **Prices:** every code was looked up live (`jlc_get_part live=true`, JLCPCB, 2026-11-21 21:04 UTC). Each price is the tier for parts per board times assembled boards. Attrition, minimum-purchase rounding and shipping are not included.
- **Basis:** Standard PCBA, top side only. Economic is not offered with 2 oz (decision 99), and U1 C2913206 is `standard_only`.

### Change since the last full report (4-ch, 5/2, per assembled board)

The per-board figure went from 99.51 to 102.73 USD, a change of **+3.22**.
- The PCB went from 2 layers at 40.67 USD to 4 layers at 49.07 USD. That is +8.40 per order, or +4.20 per board, and it is the cost of decision 150.
- Lines went from 55 to 54, which is −1.53 per order (−0.77 per board).
- Parts went from 38.20 to 37.76 per order. Price drift and tier effects net out; the RIS swap to C844867 adds about 0.1 per order at tier (+0.66 at 1+ price).
- The first order with connectors went from about 204 to 210.5 USD (about 220 to 227 with the fixture fee).

## Levers

None is large. Largest saving first.

**Owner ruling 2026-11-22: L1 declined** (saving too small; R50–R53 stay 470 Ω, decision 17 unchanged). No design change from this review.

| # | Saving | What changes | Trade-off | Must-stay check | Effort | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| L1 | 1.53 USD per order (0.77 per board) | R50–R53 (LED sink emitter resistors) go from 470 Ω C25117 to 1 kΩ C11702, already on the BOM (R18, R20, R23, R26). C25117 leaves the BOM. Same 0402 footprint, no layout change | Panel LED current falls from 5.6 to 2.7 mA (about 2.65 V across the resistor), so panel LEDs are dimmer. Changes the ≈5 mA of decision 17 | Convenience only. The LED lines' protection (SMAJ30CA, 100 Ω, 1 nF) is untouched, and Q3–Q6 run cooler | One value swap; no heat-budget effect | BOM; decision 17; ARCHITECTURE `analog_sw` row |

Closed on inspection, not levers:
- **C13 (1 µF/50 V 0805, C28323) to C15849, one line:** design-notes §8 "C13 check" already shows C15849 fails the DC-bias check at the 29 V clamp.
- **R1/R2/R5/R6 2.2 kΩ to another value:** these sit on the channel-drive diode-OR nodes in the switch path, so I do not touch them. It would also drop a line only if R59/R67 moved too.
- **USB-C removal** (about 4 lines, about 6 USD per order): contradicts decisions 31 and 83, which keep USB-C fitted on every run.
- **Extended to basic or preferred** (200 mΩ 2512, PESD2CAN, NTC, PTC): saves nothing under Standard, where every line costs 1.53 USD. Only the part price moves.
- **BTT6010 population:**
  - C534858 costs 3.0956 USD against 1.2762 at 1+, so +1.82 per channel and +7.3 USD per board for four channels.
  - A mixed population adds one line (+1.53 per order); a full swap adds none.
  - Decision 103 has no BTT6010 build. This is a cost of a later option, not a saving.
- **Enclosure, DIN carrier, panel switches, M12 connector (decision 237):** no costed design, so not priced.

## Rule findings

- **R1 (MINOR, docs): the `fab/README.md` price table uses the wrong fee basis.**
  - It prices "15 unique extended × 3 USD = 45", which is the Economic fee.
  - The real basis is Standard: 54 × 1.53 + 34.22 = 116.84 USD fixed per order, so the README understates fixed fees by about 72 USD.
  - Cheapest fix: replace that table with the figures above.
- **R2 (INFO, docs):** `docs/CONTINUE-FROM-HERE.md` and `hardware/pdm-4ch/STATUS.md` still say "about 204 USD (220 with fixture)". It is now 210.5 (226.9) because of the 4-layer PCB.
- **R3 (MINOR): J10 KF135T-10.16-2P C475108 stock is 523 and falling.** It was 2071 on 11-07, 821 on 11-17 and 523 today; two are needed. Fix: buy it with the board order (decision 127) and recheck on the day.
- **R4 (INFO): C8 C45783 (Samsung 22 µF) is listed Obsolete at DigiKey** on 2026-11-21. JLCPCB stock is 4.38 M, so the prototype is unaffected. Pick a successor before any production run.
- **Extended where basic or preferred fits:** none found.
  - No no-fee 200 mΩ 2512 exists.
  - The preferred CAN TVS H24VNT2B C20615827 is not a drop-in (55 V clamp against the PESD2CAN's 41 V).
  - There are 15 unique extended lines, which carry no extra fee under Standard.
- **Non-standard values:** none (E12/E24 plus 0 Ω and 200 mΩ).
- **Stock below 500:** none. The lowest are L1 C96963 at 1173, BTT6050 1604, AGM601LL 1650 and LM74502H-Q1 1983. SMAJ30CA C19077548 is now 23 831 (it was 172 in the 11-17 README).
- **Single source:** ESP32-S3-MINI-1-N8 (the N4R2 variant is dearer at 5.01 USD), BTT6050, LM74502H-Q1, LMR51610, CA-IS3050G, AGM601LL, Vishay C844867, and the Kefa/Kangnex connectors. All known; L1 C96963 has approved alternates (decision 49).
- **2 oz DFM:** JLCPCB's copper-weight page gives a 0.16 mm minimum trace/space for 2 oz outer. The board's thinnest segment is 0.2 mm (4732 segments, none below 0.19 mm), so it passes.
