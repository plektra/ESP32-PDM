# Plan T-0131 (2026-11-03): three approved items, all pdm-4ch

**Result:** one package, one deferred item, no decision rows. None of the items has an owner ruling. All three are
two-way doors, and no board is ordered.

## Package

**P1 – Q30 cost lever and the §8.6 (b) correction (T-0126, T-0129), scope `pdm-4ch`.** Both items concern Q30. The
corrected §8.6 (b) text has to name the avalanche rating of whichever part stays, so the swap check comes first and the
correction is written second. T-0129 alone would fit `sync`, but it would then have to wait for T-0126's result in a
second session.

**Expect the swap to be dropped.** This is an estimate, and the datasheet check in the package decides:

| Input current | Q30 Tj, 0.75 mΩ (today) | Q30 Tj, 1.25 mΩ (estimate) |
|---|---|---|
| 16 A | 54 °C | 60 °C |
| 24 A | 67 °C | 82 °C |
| 32 A | 86 °C | 123 °C |
| 40 A | 122 °C | ≈ 221 °C (Tj max of the present part: 175 °C) |

- **Method:** the §8.6 method: 40 K/W on 6 cm² of 2 oz copper, with enclosure air from `tools/heat_budget.py`. The
  0.75 mΩ column reproduces the §8.6 table.
- **Assumption:** the 1.25 mΩ column scales the IPT007N06N max curve to 1.25 mΩ at 25 °C. AGM601LL's own curve and RthJA
  are not read yet.
- **Why more loss feeds on itself:** at 40 A the extra loss heats the junction, which raises RDS(on) further. That is
  why 0.5 mΩ more at 25 °C costs about 100 K, and why the cost report's "≈ 2 W" is too low (≈ 4.2 W).
- **The saving at stake:** about 2.2 USD per board at 10 boards. If the swap is dropped, Q30 stays and P1 only changes
  documents.

**Two corrections to the items as written, both in the brief:**

1. T-0126 says to rerun `tools/heat_budget.py` with Q30 at 1.25 mΩ. That is not enough. The tool has no Q30 junction:
   `--r-path` only adds path loss to the enclosure air, so it would pass while Q30 overheats. The brief requires the
   §8.6 junction calculation as well.
2. T-0129 gives about 45 mJ, which is the energy stored in the VS capacitors. The energy Q30 absorbs is the charge
   leaving those capacitors times the avalanche voltage, because D30 holds the source at −48.4 V. For 109 µF that is
   about 115 mJ at 60 V (about 82 mJ at 66 V). The item's conclusion stands, since EAS is 1100 mJ, but the text should
   carry the right figure.

## Outside the items – for the owner, not planned

**Decision 82 and 50 A.** The owner's ruling asks for a blocker "sized for the 40-50 A input path", but §8.6 only checks
up to 40 A. With today's Q30 and the same method, 50 A gives Tj ≈ 200 °C, using enclosure air no hotter than the 40 A
case, so a real figure would be higher. The Tj limit of 125 °C is reached at about 40 A and Tj max 175 °C at about
47 A. `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` gives the pdm-4ch input terminal as ≈ 40 A, and 50 A belongs to HC2, so this may be only
wording in the ruling. No item asks for a change, so none is planned. It can be raised in the next `/system` session.
