### MINOR

**E18 – pdm-4ch, root sheet: Q30 (60 V) can see more than 60 V across it when D30 clamps a negative transient while VS is charged.**
- Parts: Q30 IPT007N06N V(BR)DSS 60 V min (`C122552.md`), D30 SMCJ30CA, C31 and C104–C404 holding VS.
- Evidence: design-notes §8.6 (b) says that in reverse Q30 blocks VDS = VS − VBAT_FL ≤ 48.4 V. That is true for a steady
  reversed battery with VS discharged. For a negative transient on VBAT_IN, D30 clamps to about −48.4 V while the
  capacitors still hold VS at 12–29 V; U30 turns Q30 off (SOURCE below POR) and VDS = 29 V + 48.4 V ≈ 77 V (12 V system:
  ≈ 63 V). U30 SOURCE (−65 V) and Q30 are the only parts that see this; the PROFETs are protected by the blocked path.
- Mechanism and consequence: Q30 goes into avalanche for the length of the pulse. Energy is bounded by the charge in
  C31 and C104–C404 (about 110 µF, ≈ 45 mJ at 29 V) against EAS 1100 mJ (single pulse, `C122552.md`), so Q30 survives;
  VS then sags toward ≈ 18 V for that moment. Whether such negative transients occur on the Vega supply is `UNVERIFIED`
  (ISO 7637-type pulse 1 is the textbook case; the design target is IEC 60945, decision 28, and decision 12 already
  declares load dump system-level). Hence MINOR.
- Fix: no part. Correct the statement in design-notes §8.6 (b) to "VDS = VS − VBAT_FL, up to ≈ 77 V for a negative
  transient with VS charged; Q30 avalanches, EAS 1100 mJ covers the stored energy". Not adding a TVS on VS: it would add a
  part and contradict the "no TVS on VS" wording that §8.6 (c) relies on, for an event that is already covered by EAS.
- Door: two-way (documentation).

## Layout rules (PCB phase)

Rules 1–9 of the last report stand. Additions and changes for T-0118–T-0121:

10. **Q30/U30/C32 (4-ch).** Order along the battery path: J10 → D30 and C30 on the terminal copper (VBAT_IN) → JP30/FL30
    → Q30 source → Q30 drain (VS copper, ≥ 6 cm² 2 oz) → C31 → the VS tabs. Keep J10–C30–Q30–C31 as one compact loop; C31
    right at Q30's drain, not across the board (Q30 is now between the D30 clamp and the bulk capacitor, so the
    clamp-to-C31 loop includes Q30 and its copper).
11. **U30 beside Q30.** C32 at U30 pins 4/5 (VCAP–SOURCE), U30 SOURCE pin and Q30 source pin joined by a short, wide
    track, GATE track short and not routed near the buck switch node or the ESP32 antenna. GATE is internally tied to
    VCAP when on, so C32 is also the RF hold-up of the gate. Add 22 nF to 100 nF at the SOURCE pin only if the track from
    C30 is longer than a few cm (datasheet CIN advice).
12. **Series resistors R54–R56 (2512).** Place between the PTC and the TVS of the same line, pad-to-pad in a row (F → R →
    TVS), clamp return to GND short. F3/R55 stay on the GND_CAN side only.
13. **USB shield C18/R13** next to J3 shield pads, return to the ground plane by a via within 3 mm.
14. **C104–C404** same footprint as before (1206): at the BTT6050 VS tab, ground via beside the pad.

## Installation guidance (for the README)

Unchanged from the previous report. One addition: the pdm-4ch now blocks a reversed battery, but the blocker is a
protection of last resort; still check polarity, and still fuse the battery at the source.
