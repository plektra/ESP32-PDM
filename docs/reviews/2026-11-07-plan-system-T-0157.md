## Three things to look at

These came out of checking the items against the schematics. None stops the work. Where I chose a default, it is a
cheap undo and it is marked as mine, not yours.

1. **T-0151 assumes pins that are not spare on pdm-hc2.** IO35, IO36 and IO37 are unconnected on pdm-4ch, but on
   pdm-hc2 they carry the fault signals FLT_T1, FLT_I2 and FLT_T2 (`ARCHITECTURE.md` pin map, confirmed in the
   netlist).
   - The item also asks for each pin to be checked against the pin map, so I followed that check. Both boards get the
     same unfitted footprint, but on pdm-hc2 those three positions stay unconnected and only IO42 is brought out.
   - pdm-hc2 therefore gets one spare signal instead of four. That matters little for now, since decision 102 puts
     pdm-hc2 after pdm-4ch bring-up.
   - IO34 on pdm-4ch and IO33 on pdm-hc2 are also free, but the item does not ask for them, so I did not add them. If
     you want more spare pins on pdm-hc2, IO33 is the obvious one.
2. **T-0148 weakens the connector keying between the two boards.** The new pdm-4ch J10 is the same 7.62 mm set as
   pdm-hc2 J11.
   - On one board the keying still holds, because pdm-4ch J11 is 5.08 mm.
   - Across boards it does not: a pdm-4ch battery plug fits a pdm-hc2 output socket, which decision 98 meant to
     prevent. This can happen where both boards share an enclosure (decision 97).
   - It does not affect the first order, which is pdm-4ch only (decision 102). The package carries out your ruling and
     records this on the "For /system" list, so you can choose before pdm-hc2 is laid out, for example by labelling
     the plugs.
3. **T-0149 says "HC2 R9", but R9 is on the shared `power-usb` sheet.** The gates require that sheet to be identical
   on both boards, so R9 goes to C36871 on both. pdm-hc2 still loses a line. pdm-4ch swaps one line for another with
   no saving and no extra fee.

Also note for T-0149: the cached facts for C15849 hold no DC-bias curve. If the fixer cannot find a primary curve for
29 V, C13 stays on C28323, as the item says. The saving is then one line smaller.
