# Fix T-0161 – buck input filter position (shared, `power-usb`), 2026-11-07

Unattended board-fixer run. Item: **T-0153** (owner ruling "yes", 2026-11-06). The change is on the shared sheet `power-usb`
and is identical on both boards: the two files differ only in `(project "…")`. pdm-hc2's file is pdm-4ch's file with the
project name replaced.

## What changed and why

1. **Net split.** Before, Net-(D2-K) held D2.K, D3.K, C1, C19 and U3 VIN/EN. Now the order is D2.K → JP1 ‖ L2 →
   Net-(D3-K) = C1, C19, C22, D3.K, U3 VIN/EN. D3 (USB) is on the U3 side, so USB-only input is unchanged (3.74 V at 0.40 A,
   decision 87). C1 and C19 stay at U3 (decisions 30, 48).
2. **Bridge = copper, not a 0 Ω part.** First I checked C17168 (0402) and C21189 (0603) through part facts. Each is rated
   1 A, with a 2 A overload (5 s) and < 50 mΩ, and neither gives **any pulse or fusing figure**. A short behind the position
   while F1 trips can reach ≈ 86 A peak and ≤ 9.6 A²s (decision 81 figures). A chip jumper cannot be shown to survive that,
   and one that opens would act as an unrated fuse in the F1 path. That would change the protection. A larger chip jumper
   has the same gap: no maker data. The brief allows a copper bridge, and its survival can be calculated. **JP1** uses
   KiCad's `Jumper:SolderJumper-2_P1.3mm_Bridged_Pad1.0x1.5mm`: a 0.6 × 0.5 mm copper bar, net tie, cuttable, no BOM or
   placement entry. It is marked `in_bom no` and `dnp yes`, as JP30 is (decision 41 precedent). By Onderdonk at 40 °C it takes
   87 A²s at 2 oz (decisions 99, 101) and 35 A²s at 1 oz, against the 9.6 A²s bound. Its resistance is ≈ 0.2 mΩ.
3. **L2** DNP, SWPA6045S8R2MT C96963 (on the BOM as L1), `Inductor_SMD:L_Sunlord_SWPA6045S`, example 8.2 µH. I_rms 2.6 A
   covers every current F1 carries for longer than ≈ 0.3 s, since F1 holds 0.5 A and trips at ≥ 1 A. In a hard short L2
   saturates and R54 + PTC limit the current as today.
4. **Damping option** DNP: C22 2.2 µF/100 V C170101 (same as C1) in series with R73 10 Ω 1206 C17903 (same as R44), from the
   C1/U3 node to GND. No fitted part, no new line.
5. **Checks** (design-notes §8.3, "Buck input filter position"):
   - With the bridge fitted, the only change to the buck input is ≈ 0.2 mΩ.
   - With the inductor fitted: f0 is 33–57 kHz (L ± 20 %, C 1.2–2.3 µF with DC bias), giving 49–58 dB at f_sw.
   - Middlebrook: |Z_in| is 49 Ω at 9.5 V and 29 Ω at a 7.3 V cranking dip, with R_s = R54 + PTC + DCR = 0.39–1.26 Ω. Undamped,
     the peak |Z_out| reaches 21 Ω, which is too thin (1.4×). With 10 Ω + 2.2 µF it is ≤ 7.1 Ω (4.1×), with 3.3 Ω ≤ 4.9 Ω.
   - Drop: 11 mV at 0.19 A.
   - LMR51610 input requirements are kept: C1 2.2 µF + C19 0.1 µF stay at the pins, and VIN stays ≥ 4 V.
   - The scan starts from 10 Ω + 2.2 µF and moves to 3.3–4.7 Ω if needed.
6. **STATUS** (both): a dated entry; the layout rule (the input loop C1/C19/U3 stays tight and unchanged, and the position
   sits before C1); the decision 28 pre-compliance item; and decision 81's hard short behind F1 made with JP1 fitted.

FINDING (MINOR, documented, no change needed now): with L2 fitted and undamped, an ideal 28.8 → 48.4 V surge step can
ring U3 VIN up to ≈ 64 V, against 65 V recommended and 70 V abs max. Today the peak is ≤ 48.4 V. Damping lowers it to
≈ 58–59 V. The ideal step is pessimistic. This is why L2 is to be fitted only with C22/R73, and why the scan scopes VIN
during a hot-plug.

Safety: F1, R54 and their ratings are unchanged, and so is the prospective short current. The failure mode is in
design-notes §8.3. **A competent person reviews F1 and R54 before installation (decision 81).**
