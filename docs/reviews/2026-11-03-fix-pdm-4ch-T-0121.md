# Fix report pdm-4ch T-0121: reverse-battery blocker on VS (2026-11-03)

Item T-0088 (EMC E16, `docs/reviews/2026-11-03-emc-system.md`), owner ruling Q-0054: "Add reverse blocking now, this
revision: a reverse-blocking element (P-FET or ideal-diode controller) on VS in front of the bulk capacitors on
pdm-4ch, sized for the 40-50 A path and checked with the heat budget at 40 C. Until it is in, limit the README/design-notes
claim to the BTT6050 and warn that C31 is not reverse-rated. Still key J10 (decision 34)."
Scope pdm-4ch only. pdm-hc2 is untouched (decision 40; C106/C206 not in scope).

## What changed and why

**Step 1 – interim text (done first).** README and design-notes §8 (T-0050 paragraph) now say that the 28 V / 2 min
survival applies to the BTT6050 only, and that C31 (C487384) is not reverse-rated and can overheat and vent. After the
blocker went in, both texts were updated again (below). The C31 warning stays for the case where Q30 has failed short.

**Step 2 – topology (root sheet of pdm-4ch).** J10.1 → `VBAT_IN` (D30, C30, unchanged) → FL30 (DNP) / JP30 → **new net
`VBAT_FL`** → Q30 source → Q30 drain → `VS` (C31, C104–C404, U11–U14, F1, F4, R60, unchanged). U30 SOURCE (5) + EN (1) on
VBAT_FL, GATE (6) → Q30 G, VCAP (4) → C32 → VBAT_FL, GND (2) → GND, N.C. pins 3/7/8 typed `no_connect`. The VS
power flag on FL30.1 was removed. The one on JP30.1 moved to Q30's drain. A GND flag (#PWR0907) was added for U30.
- Why not a P-FET: the best 60 V P-FETs at JLCPCB are 3.5–4.7 mΩ at 25 °C (≈ 6–7 mΩ hot), giving ≈ 10 W at 40 A.
- Why not an ideal-diode controller: it blocks VS → VBAT_IN and breaks the OUT → body diode → VS → D30 surge path
  (invariant 4). That would need a TVS on VS, which is stop condition (ii).
- LM74500-Q1 is a reverse-polarity controller **without** reverse-current blocking. It keeps the N-FET fully on in both
  directions, so the invariant-4 path stays. (a) Invariant 1: powered from VBAT_FL, EN tied to SOURCE, so it needs
  no MCU and no 3.3 V. (b) Invariant 3: Q30 is rated 60 V and U30 65 V abs max, against the 48.4 V clamp. VGS ≤ 13.9 V
  (charge-pump regulation, 15 V abs max) against Q30's ±20 V; when reversed or off, the gate is tied to the source. The
  gate never sees the battery, so no zener is needed. (c) The path is kept. (d) The bulk capacitors charge through the
  body diode before the gate rises, so Q30 turns on at VDS < 1 V and stays out of its linear SOA.

**Step 3 – parts (all facts via `tools/part_facts.py`).**
- Q30 **Infineon IPT007N06N**, C122552, PG-HSOF-8-1 (TOLL), extended, 3.55 USD, stock 4707. VDS 60 V, VGS ±20 V,
  RDS(on) 0.75 mΩ max at 25 °C, ≈ 1.00 mΩ at 100 °C, ≈ 1.25 mΩ at 150 °C, Tj 175 °C, RthJA 40 K/W (6 cm²) / 62 K/W
  (minimal), ISM 1200 A, Ciss 21.3 nF max (`docs/part-facts/C122552.md`). KiCad `Infineon_PG-HSOF-8-1` pads: 1 G, 2 S,
  3 D, which matches the datasheet pinout (pin 1 G, pins 2–8 S, tab D).
- U30 **TI LM74500-Q1** (LM74500QDDFRQ1), C5219210, SOT-23-THIN-8, extended, 1.82 USD, stock 2923
  (`docs/part-facts/C5219210.md`). KiCad `TSOT-23-8` (0.65 mm pitch, 2.9 × 1.6 mm body, the same as DDF).
- C32 1 µF/50 V X5R 0603, C15849, **basic, already on the BOM**. The VCAP capacitor must be ≥ 10 × Ciss = 213 nF. DC-bias
  loss is not in the facts file; it may lose up to 79 % before it falls short.
- New library symbols `ESP32-PDM:IPT007N06N` (drawn from the BSC030N08NS5 symbol) and `ESP32-PDM:LM74500-Q1` in
  `lib/ESP32-PDM.kicad_sym`, embedded identically in the root sheet (no new lib_symbol_mismatch).
- No basic or preferred part exists for either function. The ruling requires the function, so the package adds two unique extended parts.
- Candidate checked and not used: Infineon BSC014N06NS (C113391, 1.45 mΩ max, `docs/part-facts/C113391.md`). One part gives
  ≈ 4.3 W at 40 A, so it would need two in parallel.

**Step 4 – heat at 40 °C.** See design-notes §8.6 for the tables. With the 4 mΩ default path + 1.05 mΩ (`--r-path 0.00505`),
`--p-other 2.3`: 4 × BTT6050 at 4 A Tj 101 → 102 °C; mix 2 × BTT6010 8 A + 2 × BTT6050 4 A 90/102 °C (unchanged); 4 × BTT6010
8 A 91 °C. The 4 A and ≈ 8 A ratings stand. Q30 itself: 0.22 W / 54 °C at 16 A, 0.51 W / 67 °C at 24 A, **1.78 W / 122 °C at
40 A** with ≥ 6 cm² of 2 oz drain copper (Tj max 175 °C). With a minimal pad it would reach ≈ 200 °C, which is why this is a PCB rule.
The drain is VS, which already has large copper. Stop condition (iii) does not apply: the heat budget shows the extra ≈ 1 mΩ is
harmless. The ARCHITECTURE figure "≤ 2 mΩ" is passed to `/system`.

**Step 6 – documents.** README reverse-battery bullet: the board now keeps a reversed battery of up to 30 V (D30 stand-off)
away from everything behind Q30, with no time limit; above ≈ 33 V D30 conducts and the fuse clears; if Q30 has failed
short, the old BTT6050 limits and the C31 warning apply; check the polarity anyway. Design-notes §8 (paragraph
updated), §8.1 (note on the new net), §4 (path note), new §8.6 (circuit, part facts, requirement checks, C32 sizing,
heat, reverse behaviour, **failure modes**, PCB rules, cost). STATUS.md: entry, PCB rule, pre-order footprint/stock check,
three "For /system" items.

**Failure-mode note (safety: F4 panel supply for navigation lights and bilge forced run is on VS).** Q30 short: no reverse
protection, as before. Q30 open: the board is dead, panel switches included (navigation lights, bilge forced run); the float
circuit stays independent (invariant 8). U30 or C32 failure without gate drive: body-diode conduction (≈ 16–24 W) overheats
Q30 until it fails short or open. A competent person reviews the blocker before installation.

- Heat budget before/after as above.

## Open points

- J10 keyed on pdm-4ch (decision 34) stays with `/system mechanical`. It is listed in STATUS "For /system".
- ARCHITECTURE root row and supply-path budget wording: listed for a sync sweep.
- Before ordering, check the footprints of Q30 and U30 against JLCPCB (in the STATUS pre-order list).
- The case where only the two J10 leads are swapped and the loads return to a different negative has not been analysed (design-notes §8.6).
- No prototype test was added (not asked).

## Suggestions (not done)

- A firmware-visible check of Q30. VBAT_SENSE (R60) measures VS, so a U30 failure (VS ≈ 1 V low, Q30 overheating) is not
  visible. One option is a second divider on VBAT_FL, or comparing VS against the expected battery voltage. That needs an
  ADC pin, so it is a `/system` question.
- A prototype test: connect a current-limited supply reversed (e.g. 24 V, 0.1 A limit) and confirm VS stays at 0 V. Then,
  powered normally, measure VBAT_FL − VS at a known load to confirm Q30 is enhanced (≈ mV, not ≈ 0.8 V).
- If FL30 is ever fitted, add a local 22–100 nF at U30 SOURCE (C30 would then be behind the ferrite).
