# pdm-4ch – CAN and USB pairs as fixed routes, rebuild (T-0210)

Date: 2026-11-11 · Role: pcb-fixer (unattended) · Board: `hardware/pdm-4ch/ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`

## Result

- The CAN H/L pair, the USB D+/D− pair and J3 CC1 are now hand-made, **locked** copper. Before this stage, both
  pairs were unlocked router output, and each build rebuilt them; CANH stayed split at FL1/R71.
- The board in the repo is the output of `hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/build_routing.sh 3 3` (Freerouting 2.5.0, 3
  parallel runs), with these routes as fixed input:
  - **0 DRC errors, 78 unconnected, parity 0**;
  - every VS/VBAT/OUT pour is whole (island count unchanged, ≥ 98 % of its area);
  - no signal is on In1, and the buck is untouched;
  - nothing crosses the CAN barrier or the antenna strip.
- No part was moved, and no zone outline changed. The schematic, `lib/`, the outline and the hole keep-outs are
  untouched.
- Unconnected went from 75 to 78. The two pairs and CC1 are now joined (7 items). The router's variance moved
  items in the analog block and the U1 strip, and the USB In2 run under U1 closes In2 southward there.

## What was drawn (details: design-notes §8.17)

**CAN** (U5 → FL1 → D9 → J4, with R71/R72 as choke bypass). H and L run side by side on F.Cu over the GND_CAN In1
plane.
- The pin order forces two crossings, and both stay on the CAN side:
  - CANL_XCVR goes under FL1 on In2.
  - CANH_BUS reaches J4.3 on B.Cu, through vias at 86.0/53.45 (from D9.1) and 85.1/58.05 (from R71.1).
- CANH_XCVR runs between the FL1 pad rows to R71.2.
- The CANL_XCVR tap leaves R72.2 through the 0.56 mm slot between the rotated 0402 pads of R71 and R72, with a
  0.15 mm neck (0.205 mm clearance).

**USB** (J3 → D5/D6 → U1.23/24). The ESD parts stay at the connector: D− runs through D6.2, and D+ tees off
0.5 mm before D5.2.
- **J3 end:** F.Cu only, because OUT1-B lies under J3 on B.Cu. CC1 leaves above the pads through a via to In2 and
  comes up beside R12.1.
- **Across the VS block:**
  - B.Cu x 17–46 at y 19.0/19.5, the free band between VS-B-front and VS-B-rear;
  - F.Cu x 46–58 at y 20.5/21.15, through the gap between U12/D201 and U13;
  - In2 x 57.5–70, where In2 is the GND plane, to U1's fan-out vias.
- Two fixed GND ties serve the J3 parts the pair walls in.

## Findings

- **MAJOR (blocked, two-way): the merge gate rejects edits to the build scripts.**
  - The brief puts hand routing into `hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/fixed_routes.py`, and decision 163 says routing is
    a build from those scripts.
  - The pcb-fixer gate (`tools/orchestrate.py` `fix_allowed`) admits only `.kicad_pcb`, `.kicad_pro`, STATUS.md
    and design-notes. The scripts became tracked in T-0209, so `fix_check` now says "outside the package scope".
  - I reverted both scripts and kept the board. Its pair copper is locked, and the build keeps locked copper as
    fixed copper, so later builds keep it.
  - The script patch is below. It needs a package whose scope covers `hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/` (or a gate change
    in a `/system` session) to land it.
- **MAJOR (fixed): a via under J3 cuts OUT1-B.** OUT1-B covers B.Cu at y 8.9–11.6 under J3.
  - A first USB version dropped D− to In2 next to A5, and the pour check rejected all runs (OUT1-B −2.5 %).
  - Later, the build's GND island tie-in put a via there (−2.6 %).
  - Fix: the J3 end is F.Cu only, and a fixed GND tie at 20.55/12.25 sits below OUT1-B.
- **MINOR (fixed): router In2 legs under U1 blocked IO4.** Moving the pair's In2 vias from x 59–60 to 57.5/58.4
  freed IO4's B.Cu lane at x 60.35.
- **MINOR (rule): fixed routes stay on the board locked.** `prep` removes only unlocked copper. When a fixed route
  moves, clear its nets first with `kpcb delete --net` (done here for USB_DN/DP). This works only when the patch
  defines the whole net.
- **MINOR: some footprint pads are rotated, and `GetSize()` does not show it.** R71/R72 and F2 are rotated, so
  their pad extents differ from `GetSize()`. Check against DRC before a 4–5 min build. A quick check was used
  each time: fixed routes on a copy of the board, refill, DRC, and only errors against unlocked router copper may
  remain.

## Left for the next stage (78 unconnected)

  U12-IS and U14-IS. The USB lane shows a way: B.Cu y 19–21 west of x 46.5, and the F.Cu gap between U12 and U13.
- U1 strip legs: IO6/7/8/11/17/18/39/40, SW1–4_IO, SW_SENSE, SW_SENSE_IO and MOD_ID. In2 is closed southward under
  U1 by the USB run at y 18.95/19.4, so these need B.Cu lanes.
- Analog block: Q3–Q6 links, R3/R45, R4, VBAT_SENSE, SWIN1–4, SW1/SW2/SW4 and LED_K3.
- VBUS (Net-(F2-Pad1)) at J3: A4–A9 and F2.1. Its via must not sit in OUT1-B, so it needs a fixed In2 route.
- D3-A, one GND island, one VS item (R60).

## Checks

- `kpcb drc`: 0 errors, 425 warnings, 78 unconnected, parity 0. No bottom SMT, nothing in a hole keep-out, nothing
  parked.
- Build pour check: whole, with 9 stitching vias added.
- `python3 tools/fix_check.py --scope pdm-4ch`: **OK – passes the merge gates**, after the scripts were reverted.

Safety: no power copper changed. The two USB vias at x 46.15 make clearance holes in VS-In2 and sit at the
OUT3-F/VS-B spine edges, and the pour check confirms every pour is whole. A competent person reviews the board
before installation, in particular the battery path, the VS copper and the outputs.
