## Packages

**P1 – pdm-4ch: trim the VS-In2 east edge for a pocket exit, then continue routing (T-0218, pcb-fixer).**

The problem comes from T-0259 (`docs/reviews/2026-11-13-fix-pdm-4ch-T-0259.md`, MAJOR finding). Five pocket nets
cannot reach U1: SW_IN1, SW_IN4, IO2, IO5 and DEN. The reason is that the In2 corridor ends at x 55.0, which is the
1.5 mm band inside the VS-In2 east edge. A via at x ≤ 55 lands in the B.Cu VS spine.

The owner ruled on this. The ruling is to pull the VS-In2 east edge back by about 2–3 mm at y 5–20. That makes room
for one In2 → B.Cu via column at x 55–57. Nothing else changes:
- the VS-B spine, the other VS outlines and the 16 A path;
- the VS-In2 limits: one island, at least 1012.1 mm², all 40 thermal vias inside;
- the 1.5 mm corridor band, now measured from the new edge.

The fixer then carries on with T-0218 under Q-0073 (decision 189), taking the two-way steps the ruling names:
- the ch2/ch3 IN/DEN links;
- U1.28;
- the DEN via off R402.1. T-0266 has already done this one (decision 193), so it only needs to be checked.

The fixer stops only when a stage gains nothing, or when a step needs another one-way door.

The trim costs about 30–45 mm² out of the current 1156.4 mm², which leaves a wide margin to 1012.1 mm². The heat
budget at 40 °C is rerun anyway (decision 9).

## Deferred

None.

## Decisions

There is one row, from Q-0074. It **amends decision 189**, which held routing to "no corridor notch". The new rule
allows one trim of the VS-In2 east edge at y 5–20 for a single In2 → B.Cu via column. The other limits from
decisions 172, 178 and 181 stay as they are.
