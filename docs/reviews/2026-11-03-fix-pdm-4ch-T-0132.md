## T-0129 – design-notes 8.6 (b) avalanche correction: BLOCKED (stop condition of the brief)

The arithmetic for IPT007N06N (it stays). C = 100 µF + 4 × 2.2 µF + 4 × 100 nF ≈ 109.2 µF. E = C·(VS + 48.4 − Vav)·Vav:
- At VS 29 V: 114 mJ at Vav 60 V and 82 mJ at 66 V. That compares with ½CV² = 46 mJ.
- VS sags to Vav − 48.4 = 11.6–17.6 V.
- 12 V system (VS 14.6 V): 20 mJ at Vav 60 V; none at 66 V.

The brief's numbers check out.

The problem: new facts in `docs/part-facts/C122552.md` (added through `part_facts.py --more`).
- The EAS of 1100 mJ is for **Tj(start) = 25 °C**.
- The datasheet has no EAS-vs-Tj curve and **no repetitive avalanche rating**.
- Diagram 13 (IAS vs tAV) at Tj(start) 125 °C holds 150 A for only ≈ 21 µs, which is ≈ 100 mJ triangular. At 25 °C the same curve gives ≈ 880 mJ. V(BR)DSS rises to ≈ 63 V at 125 °C.

At the 40 A input rating Q30 runs at Tj ≈ 122 °C, so a transient at that moment is a hot-start avalanche. I simulated the C31 discharge, with and without a 40 A load, for ESR 30–300 mΩ (`$CLAUDE_JOB_DIR/scripts/av_margin.py`):
- At a 125 °C start, the Diagram 13 time limit is only **≈ 1.0–1.9×** the pulse length for ESR 50–300 mΩ (≈ 3× at 30 mΩ).
- At a 100 °C start it is ≈ 2–7×; at 25 °C it is ≥ 9×.

C31's ESR has no lower bound in its facts file. The rating is therefore **not clearly above** the absorbed energy at the hot corner. The brief says to stop and ask here, because the question is now a safety one for the panel supply on VS. If Q30 fails short, reverse protection is lost; if it fails open, the board is dead, including the navigation-light and bilge forced-run panel switches.

§8.6 (b) is left word for word as it was; the pdm-4ch STATUS.md says it is pending.
