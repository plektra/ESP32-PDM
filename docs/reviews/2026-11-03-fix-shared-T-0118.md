# Fix report T-0118 (shared): PTC interrupt rating – C70113 on F1–F4, 0.2 Ω series resistor on F1/F3/F4

Date: 2026-11-03 · Scope: shared (`power-usb`, `can`, `analog_sw`, changed the same way on both boards) · Item: T-0018 (owner ruling Q-0060, decision 81)

## What changed and why

Owner ruling Q-0060: option (1). A 0.2 Ω 2 W alloy 2512 FOJAN FRM252WJR200TK (C7420022) goes in series with F1, F3 and F4. It is accepted on its 5 s / 50 J overload rating. F1–F4 become C70113. A 30 V hard-short test on the prototype is mandatory before installation.

1. **Part facts** (cached, via `tools/part_facts.py`):
   - C70113 mSMD050-60V: 60 V, I_max 100 A, I_hold 0.50 A, R_min 0.150 Ω, R1max 1.0 Ω, 1812, 1.78 × 3.2 mm pads.
   - C7420022: 0.2 Ω ± 5 %, 2 W at 70 °C, alloy 2512, 5 × P for 5 s (10 W, ≈ 50 J).

   Both pass. Both were in stock on 2026-11-03 (70 k and 53 k).
2. **F1, F3, F4:** value changed to mSMD050-60V and LCSC to C70113. The footprint stays `Fuse:Fuse_1812_4532Metric`.
3. **New series resistors:** R54, R55 and R56, each 200m, C7420022, `Resistor_SMD:R_2512_6332Metric`. The refs are the next numbers free on both boards. Each sits on the protected side of its PTC:
   - F1.2 → R54 → D1.2/D2.2
   - F3.1 → R55 → D10.2/FB2.2
   - F4.2 → R56 → D55 anode

   How they were placed: each PTC moved 7.62 mm towards its supply, and the resistor took its old position.
   - F1 now touches the Vin hierarchical label directly.
   - For F3, the wire to J4.2 was rerouted to x = 167.64.
   - For F4, the VS power symbol moved with the PTC.

   The pdm-hc2 sheets are byte copies of the pdm-4ch sheets with only the project name swapped, so the new objects have the same UUIDs on both boards.
4. **F2:** changed to C70113, and the footprint from `Fuse_0805_2012Metric` to `Fuse_1812_4532Metric`. F2 gets no series resistor.
5. **Checks** (written into design-notes §8.3):
   - (a) **F4 / switch path:** F4 + R56 is 1.2 Ω, less than the old F4 alone (1.4 Ω). At 60 mA that is 72 mV at SWIN and ≈ 23 mV at IN. Even if all 23 mV comes off the thinnest case, IN is ≈ 2.03 V, above the 2.0 V minimum. **Pass.**
   - (b) **F3 / L78M05:** with NET-S at its 9 V minimum, the L78M05 input is ≈ 8.48 V against the 7 V it needs. **Pass.**
   - (c) **F1 / buck:** the drop is 0.22 V at 0.18 A (0.60 V at I_hold). The buck still sees ≥ 9.5 V at a 10.5 V battery. **Pass.**
   - (d) **USB-only:** at the 0.40 A peak the buck input is 3.74 V with typical D3 (3.80 V with the old F2) and 3.53 V with D3 at its 0.70 V limit (3.59 V before). With either part it is below the 4 V minimum, so USB-only power did not reach the minimum before either. Against the falling UVLO the result is the same in every corner. The STOP condition is therefore not met, and F2 was swapped. The new part costs 60 mV (≈ 90 mV if the load is taken as constant power).
6. **Documents:**
   - design-notes §8.3: new T-0118 block with the parts table, checks (a)–(d), heat, cost, the pre-order footprint checks, the mandatory 30 V short test and the failure-mode note. The F2 row in the L3 swap table now notes the swap.
   - design-notes §3 heat line and §5 J5 table: F4 now named as C70113 + R56 (1.2 Ω).
   - Both STATUS files:
     - new T-0118 entry;
     - the "Blocked: PTC interrupt rating" paragraph replaced by the result;
     - pre-order line replaced by the footprint and stock checks;
     - "For /system" line replaced by the result;
     - the mandatory test added to the prototype test list. pdm-hc2 had no such section, so I added "## Prototype test list" with only this test.

**Failure mode (safety):** an overstressed series resistor fails open.
- **F4/R56:** the panel supply is off for good. The panel switches stop working (bilge forced run, navigation lights), and so do the panel LEDs. NMEA 2000 bus control keeps working. Unlike a tripped PTC, it does not reset.
- **F1/R54:** the board loses its battery feed.
- **F3/R55:** the CAN transceiver side loses power.

**A competent person must review F1–F4 and R54–R56 before installation.**

**Heat budget:** no output changed, and the new losses are in milliwatts (R54 6.5 mW, R55 1.2 mW, R56 ≤ 0.7 mW). The tool was not rerun.

  - pdm-4ch: ERC 0 errors / 5 warnings; 207 parts (+3), 61 unique (+0).

- Shared sheets: equal on both boards apart from the project name.

## Open points

- Pre-order:
  - Check `Fuse_1812_4532Metric` against the JLCPCB footprint of C70113. The KiCad pads are 1.125 × 3.4 mm at 4.275 mm pitch; the datasheet gives 1.78 × 3.2 mm with a 3.2 mm gap.
  - Check `R_2512_6332Metric` against the JLCPCB footprint of C7420022.
- Mandatory prototype test: 30 V hard short at J5.5, behind F1 and behind F3.
- The schematic placement was scripted; tidy it in KiCad.

## Suggestions (not done)

- The USB-only margin at peak current was already short with the old F2. A lower-V_F D3, or measuring the buck input on the prototype in USB-only operation with WiFi transmitting, would settle it. USB is a service port only.
