## Calculation (design-notes §8.3)

Corner 1/(2π · 1 MΩ · 1 nF) ≈ 159 Hz (was ≈ 34 Hz with 4.7 nF). |Z_C| at 100 MHz ≈ 1.6 Ω (was ≈ 0.3 Ω), ESL ignored.
At 50 Hz |Z_C| ≈ 3.2 MΩ, so the shore-PE loop still sees ≈ 0.76 MΩ. The 2 kV rating is higher than the old 1 kV.
