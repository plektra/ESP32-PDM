### T-0030 (Q-0023) – CA-IS3050G check, then swap: **done (swapped)**

Package and pinout:
- Pinout (Table 6-1): 1 VCC1, 2 TXD, 3 RXD, 4 GND1, **5 GND2, 6 CANL, 7 CANH**, 8 VCC2. The ISO1044 has 5 CANL, 6 CANH,
  7 GND2, so the parts are not pin-compatible.

- KiCad `Package_SO:SOIC-8_7.5x5.85mm_P1.27mm` is compared with the drawing in design-notes §8.5 and accepted. Its row
  pitch is 10.65 mm against Chipanalog's 10.90 mm, and the pads cover the feet.

JLCPCB C528763: extended, stock 5970, EasyEDA footprint present, 0.917 USD at 10+ against 1.62 USD → −0.71 USD/board.

**LEN:** the datasheet has no 50 %-duty supply current. The worst case (73 + 12) / 2 + 6.8 mA = 49.3 mA → still
LEN 1, but with only 0.7 mA margin (all values at their maxima). Continuous dominant is 79.8 mA → LEN 2.

Change:
- New library symbol `ESP32-PDM:CA-IS3050G`: the ISO1044BD graphic with pins renumbered by name, so no wire moved.
- U5 lib_id, Value, Footprint, Datasheet, Description and LCSC C528763 updated.
- The netlist shows U5.5 on GND_CAN, U5.6 on CANL and U5.7 on CANH.

### T-0010 (Q-0004) – barrier Y-cap and CMC footprints: **done**

**C70, DNP:** 1 nF/2 kV X7R 1206 from GND_CAN to GND, FH 1206B102K202NT C9196.
- Basic part, already fitted as C50–C53.
- Not Y2-certified. Both sides are SELV, so the barrier is functional isolation; noted in §8.5.

**FL1, DNP:** cjiang FACT4532-510-2P-T (C20624726, extended, 51 µH, 1 kΩ min at 10 MHz, 200 mA, AEC-Q200).
- Placed between the D9/J4 node and U5.
- Windings 1→2 / 4→3: bus side on pins 1/4, transceiver side on pins 2/3, so it acts in common mode.
- Footprint `Inductor_SMD:L_CommonModeChoke_Coilank_ACM4532`.

**R71/R72, fitted:** 0 Ω 0402 C17168 (basic, already fitted as R43), bridging each winding.

**Nets:** the old CANH/CANL nets are split into the new nets `/CAN/CANH_BUS`, `/CAN/CANH_XCVR`, `/CAN/CANL_BUS` and
`/CAN/CANL_XCVR`.

Layout rule 9 (barrier clearance, C70 the only crossing) is written into both STATUS files.

## Open points

- **FL1 pad numbering:** the cjiang drawing does not number the pads. Check them against the JLCPCB footprint of
  C20624726 before FL1 is ever fitted, because a swapped pair turns it into a differential-mode choke. This is in the
  pre-order checklist.
- **U5 footprint:** check the KiCad SOIC-8-WB footprint against the JLCPCB footprint of C528763 (pre-order checklist).
- **LEN 1 margin** is 0.7 mA at worst-case values. Measure the NET-S current on the first prototype.
- **R70 power-up glitch:** R70 overrides a floating or weakly pulled GPIO17. A glitch driven low by the pin itself would
  still reach the bus (but stops within the 2–8 ms timeout). Scope TXD at power-up on the prototype.
- **ARCHITECTURE `can` row** still names the ISO1044. This is listed under "For /system" in both STATUS files.
- The `can.kicad_sch` diff is large because the `ksch` serialiser reformats the file. The content change is only what is
  listed above.

## Suggestions (not done)

- If the scan asks for C70, consider a certified Y2 1206/1808 capacitor (fails open) instead of the X7R.
- `lib/ESP32-PDM.kicad_sym` still holds the unused `TPS7B91` symbol (U6 is now L78M05).
