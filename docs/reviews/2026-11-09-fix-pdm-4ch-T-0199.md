# pdm-4ch layout stage 3: logic half and remaining parts (T-0199, 2026-11-09, pcb-fixer, unattended)

This stage places every part that was still parked: none is left off the board. With the board now 100 × 75 mm
(decision 138), the space problem from the rejected first attempt is gone. That attempt's report is
`2026-11-09-fix-pdm-4ch-T-0199-rejected.md`; its U1 position, channel-2 move and buck order are reused here.

The full layout reasoning is in design-notes §8.11. Coordinates are in mm from the top-left corner: x = 0 is the power
edge, y = 0 the front, y = 75 the rail side.

## What was placed or moved

- **Signal edge:**
  - J5 and its protection cluster moved 7.4 mm toward the front (J5 pin 1 at y 46.1).
  - J4 sits at 93.3/63.9, rot 90, pin 1 at the rail end.
  - Both fit between the H2 and H4 keep-outs, with a 0.6 mm courtyard gap.
  - D52 moved 1.3 mm outward, clear of U1.
  - The SW_SUPPLY chain is D54/C54 → D55 (68.5/30.9) → R56 (upright, 63.5/33.6) → F4. F4 is unchanged.
- **U1** at 69.25/9.75, antenna on the front edge in the signal-end corner. The keep-out strip x 46.5–92, y 0–4.5 is
  clear.
  - Channel 2 moved 2.6 mm toward the rail.
  - Its R204/D202/R203/R207/D201 now form a column beside the module.
- **Front corner:** D8/R67 and D7/R59 (visible), C6/C9/R15 (EN), C7/R16 (IO0), and C50/C52/C53 with R25/R28.
  C51/R22 sit in the gap of the even TVS column.
- **Below U1:** C8/C10, R60/R61/C60, R62/C61, R29/R42, R58, and Q3–Q6 with R17/R18/R20/R21/R23/R24/R26/R27/R50–R53/R19.
- **USB:** J3 at 15.2/4.2, rot 180, at the front edge in the power-end corner. D4–D6, R11/R12, R13/C18/R14, F2,
  FB1, C4 and C5 are around it. D3 sits at the buck's C1.
- **Buck along the rail edge.** The path is VS (D31 drain pad) → F1 → R54 → D1/D2 → JP1 ‖ L2 (DNP, room for its
  body) → C1/C19/U3 → L1 → C3.
  - C1/C19/U3 are upright, VIN pads in one row: an input loop of about 3 mm with no via.
  - FINDING (MEDIUM, fixed): the first pass had C19, L1 and the F1…L2 chain turned against the flow. Checked from the pad
    positions and rotated.
  - C22/R73 (DNP) sit next to C1. R44/C20, C2/R43 and R9/R10 sit at their U3 pins.
- **CAN barrier block (PCB rule 9):**
  - U5 at 71.5/56.5: logic pins x 66.2, bus pins x 76.8.
  - The GND_CAN island (x ≥ 78.1, y ≥ 51.4) holds D9, FL1, R71/R72, F3, R55, D10, FB2, D11, C13, U6 (DPAK at 86.7/71.4),
    C11 and C12.
  - C70 is the only part across the parts-free band x 65–78.1.
  - R70 is on the logic side.
- **Switch inputs:** D12–D15, D20–D24, R30–R33, R38–R41, R1/R2/R5/R6, R3–R8 and R45–R48 sit on the logic side and along
  the rail strip (x 42–65, y 52–75). R49/R57/C21 sit at the SW_SUPPLY end, next to R56.
- **J6** stands upright at 55.2/55.1, pin 1 (GND) at the top.
- **Buttons:** SW_RESET1 at 39/56, SW_BOOT1 at 5/59.
- **U30** is at its reserved spot, 42.5/39.3, rot 180 (TI_DDF0008A). It gives no DRC error now.

## Open points for the next stages

- **FINDING (LOW): J6 is 35–55 mm from U1.**
  - There is no 21 mm free strip near the module.
  - Routing keeps TXD0/RXD0/IO35–37 away from the switch inputs and the CAN pair.
  - A later rearrangement could bring J6 closer.
- **FINDING (LOW): only 0.6 mm between the J5 and J4 courtyards.** That is the geometry of decision 138.
  - Silkscreen: add "PANEL" and "CAN" and the pin-1 marks (decision 127 item 3).
  - Check the plug fit on the first boards (prototype list).
- **Copper:**
  - C8/C10 → U1 pin 3 as a short wide trace (about 12 mm).
  - The USB pair through the corridor between the switch rows.
  - U6's output to C11/C12/U5 pin 8 on the island (about 20 mm).
  - The GND and GND_CAN pours at least 8 mm apart across U5, with C70 the only bridge.
  - The antenna strip free of copper on both layers.
  - At least 1 mm from VS/VBAT_FL (F1, D31) to the switch-input parts.
  - VS → F1 → R54 at least 0.6 mm wide.
- **Safety:** no battery-path, Q30/D31 or output part moved. F1/R54/D1/D2 feed only the buck. A competent person
  reviews the board before installation.
