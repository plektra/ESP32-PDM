# pdm-4ch – layout stage 6b: room in the U1 strip and U1 fan-out (T-0208)

Date: 2026-11-10 · Role: pcb-fixer (unattended) · Board: `hardware/pdm-4ch/ESP32-PDM-4CH.kicad_pcb`

## Result

The stage is done. The board has 0 DRC errors, parity 0, and every power pour is whole.
- **Room:** the U1 corridor has more room, and the six J6 service lines are routed on In2.
- **Fan-out:** every used U1 pin has a stub, and all but IO3 have a via.
- **Unconnected:** 222 → 216.
- **Not touched:** no part was moved. The schematic, `lib/`, the outline, the hole keep-outs, the antenna strip and
  the CAN barrier are unchanged.

All edits were made with `tools/kpcb.py` (zone, delete, via, track, fill). No autorouter was used: the geometry was
planned by hand and applied by script.

## The bottleneck

Every net from U1 to the south half of the board has to pass the corridor x 59.5–67.5, y 34.5–52.8:
- the destinations are J6, D12–D15, R30–R33, U5 and the switch inputs;
- the VS copper (Q30 drain, C31) lies to the left of the corridor, and the CAN barrier to the right;
- on F.Cu the corridor is closed by C31 and C14–C17, so only In2 and B.Cu are left.

A netlist count gives 27 nets that cross it:
- +3V3 and EN;
- IO6, IO7, IO8, IO11, IO17 and IO18;
- the six J6 lines;
- SW1–4_IO, SW1–4 and SWIN1–4;
- SW_SENSE.

## What the stage did

1. **VS narrowed (two-way, the third option in the brief):**
   - VS-B-spine now ends at x 59.5 instead of x 61.0 between y 35 and 52.8; VS-In2 does the same between y 34.5 and
     50.5.
   - This side area holds no VS pad or via. The drain vias are at x 57.4, and the 16 A path runs north into the
     spine.
   - Copper: −27 mm² on B.Cu (549 → 522 mm²) and −24 mm² on In2 (1218 → 1194 mm²).
   - The ≥ 2 cm² drain rule is met on F.Cu, which is unchanged.
   - Heat budget at 40 °C: unchanged, 4 × 4 A gives Tj 101 °C.
   - Both zones keep their priority, clearance and solid pads.
2. **Stitching vias:**
   - Removed from the corridor: (65.54, 34.0), (63.04, 43.0), (63.04, 51.0).
   - Removed from the fan-out lanes: (77.9, 21.2), (60.35, 12.87), (60.35, 17.92).
   - Re-added: GND vias at (59.9, 10.2) and (59.9, 15.6).
3. **U1 fan-out:** 0.2 mm F.Cu stubs to 0.6/0.3 mm tented vias.
   - Most vias sit under the module, staggered at ≥ 1.2 mm centre to centre, so that the In1 plane keeps webs:
     - left column at x 63.4/64.6 (+3V3, IO0, MOD_ID, IO6, IO7, IO8, VBAT_SENSE);
     - bottom row at y 18.0/16.8 (IO13, SW1–4_IO, IO17, IO18, USB_DN/DP), plus DEN at (72.5, 17.75);
     - right column at x 75.0/73.8 (RXD0, TXD0, IO35–IO42).
   - Why under the module: right of U1 there is only 1.3 mm to C50/C52/C53. A via column at the 0.85 mm pin pitch
     would also slot the In1 plane.
   - Outward vias, toward the destination:
     - IO2, IO4, IO5, NTC and IO11 at x 61.0/59.95;
     - SW_SENSE_IO at (63.0, 20.35);
     - EN at (77.25, 6.35).
   - IO3 has a stub only, because its parts R204/D202 sit beside U1 on F.Cu.
4. **J6 on In2 (the first option in the brief):**
   - IO35, IO36, IO37 and IO42 run on In2 the whole way. Each goes east of the via columns, takes one 45° run under
     the analog block, goes down the corridor at x 60.2–61.7, and takes a second 45° run to J6.
   - TXD0 and RXD0 run down In2 lanes 62.2/62.7, then hop to B.Cu for the last 5 mm, through vias at (60.9, 58.3)
     and (60.75, 59.75). RXD0 is above TXD0 on U1 but below it on J6, so no single-layer route avoids a crossing.
   - All tracks are 0.2 mm with ≥ 0.2 mm clearance and ≥ 0.6 mm from VS-In2. Nothing is on In1, in the antenna
     strip or across the barrier.

The analog block was not spread. With J6 on In2 and VS narrowed, the corridor has room for the remaining corridor
nets: about 18 lanes on B.Cu (x 60.6–67.4) and about 12 on In2 (x 63.0–67.4), against 21 nets. Parts can still be
moved later if the routing stage needs it.

## Findings and open points for the next stage

- **MAJOR – nine nets with no route to the west half.** IO0 (SW_BOOT1), EN (SW_RESET1), DEN (R102/R402),
  USB_DN/DP (J3), IO2 (U11 IS), IO5 (U14 IS), NTC (TH1) and +3V3 (D102/D402) have to reach the west half.
  - Between y 1 and 59 they cannot cross the VS block on In2 (VS-In2) or on B.Cu (VS spine x 45.5–55).
  - On F.Cu they meet U12/U13, the OUT2/OUT3 pours and the antenna strip.
  - If they all take the corridor and then the bottom edge, the corridor has about 30 nets for about 30 lanes.
  - The next stage should first look for an F.Cu path between U12 and U13. It should also consider a short B.Cu
    gap in the VS spine (a two-way door, after the VS current check).
- **Route from the fan-out vias:**
  - IO38/IO39 to R18/R20 and IO40/IO41 to R23/R26 (B.Cu);
  - MOD_ID, VBAT_SENSE, NTC and SW_SENSE_IO into the analog block;
  - the corridor nets down to D12–D15, R30–R33 and U5.
  - The In2 J6 band runs diagonally under the analog block (x + y ≈ 96–100). Vias at R42, R58, R18 and R20 go on
    either side of it.
- **Still open from T-0207:** D3-A (FB1–D4–D3), the panel/switch nets and +3V3 distribution.

A competent person reviews the board, in particular the battery path, the VS copper and the outputs, before
installation.
