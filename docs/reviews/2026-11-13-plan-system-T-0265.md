# Plan T-0265 (2026-11-13, unattended)

There is one approved item, T-0264 (pdm-4ch, MINOR, two-way). It is a copper-only fix, so it goes into one pcb-fixer
package. Nothing is deferred. No decision rows are needed because no owner ruling came with the item.

## Packages

**P1 – pdm-4ch: move the DEN via off pad R402.1 (T-0264, pcb-fixer).** The finding is M1 in
`docs/reviews/2026-11-13-validate-pdm-4ch-4.md`. The last DEN leg (decision 192, T-0259) ends in a 0.6/0.3 mm via
that sits on the 0402 pad R402.1. The drill is open, so solder can wick into it during reflow. The result can be a
starved joint or a tombstone, and ch4 then loses its DEN link (current sense and diagnosis).

The copper comes from the build, not from a hand edit (decision 163). It is in
`hardware/pdm-4ch/scripts/fixed_routes.py`, lines 1347–1348: a B.Cu stub ending at (37.60, 24.30) and `V('/DEN', 37.60,
24.30)`. The board shows the via at (37.55, 24.25). The fix moves that via so its edge is at least 0.5 mm from the pad.
It then runs a short F.Cu link from the via to R402.1 and rebuilds the board. No part, schematic or pour changes, and no
new footprint or option is added.

## Deferred

None.

## Decisions

None. T-0264 is a two-way agent decision, and the fixer records its own **auto** row.
