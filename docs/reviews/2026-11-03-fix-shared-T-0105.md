# Fix report T-0105 – panel switch loads 2 k + 2 k (scope shared)

Date: 2026-11-03 · Board-fixer, unattended · Item: T-0046 (validator report 2026-10-30, Q-0048 → decision 74)

## T-0046: R3/R4/R7/R8 → 2 k + 2 k 1206 basic in series – done

Owner ruling Q-0048: "(a) 2 k + 2 k 1206 basic (C17944) in series for R3/R4/R7/R8".

**Part facts** (`docs/part-facts/C17944.md`, new): UNI-ROYAL 1206W4F2001T5E, 2 kΩ ±1 %, 1206, basic. 0.25 W at 70 °C,
linear derating to 0 at 155 °C; max working voltage 200 V, but at 2 kΩ the RCWV is √(P·R) = 22.4 V; short-time overload
2.5 × RCWV = 55.9 V for 5 s (datasheet §9, test 4.13). No sub-5 s pulse curve is published.

**Schematic** (`analog_sw`, script `split_loads.py` with `tools/ksch.py`): R3/R4/R7/R8 Value 4.7k → 2k, LCSC
C17936 → C17944; new R45/R46/R47/R48 (2k, 1206, C17944) between each and GND; each GND symbol (#PWR054/059/064/069)
moved down one resistor length. New mid-point nets Net-(R3-Pad2), … Net-(R48-Pad1). Edited on pdm-4ch, then copied to
pdm-hc2 with only the `(project …)` name changed. R45–R48 were free on both boards.

**Ratings:** at SWIN 29.6 V (30 V behind D55): 7.4 mA, 14.8 V and 0.11 W per part (44 %, below the RCWV). At the
SMAJ30CA clamp (≈ 48 V): 24 V and 0.29 W per part for the surge's duration, inside the 5 s overload level of 55.9 V. Was
0.19 W / 0.49 W in one part.

**Design-notes §3 recheck:** the load sits in parallel with the divider, so the ON/OFF thresholds do not change. The extra
+0.4 mA per line at 10.5 V costs ≤ ≈ 6 mV at SWIN (F4, D55, 10 m of cable), ≤ ≈ 2 mV at IN. The thinnest case (battery
10.5 V via D55, 3V3 down) stays ≈ 2.05 V ≥ VIN(H) 2.0 V: the stop condition is not reached. Leakage across a contact
(dry-contact rule): about 2.4 mA is now needed to reach the ≈ 7.5 V EN threshold, up from 2.1 mA, so rejection is
slightly better.

**Heat (§4):** panel loads 0.75 → 0.88 W, p_other ≈ 2.2 → 2.3 W. The 4 A rating stands (see checks). The
`heat_budget.py` default stays at 2.2 W because a shared package may not edit `tools/`. It is listed for a sync package
in pdm-4ch STATUS "For /system".

**ARCHITECTURE:** `analog_sw` row "4.7 k load" → "2 × 2 k load". The panel-switch-supply budget is now ≈ 60 mA
(4 × 7.5 mA load + 4 × 2.6 mA divider + 4 × 5 mA LEDs), with decision 74 cross-referenced. This is more than the brief's
"about 55 mA" because the divider current is now counted, as in §4's D55 figure. F4 hold is unchanged. Invariants not
touched.

**Failure mode** (design-notes §5): if one half fails open, that line loses its load (no wetting current, no leakage
rejection) and the switch still works. If one half shorts, the line has a 2 k load and the other part is overloaded
(0.44 W) until it drifts or opens. The old single 4.7 k could short SWIN to GND and trip F4 for all panel lines; that
now needs a double fault. These inputs drive safety-relevant outputs (bilge forced run, navigation lights): **a
competent person must review them before installation.**

**STATUS:** both boards have a T-0105 entry and "Updated" is now T-0105. pdm-4ch: the "Blocked: T-0046" note and the
"For /system: T-0068/T-0046" item are removed, and one sync item (tool default) is added. pdm-hc2 had neither note.

Growth: +4 parts per board, +0 unique (C17936 leaves the BOM and C17944 replaces it; both basic, no loading fee).
