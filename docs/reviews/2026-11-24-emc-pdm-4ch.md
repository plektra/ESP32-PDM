# EMC review, scope pdm-4ch, layout (2026-11-24)

Re-check of the pdm-4ch PCB after decision 239 (C10 moved to U5 VCC1, all signal nets rerouted, power copper and
pours unchanged). The schematics did not change since the 2026-11-17 review (`git log` since that date shows only
`.kicad_pcb`, `scripts/`, `fab/`, silkscreen and title blocks), so findings E1–E25 of the earlier reports are not repeated.
This pass checks the new signal copper against the layout rules (`docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "PCB layout rules" 1–17) and the
three open items of `docs/reviews/2026-11-17-emc-pdm-4ch.md`.

Sources: `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` (invariant 4, layout rules), `docs/decisions.md` (219, 223, 239),
`hardware/pdm-4ch/STATUS.md`, `docs/reviews/2026-11-17-emc-pdm-4ch.md`. Method: the board file (copied to a scratch folder)
was loaded with KiCad's Python (`pcbnew`, read only); tracks, vias, pads and filled-zone outlines were dumped and distances
computed from the dump (outlines sampled every 0.25-0.3 mm, good to about 0.1 mm); `scripts/emc_report.py` was run on the
board. Nothing in the repository was changed except this report.

## Checked on the rerouted copper

- **Filters at J5 (rule 13, E22).** `emc_report.py`: R19/C50 0.8 mm, R22/C51 4.3 mm, R25/C52 0.8 mm, R28/C53 0.8 mm,
  R34-R37/C14-C17 1.5-1.6 mm; all within the 5 mm rule. E22 stays fixed after the reroute (it was 21-25 mm).
- **TVS flow-through (rule 14, E23).** Every J5, J4 and J3 line passes through its TVS pad: stub 0.0 mm, except USB D+ 0.5 mm,
  SWIN4 1.0 mm (exactly the limit) and SW_SUPPLY, which the tool flags with 3.7 mm "toward R49.1". The SW_SUPPLY flag is a
  tool artefact: the netlist order is J5.5 -> D54.2 (3.7 mm track) -> C54 -> D55 -> R56 -> F4 -> VS, and the R49 sense tap
  (10 k) leaves the net at D55.1 on In2, after C54, so nothing taps upstream of the clamp (known from STATUS, "SW_SUPPLY stub").
  CANH_BUS now reaches D9 pad 1 as a flow-through (J4.3 to D9.1 8.8 mm, CANL 6.8 mm; the 2 mm difference is common-mode and
  harmless). Clamp distances from the connectors: D16, D18, D51, D53, D54 1.6-3.7 mm; D17, D19, D50 (1.6 mm), D52 8.4-9.8 mm
  along cable-side copper before the clamp (the clamp still sees the pulse first, as in 2026-11-17).
- **Buck (rule 11, 16, E24).** SW is 0.5-0.6 mm F.Cu from U3.6 to L1/C2 with no via and no signal within 2.2 mm of any SW copper
  (nearest other-net copper: the SW snubber spur, +3V3, CB; IO0 5.3 mm on B.Cu). The IS, NTC and VBAT_SENSE nets are far from
  it. E24 unchanged: FB (R10 pad 1) 0.70 mm from the SW pad of DNP R44 on F.Cu (the build's own CHECK line). Open as before,
  waits for the T-0161 scan ("scope U3 FB and +3V3 at light load").
- **C10 / U5 (decision 239).** C10 pads (66.3, 52.17) GND and (66.3, 53.13) +3V3; the +3V3 path to U5.1 is about 2.4 mm of 0.3 mm
  copper; GND vias at 0.7 mm from the C10 pad (66.35, 51.45) and at 1.2 mm from U5.4 (65.02, 58.01); both return through the
  solid In1 plane beneath (0.21 mm). The loop is one plane-referenced leg of about 3 mm plus the pin spacing inside the package,
  against 33.6 mm before. Fine for a CA-IS3050G with 1 Mbit/s CAN, `UNVERIFIED` as a measured ripple.
- **CAN barrier (rule 6).** GND_CAN pour to GND pour: 4.0 mm on F.Cu, B.Cu, In1 and In2 (nearest points at the island's top
  edge); across the U5 band y 53.1-66.7 only C70 (DNP) has pads in x 67.3-75.7 (GND pad at 70.0, GND_CAN pad at 73.0, 0.5 mm
  GND-to-GND-track stubs both sides). No logic track or via enters the band; C10 (y 52.2-53.1) is outside it.
- **Antenna (rule 7).** No track, via or pad in x 45.85-92.5, y 0-4.5.
- **Creepage (rule 4).** Every track, via and pad of another net on F.Cu and B.Cu keeps at least 1.0 mm from the VS, VBAT_IN and
  VBAT_FL pours (script on the filled-zone outlines, 0 hits). The OUT pours are not under rule 4 (0.4-0.6 mm at the PROFET pads
  and some signal tracks beside them, as the pitch of the package forces). Zone-to-zone gaps (GND fill to VBAT_FL at x = 40.25,
  0.3 mm) were not re-measured; accepted in 2026-11-17.
- **Raw SWIN1 net, observation, not a finding.** R3/R45 (4 k load) sit at (58.4, 59.9), so the cable-side SWIN1 copper (after
  D16 and R34's input) is about 53 mm long (39 mm F.Cu, 14 mm In2) and runs through the logic area, 0.2 mm beside /SW1 and /IO6
  (channel 1 drive, 3.3 mm), /SW2 (2.3 mm) and /IO17 (CAN TX, 8.5 mm on In2). Estimate: about 0.1 pF coupling onto a 3.3 V node
  gives 0.2 V at a 24 V edge and about -30 dB at 160 MHz, below the PROFET and ESP32 thresholds (`UNVERIFIED`, estimate only).
  A closing switch turns the channel on anyway. Route R3/R45 nearer J5 only if a later reroute has room; no failure mechanism
  at the present numbers.
- **Output edges.** Unchanged: BTT6050 0.3/0.8/1.4 V/us (2026-11-17), outputs and OUT pours not rerouted.

## Layout rules

All rules of `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md` "PCB layout rules" 1-17 stand. Additions from this pass: none. One reminder for the rule 17
re-check after every reroute: besides `emc_report.py`, look at where the load of a raw cable-side net (R3/R45, R49) ends up, since
the tool measures filters and clamps, not the length of the unfiltered node behind them.

## Installation guidance

Unchanged from 2026-11-17 and the README: screened panel cable with the drain at the PDM end on J5.10; twisted battery pair to
J10; load return outside the board, freewheel diode or TVS at inductive loads (D103-D403 footprints stay empty); NMEA 2000
drop cable on J4 without tying J4 pin 1 to the boat negative; coat the board in a salt-air enclosure.
