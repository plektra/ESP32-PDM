# Fix report T-0104 – PTC interrupt rating: 100 A PTC + series resistor, F2 swap (2026-11-03, unattended)

Scope: shared (`power-usb` F1/F2, `can` F3, `analog_sw` F4). Item: T-0018 (Q-0047, decision 73).
**Result: blocked at the ruling's own stop point. The series resistor's pulse check cannot be shown to pass with a basic or
cheap part, so nothing was changed in the schematics.** Documents only: design-notes §8.3, both STATUS files, eight new
part-facts files.

## T-0018 – step by step

### 1. PTC datasheet check: passed (both candidates)
| | TECHFUSE mSMD050-60V C70113 | JDT ASMD1812-050-60V C427396 |
|---|---|---|
| V_max / I_max | 60 V / **100 A** (p. 1) | 60 V / **100 A** (p. 2/7) |
| I_hold 25 / 40 / 60 °C | 0.50 / 0.45 / 0.35 A | 0.50 / 0.45 / 0.35 A |
| I_trip 25 °C | 1.00 A | 1.00 A |
| R_min / R1max | 0.150 / **1.000 Ω** | 0.150 / 1.400 Ω |
| Trip time | ≤ 0.15 s at 8 A; curve stops at ≈ 10 A | same |
| Body | 1812, 4.37–4.73 × 3.07–3.41 mm (same as 1812L050/60GR) | same |
| JLCPCB | extended, 0.088 USD, stock 70 k | extended, 0.0725 USD, stock 14.8 k |

Both fit the present `Fuse_1812_4532Metric` body. The datasheet pads (1.78 × 3.2 mm, 3.2 mm gap) are larger than the KiCad
pads (1.125 × 3.4 mm, 3.15 mm gap), so this goes on the pre-order footprint check. For the later swap I would pick
**C70113** for all four positions, not the cheaper JDT. The reason is F2 on USB VBUS (≈ 0.40 A peak): R1max 1.0 Ω gives a
0.40 V worst-case drop, against 0.56 V for JDT and 0.34 V for the present SMD0805B050TF. That costs +0.016 USD per position.
Both pass I_hold for F2 (0.35 A at 60 °C against 0.33 A for the present part).

### 2. Series resistor: sizing passes, pulse energy **not shown to pass**
- R_s ≥ 30 V / 100 A − 0.150 Ω = 0.150 Ω. 0.150 Ω leaves zero margin, so the choice would be 0.20 Ω (E24): I_pk = 86 A
  (0.22 Ω: 81 A).
- Continuous: F1 ≈ 0.18 A → 6.5 mW / 36 mV (at I_hold 0.5 A: 50 mW / 0.1 V); F3 ≤ 77 mA → 1.2 mW; F4 ≈ 55 mA → 0.6 mW / 11 mV.
- SWIN margin (design-notes §3): +11 mV at SWIN ≈ 3.5 mV at IN, so the thinnest case stays ≈ 2.05 V ≥ 2.0 V. **Pass.**
- Pulse: no PTC datasheet gives a trip time at 80–100 A. Bound: I²t ≤ 8² × 0.15 = 9.6 A²s (the tabulated max point; adiabatic
  I²t can only fall at higher current). Typical from the curve: 1–2 A²s. Energy in 0.20 Ω: **≤ 1.9 J** (typ. 0.2–0.4 J) at
  **≈ 1.5 kW peak** for ≈ 1.3 ms (typ. 0.15–0.3 ms).
- Candidates (all part-facts checked): basic UNI-ROYAL 0.1 Ω 1206 **C25334** (the only basic part in 0.1–0.33 Ω), FOJAN
  FRL2512 0.22 Ω **C2960770**, Yageo RL1206 0.22 Ω **C327006**, alloy FOJAN FRM2512 0.2 Ω 2 W **C7420022** (0.028 USD), alloy
  UNI-ROYAL LR12 0.2 Ω 3 W **C19633830**. **None publishes a 0.1–10 ms pulse rating.** They give only 5 s short-time overload
  tests (1.6–12 W; 50–60 J for the alloy parts). A 5 s, 10 W test says nothing about a 1.5 kW, about 1 ms pulse, because the
  heat cannot spread in that time. A rough thermal estimate for thick film gives several hundred kelvin at the bound. So I do
  not count this as a pass, and the ruling says "if no basic/cheap part passes, return to the owner".

FINDING (MAJOR): the ruled fix stops at the series resistor. No basic or cheap 0.15–0.22 Ω resistor at JLCPCB has a datasheet
pulse rating for the ≈ 1.5 kW / ≈ 1 ms pulse until the PTC trips. Both 100 A PTCs pass their own check.

### 3.–4. Edits and F2 swap: not done
Following the ruling and the brief's stop rule, F1/F3/F4 stay 1812L050/60GR (C19078719) and F2 stays SMD0805B050TF (C269104).
I did not swap only the PTC. The ruling couples the PTC swap with the resistor, and the F2 swap comes after both.
A 100 A PTC alone (188 A prospective) would still not meet I_max. This is cheap to do in the follow-up package once the
owner answers (value + LCSC only on F1/F3/F4; F2 footprint 0805 → `Fuse_1812_4532Metric`; there is no PCB yet).

### Failure mode / safety
Unchanged from T-0065: a hard short at J5.5 (F4, VS-fed) or behind F1/F3 can exceed the PTC's I_max (40 A), and the PPTC may
crack, arc or char instead of tripping. With the ruled fix, an overstressed series resistor fails **open**. For F4 that equals
a tripped F4 (panel switches disabled, bus control works, decision 17) but does not reset, and a brief arc at 30 V cannot be
excluded from the datasheets. **F4 feeds the panel switches (bilge forced run, navigation lights): a competent person must
review F1/F3/F4 before installation.**

## Open points / question for the owner
Which way for the series resistor? Options (cost per board, 3 positions):
1. **Alloy 2512 shunt FOJAN FRM252WJR200TK C7420022** (0.2 Ω 2 W, 0.028 USD, extended → one loading fee), accepted on its
   5 s / 50 J overload rating without a ms pulse rating, **plus a prototype test**: hard short at J5.5 from a 30 V battery
   supply, scope the current, inspect the resistor and the PTC afterwards. This is my recommendation.
2. Two basic C25334 0.1 Ω 1206 in series per PTC (no fee, +6 parts), also without a pulse rating and with less thermal mass.
3. A part with a published pulse curve (anti-surge or metal-strip from Vishay, Yageo, Panasonic or Bourns). It is probably
   not stocked at JLCPCB in 0.2 Ω and costs more.
4. Option (b) of Q-0047: 100 A PTC only, and document the residual risk.

## Suggestions (not done)
- Pre-order: check the 1812 footprint against the JLCPCB footprint of C70113/C427396 (datasheet pads 1.78 × 3.2 mm).
- USB-only operation: at VBUS 4.75 V minus F2, FB1 and D3, the buck input is close to the LMR51610's 4 V minimum even with
  the present F2. Worth a line in design-notes when F2 is swapped.
