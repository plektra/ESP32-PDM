## Blocked – owner (one-way door): J4 does not fit on the signal edge

- **FINDING (HIGH): J4 does not fit.** Decision 128 puts J5 (panel) and J4 (CAN) together on the 65 mm signal edge.
  - The H2 and H4 Ø 8 mm keep-outs are centred 4 mm from the corners. Any connector whose courtyard reaches the edge
    must stay outside y 0–8 and y 57–65, which leaves **49 mm**.
  - J5 takes 39.0 mm of body (40.1 mm courtyard) and J4 16.1 mm (17.2 mm courtyard): **55.1 mm at the least, 57.3 mm
    with courtyards**, before any gap between them (decision 127 asks for them to be apart).
  - Decision 128's "42 + 55 mm" count used the full edge.
- **What was done.** J5 is placed (toward the rail); J4, D9, D10 and its GND_CAN-side parts stay parked. Holes, outline and
  connector edges are owner-fixed (decisions 123/128), so this is a one-way door.
- **Question for the owner:** where does J4 go? Options:
  - (a) on the front long edge near the signal end. Simplest; the ESP32 antenna then moves along the front edge,
    away from J4.
  - (b) on the power edge. Against the split rationale of decision 128 (keep CAN away from the switching current).
  - (c) a shorter J5. Needs a schematic change; not proposed.
  - (d) holes inset further, or a longer edge. Changes decisions 123/128 and the shared DIN mount.
- **Suggested default:** (a). J4 sits on the front edge 10–15 mm from the signal-edge corner, plug outward. The CAN
  barrier (U5, GND_CAN island) goes beside it, and the antenna goes to the front edge between the power half and J4, or
  to the signal-edge corner above J5 if the keep-outs allow.
